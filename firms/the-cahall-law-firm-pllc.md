# The Cahall Law Firm, PLLC

The Cahall Law Firm, PLLC is a Bradenton, Florida litigation firm led by attorney Don Cahall, focusing its practice on personal injury, business litigation, construction law, and consumer protection matters. The firm represents individuals and small businesses in cases ranging from car accidents, motorcycle accidents, and premises liability claims to breach of contract disputes, construction defects, and insurance litigation. Mr. Cahall personally handles each case from start to finish, offering pre-litigation guidance through mediation, arbitration, and trial in both state and federal court. Personal injury clients pay no attorney's fees or costs unless the firm recovers compensation on their behalf. The Cahall Law Firm, PLLC is located at 1909 Manatee Ave E, Bradenton, FL 34208 and can be reached by phone at 941-281-2019.
