# Pita Weber Del Prado

Pita Weber Del Prado is a Miami law firm focused on personal injury, wrongful death, and medical malpractice cases throughout Miami-Dade County and Florida. Attorneys H.K. Skip Pita, Shannon Del Prado, Randy M. Weber, and Rosanna Molinari Weber represent injured victims and their families in car, truck, motorcycle, boat, cruise ship, and rideshare accidents, premises liability, nursing home abuse, negligent security, product liability, and medical errors such as failure to diagnose, birth injuries, and medication errors. The firm also handles insurance coverage disputes and commercial litigation. Its results include a $10.5 million recovery for a personal injury victim, a $9.5 million recovery for an assault injury victim, an $8.17 million recovery for a drunk driver victim, and a $5.3 million medical malpractice claims bill. Recognitions include Super Lawyers, Best Lawyers, Best Law Firms, AV Preeminent, Florida Legal Elite, and an AVVO Rating of 10.0. The attorneys are fluent in English and Spanish, and cases are handled on a contingency fee basis with no fee unless there is a recovery. Pita Weber Del Prado is located at 9350 S Dixie Hwy, Suite 1200, Miami, FL 33156 and can be reached by phone at 305-670-2889.
