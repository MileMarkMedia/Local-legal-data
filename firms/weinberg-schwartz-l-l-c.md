# Weinberg & Schwartz, L.L.C.

Weinberg & Schwartz, L.L.C. is a Columbia, Maryland firm that has practiced family law exclusively since 1995, serving Howard, Baltimore, Anne Arundel, Montgomery and Prince George's counties and clients across Maryland. Partners Jolie Gelman Weinberg and Marni B. Schwartz lead a team that handles divorce, child custody, child support, alimony, property division and retirement orders, prenuptial and postnuptial agreements, adoption, guardianship, domestic violence protective orders and modifications. The firm's attorneys are trial-tested litigators who also practice mediation and collaborative law, choosing the approach that fits each family. Jolie Weinberg was the founding President of the Howard County Collaborative Professional group, and Marni Schwartz later served two terms as its president. The Maryland State Bar Association seeks out the firm's attorneys to educate other lawyers, and courts regularly appoint them to represent children's interests in high-conflict custody cases. Recognitions include Super Lawyers, including its Top 50 Women Maryland list, an Avvo Rating of 10.0 Superb the Avvo Client's Choice award, and Howard Magazine's Best of Howard County. Weinberg & Schwartz, L.L.C. is located at 10632 Little Patuxent Parkway, Suite 446, Columbia, MD 21044 and can be reached by phone at 410-997-0203.
