# FL DUI Group

FL DUI Group is a DUI defense firm serving Orlando and Central Florida, with cases handled in Orange, Osceola, Brevard, Volusia, and Lake counties. The firm was founded by Matt Olszewski, who served as the first Traffic Safety Resource Prosecutor in Florida and coordinated the statewide TSRP Program. He was one of six people chosen by the Florida Department of Law Enforcement to help write its DUI Instruction Manual, and the only DUI defense lawyer on that project. Matt has handled over 100 criminal trials and personally manages each client's case. His published work includes Strategies for Defending DUI Cases in Florida and Trends in DUI Discovery, both from Thomson West. He received the DUI Gladiator Award from the Florida Association of Criminal Defense Lawyers and holds an Avvo Rating of 10.0 and a 2019 Avvo Client's Choice Award. The firm handles breath and field sobriety test challenges, refusals, felony DUI, and license suspension hearings. Reported results include a not guilty jury verdict in a .272 breath test case in Brevard County and dismissal of a 4th offense felony DUI in Orange County. Spanish-language services are available during regular office hours. FL DUI Group is located at 200 East Robinson Street, Suite 1140, Orlando, FL 32801 and can be reached by phone at 321-256-1141.
