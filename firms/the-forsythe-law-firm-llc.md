# The Forsythe Law Firm, LLC

The Forsythe Law Firm, LLC is an Atlanta law firm focused on employment law, personal injury and small business legal services for clients in Atlanta and surrounding areas, including Buckhead, Midtown and Decatur. Founder and lead attorney Christine A. Forsythe, a University of Georgia School of Law graduate, has practiced law for over a decade. She spent most of her career as a trial attorney for a large national insurance company, defending personal injury claims for a major auto insurer, and has served as in-house claims and litigation counsel for a national company. Licensed in Georgia and Florida, she represents clients in state and federal courts and has tried numerous cases to verdict in complex litigation matters. The firm handles discrimination, harassment, retaliation, wrongful termination, wage and hour, FMLA and severance matters for employees, as well as car, truck, motorcycle, slip and fall and wrongful death claims. Small businesses work with the firm on entity formation, business litigation and outside general counsel services. Christine Forsythe is active in the American Bar Association and the National Employment Lawyers Association. The Forsythe Law Firm, LLC is located at 1050 Crown Pointe Parkway, Suite 1240, Atlanta, GA 30338 and can be reached by phone at 404-476-2717.
