# Rabin Kammerer Johnson

Rabin Kammerer Johnson is a boutique law firm in West Palm Beach, Florida, focused on complex business litigation, whistleblower and qui tam actions, and securities arbitration and litigation, with clients throughout Florida and a whistleblower practice that is national in scope. Attorneys Adam T. Rabin, Christopher W. Kammerer, and Lauren E. Johnson represent businesses and individuals in disputes involving contracts, shareholder and LLC conflicts, fraud, non-compete agreements, intellectual property, and real estate. The firm also handles False Claims Act cases involving Medicare and Medicaid fraud, pharmaceutical fraud, and government contract fraud and FINRA securities arbitrations. Adam Rabin has handled complex business litigation for thirty years, is Florida Bar Board Certified in Business Litigation, has represented whistleblowers since 2010, and served on The Florida Bar Board of Governors and as President of the Palm Beach County Bar Association. The firm has been ranked Tier 1 in Commercial Litigation by Best Law Firms, and its attorneys have been recognized by Florida Super Lawyers and Florida Trend's Legal Elite. Rabin Kammerer Johnson is located at 1601 Forum Place, Suite 201, West Palm Beach, FL 33401 and can be reached by phone at 561-659-7878.
