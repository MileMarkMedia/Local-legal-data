# Turzi Law Group

Turzi Law Group is a personal injury firm serving Manassas, Alexandria and communities across Northern Virginia, Maryland and Washington, D.C. Founder and lead attorney Rocco Turzi is licensed in Virginia, Maryland and Washington, D.C., and brings an academic background in political science and economics to his trial work. He practices alongside personal injury attorney Stella Villalobos, who previously handled immigration defense and defense-side civil litigation. The firm represents victims of car, truck, motorcycle, rideshare, pedestrian, bicycle and electric scooter accidents, as well as wrongful death, premises liability, slip and fall, dog bite, burn injury, medical malpractice, product liability and workplace injury claims. The team handles evidence gathering and insurer negotiations, pursuing medical costs, lost wages and long-term losses. Cities served include Fairfax, Springfield, Bethesda, Silver Spring, College Park and Waldorf, and the firm has a second office in Alexandria. The firm also serves clients in Spanish. Cases are handled on a contingency fee basis, so clients pay nothing up front and nothing unless the firm wins. Turzi Law Group is located at 9116 Center St, Suite 107, Manassas, VA 20110 and can be reached by phone at 540-900-6824.
