# Cheeley Law Group

Cheeley Law Group is a trial-focused personal injury law firm based in Alpharetta, representing individuals and families throughout Metro Atlanta and across Georgia. Founder and managing partner Robert D. Cheeley, admitted to the Georgia bar in 1982, leads trial attorneys Gabrielle W. Waldrop, McKenzie R. Nadel, and Andrew Craft, and the firm brings more than 50 years of combined experience to truck, car, motorcycle, and pedestrian accidents, catastrophic injury, wrongful death, defective products, and business torts. The firm has recovered $1.36 billion for clients, with results including a $150 million settlement against General Motors, a $105 million jury verdict against General Motors, and a $90 million settlement against Suzuki. Robert Cheeley holds an AV Preeminent rating from Martindale-Hubbell and was named National Trial Lawyer of the Year by Public Citizen in 1993, and the firm has earned Super Lawyers and Top Verdict recognition. The firm serves clients in seven languages, including Spanish and Portuguese, limits its caseload so clients have direct attorney involvement, and prepares every case as though it will be tried before a jury. Cheeley Law Group is located at 2500 Old Milton Pkwy, Suite 200, Alpharetta, GA 30009 and can be reached by phone at 770-814-7001.
