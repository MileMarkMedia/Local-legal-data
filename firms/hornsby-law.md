# Hornsby Law

Hornsby Law is a family personal injury and business litigation firm serving Orlando, Maitland, Winter Park, and clients throughout Florida, with offices in Orlando and Maitland. James Russell Hornsby founded the family firm in 1950, and Larry Hornsby started his own personal injury practice in 1976. Today Larry Hornsby and Lawrence "Hank" Hornsby, Jr., a Certified Fraud Examiner, handle car, truck, motorcycle, rideshare, and pedestrian accidents, medical malpractice, premises liability and slip and fall claims, workers' compensation, wrongful death, homeowner insurance disputes, investment fraud, and business and commercial litigation. The firm's recoveries include a $3,000,000 settlement for a motorcyclist hit by a tourist driving a rental car, a $1,250,000 car accident settlement, and a $1,000,000 settlement for the death of a pedestrian caused by a drunk driver. Honors include an AV Preeminent rating, Super Lawyers, Best Lawyers, the Multi-Million Dollar Advocates Forum, and The National Trial Lawyers Top 100. Clients meet personally with the attorney handling their case, Spanish language services are available, and injury cases are handled on a contingency basis. Hornsby Law is located at 1317 Edgewater Dr, Suite 216, Orlando, FL 32804 and can be reached by phone at 407-499-8887.
