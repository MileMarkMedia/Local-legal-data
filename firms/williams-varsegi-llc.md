# Williams & Varsegi, LLC

Williams & Varsegi, LLC is a boutique family law firm in Boca Raton practicing exclusively in divorce and family law throughout Palm Beach and Broward counties, including Boca Raton, Coral Springs, Parkland, Delray Beach, Deerfield Beach, Coconut Creek, Boynton Beach and Pompano Beach. Attorneys Emerald Williams and Katie Varsegi lead the firm, which brings more than twenty years of experience to contested, uncontested, high-net-worth and post-judgment divorce, alimony, child custody and time-sharing, child support, equitable distribution, parental relocation, paternity, adoption, modifications and prenuptial agreements. The firm also works with business owners, physicians, federal employees and police and fire personnel going through divorce. Emerald Williams has been recognized by Super Lawyers and Rising Stars and holds an Avvo Rating of 9.3, and the firm was named to the 10 Best for Attorney Client Satisfaction by the American Institute of Family Law Attorneys. Depending on each client's circumstances, the team resolves matters through negotiation, mediation, collaborative divorce or litigation. Williams & Varsegi, LLC is located at 2701 NW Boca Raton Blvd, Suite 104, Boca Raton, FL 33431 and can be reached by phone at 954-255-5655.
