# Sweeney Law, P.A.

Sweeney Law, P.A. is a Fort Lauderdale law firm focused on business law, construction law, real estate law and consumer law, serving clients throughout Palm Beach, Broward and Miami-Dade counties. Founder and principal attorney Brendan A. Sweeney has more than 12 years of legal experience, including prior large firm work representing lenders, debt buyers, subprime lenders and banks, insight he now applies to represent consumers, small businesses and homeowners. The firm handles business formation and transactions, commercial litigation, contract and non-compete disputes, construction contracts, Florida construction liens and defect claims, real estate transactions and closings, title and boundary disputes, evictions, foreclosure, and matters for HOAs and condo associations. Its consumer practice covers TCPA robocall claims, debt collection and credit reporting violations, identity theft, lemon law and student loan issues. Brendan Sweeney has been recognized by Super Lawyers, including the Rising Stars edition, and named to Florida Legal Elite. The firm serves clients in English, French and Spanish, with evening and weekend meetings available by appointment. Sweeney Law, P.A. is located at 6750 N Andrews Ave, Suite 200, Fort Lauderdale, FL 33309 and can be reached by phone at 954-440-3993.
