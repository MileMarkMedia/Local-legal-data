# Fanning Law, LLC

Fanning Law, LLC is a La Plata, Maryland law firm focused on divorce and family law, serving clients throughout Charles, Calvert, Prince George's and Anne Arundel Counties and across Southern and Central Maryland. Founder and principal attorney William C. Fanning, Jr. has been in private practice since 1993, bringing more than 30 years of family law experience in the courtroom and in settling complex cases. He is a graduate of Salisbury College and the Catholic University Columbus School of Law and spent five years as an Adjunct Professor of Family Law and Paralegal Studies at the College of Southern Maryland. The firm handles divorce, child custody and visitation, child support, alimony, marital property division, prenuptial agreements, modifications and enforcement, paternity, domestic violence, guardianship and adoption, along with personal injury, criminal defense, wills and trusts, and business matters. William Fanning has been recognized as an Avvo Top Attorney and provides pro bono help through the Maryland Volunteer Lawyers Service and the Child's Voice Project. Fanning Law, LLC is located at 201 Centennial St, Suite 2A, La Plata, MD 20646 and can be reached by phone at 301-934-3620.
