# D'Amore Personal Injury Law, LLC

D'Amore Personal Injury Law, LLC is a Baltimore medical malpractice and catastrophic injury firm founded in 2015 by trial lawyer Paul D'Amore, serving clients throughout Maryland and Washington, D.C. Paul brings more than 25 years of experience, including a decade spent defending hospitals, physicians, and insurers before he chose to represent injured patients, so he knows how the defense evaluates a claim and where its arguments break down. The firm handles birth injury and cerebral palsy cases, surgical and anesthesia errors, delayed diagnosis, traumatic brain and spinal cord injuries, serious truck and car crashes, and wrongful death, and has taken on Johns Hopkins, University of Maryland Medical Center, and other major health systems. It has recovered more than $150 million for clients, including a $12.5 million medical malpractice recovery and a $4.26 million Baltimore City jury verdict. Paul has been recognized by Super Lawyers, Best Lawyers, the Multi-Million Dollar Advocates Forum, and The National Trial Lawyers Top 100. Consultations are free, case costs are advanced, and there is no fee unless the firm wins. D'Amore Personal Injury Law is located at 145 W Ostend St, Suite 600, Baltimore, MD 21230 and 1200 G Street NW, 8th Floor, Washington, D.C. 20005, and can be reached by phone at 410-324-2000 or 202-780-9000.
