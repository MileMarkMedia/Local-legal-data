# Goldshaw Greenblatt Pierce LLC

Goldshaw Greenblatt Pierce LLC is a Philadelphia law firm concentrating in employment law, criminal defense, civil rights, and survivor's rights, serving clients throughout the region from offices in Philadelphia and Haddonfield, New Jersey. Managing Partner Ronald L. Greenblatt began his career at the Defender Association of Philadelphia in 1987, where he was one of two lawyers appointed to its Special Defense Unit. Scott B. Goldshaw, Chair of the Employment Law Department, has practiced employment law and litigation since 1996 and was named a 2022 Best Lawyers Lawyer of the Year. Patricia V. Pierce, Chair of the Civil Rights and Survivor's Rights Departments, has a career spanning nearly 50 years and was named Best Lawyers Attorney of the Year in 2013 and 2023. The firm's attorneys have been recognized by Best Lawyers in America, Super Lawyers, and Martindale-Hubbell. Results achieved by its attorneys include a recovery of more than $30 million for 100 workers discriminatorily laid off by a Fortune 500 company and a $9 million class jury verdict for defrauded students. The firm builds strategies around each client's objectives, whether through advice, negotiation, or litigation. Goldshaw Greenblatt Pierce LLC is located at 1500 John F Kennedy Blvd, Suite 1230, Philadelphia, PA 19102 and can be reached by phone at 215-978-9090.
