# The Stanley Law Group

The Stanley Law Group is a personal injury firm serving Columbia, South Carolina, Richland County and communities across the state. H. Ronald Stanley founded the firm in 1990, and he practices alongside attorney Mark B. Stanley, who is licensed in South Carolina and Florida. With more than 100 years of combined legal experience, the firm handles car accidents, truck and tractor trailer accidents, motorcycle accidents, pedestrian and bicycle accidents, slip and falls, nursing home abuse, product liability, wrongful death and other serious injury claims, along with probate and estate administration matters. The firm has recovered over $100 million for injured clients, with reported results including an $11 million wrongful death recovery, a $4.5 million motor vehicle accident recovery and a $4 million commercial vehicle accident recovery. Recognitions include Super Lawyers, the Million Dollar Advocates Forum, The National Trial Lawyers Top 100, The National Trial Lawyers Top 40 Under 40 and Lawyers of Distinction. Personal injury cases are handled on a contingency fee basis with a free initial consultation. The Stanley Law Group is located at 1418 Park St., Columbia, SC 29201 and can be reached by phone at 803-799-4700.
