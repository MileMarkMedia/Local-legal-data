# Farley & Weed, LLC

Farley & Weed, LLC is a full-service law firm in Hawley, Pennsylvania serving individuals and families throughout Pike and Wayne Counties, including Honesdale and the Lake Wallenpaupack area. The firm handles criminal defense, DUI, estate planning, wills and trusts, family law, divorce, personal injury, real estate, and civil litigation. Partners Thomas F. Farley, admitted to the bar in 1985, and John H. Klemeyer, admitted in 1973, lead a team of attorneys with more than 75 years of combined experience. The lawyers are Pennsylvania natives, raised and educated in the Commonwealth, who have served as public defenders, district attorneys, and solicitors and general counsel for a number of townships. They are admitted to practice in all Pennsylvania courts as well as several federal courts, including the U.S. Supreme Court, the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Third Circuit and the U.S. District Court for the Middle District of Pennsylvania. The firm offers a free initial consultation. Farley & Weed, LLC is located at 2523 Route 6, Suite 1, Hawley, PA 18428 and can be reached by phone at 570-226-5771.
