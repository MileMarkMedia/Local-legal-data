# Ronald Cutler, P.A.

Ronald Cutler, P.A. is a Florida tax law firm focused on resolving state and federal tax problems, including bank levies, wage garnishments, liens, payroll and sales tax disputes, unfiled returns, and IRS offers in compromise. The firm also handles bankruptcy and elder law matters. Ronald Cutler is a licensed attorney, Certified Public Accountant, and former Special Agent for the FBI who has spent over 50 years handling state and federal tax matters, including appearances before the U.S. Tax Court. Attorney Michael McAnnany also works with the firm. Clients receive personal attention from someone directly involved in their case rather than being passed off to staff. The firm serves clients throughout Florida, including Daytona Beach, Orlando, Jacksonville, Tampa, and Miami, as well as nationwide tax matters. Ronald Cutler, P.A. is located at 1162 Pelican Bay Drive, Daytona Beach, FL 32119 and can be reached by phone at 386-490-9949.
