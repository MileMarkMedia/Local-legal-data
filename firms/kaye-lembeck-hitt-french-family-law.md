# Kaye, Lembeck, Hitt & French Family Law

Kaye, Lembeck, Hitt & French Family Law is an Atlanta family law firm serving clients in Atlanta and the surrounding area. The firm is led by partners Amy L. Kaye, Alyson F. Lembeck, Elinor H. Hitt, and Brooke M. French. Founding partner Amy L. Kaye has practiced family law in metro Atlanta for well over 20 years and previously taught family law as an adjunct professor at Emory Law School. Together the firm's attorneys bring more than 100 years of combined experience to divorce, including high net worth, business owner, military, and same-sex divorce, as well as child custody, child support, alimony, relocation, paternity and legitimation, adoption, grandparents' rights, prenuptial and postnuptial agreements, property and retirement division, modifications, and appeals. The partners are trained as civil and domestic relations mediators, domestic violence mediators, arbitrators, and guardians ad litem, which adds depth in cases involving children. Amy L. Kaye has been recognized by Super Lawyers in family law since 2004. The firm practices solution-focused advocacy, involving clients in decisions and working to resolve cases amicably while remaining prepared for court. Kaye, Lembeck, Hitt & French Family Law is located at 5555 Glenridge Connector, Suite 675, Atlanta, GA 30342 and can be reached by phone at 404-233-2800.
