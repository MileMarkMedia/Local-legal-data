# The Beach Law Firm

The Beach Law Firm is a Palm Harbor, Florida family law and divorce practice led by attorney Shelly Beach, a fourth-generation Pinellas County resident who has spent more than 20 years honing her litigation and negotiation skills in family and civil matters. The firm handles contested, uncontested, collaborative, high net worth and LGBTQ divorce, along with child custody, child support, alimony, equitable distribution, modification and enforcement, relocation, paternity, adoption, annulment, postnuptial agreements, grandparent custody and visitation, and domestic violence matters. Shelly Beach is a Florida Supreme Court Certified Family Law Mediator trained in collaborative divorce, and the firm also provides mediation services. Each case begins with a consultation to understand the client's goals and priorities, and the firm uses mediation and collaborative law when appropriate while remaining prepared to litigate when negotiations fail or court intervention is needed. Complimentary consultations are available. The Beach Law Firm is located at 2142 US-19 ALT, Suite B, Palm Harbor, FL 34683 and can be reached by phone at 727-315-0015.
