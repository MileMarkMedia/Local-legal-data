# Kobal Law

Kobal Law is a Tampa workers' compensation firm representing injured workers throughout Hillsborough County, including Brandon, Riverview, and Carrollwood. Founder and principal attorney Jason Kobal has 18 years of experience pursuing Florida workers' compensation benefits for injured workers. Before representing injured workers exclusively, he worked on the insurance defense side of workers' compensation disputes, giving him insight into how carriers build their cases to deny or reduce benefits. The firm handles workplace accidents, repetitive motion injuries, occupational diseases, toxic and asbestos exposure, hearing loss, uninsured employer claims, and denied medical bills, along with personal injury matters such as car and truck accidents, slip and falls, wrongful death, and defamation, and statewide fair debt claims involving medical bill collection. Jason Kobal received Tampa Magazine's 2019 peer-voted recognition for Tampa Bay workers' compensation attorneys and was named a Tampa Style Magazine Attorney of the Year in 2021. Spanish is spoken in the office, consultations are free, and cases are handled on a contingency fee basis with no upfront fees. Kobal Law is located at 12169 West Linebaugh Avenue, Tampa, FL 33626 and can be reached by phone at 813-873-2440.
