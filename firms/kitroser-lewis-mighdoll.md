# Kitroser Lewis & Mighdoll

Kitroser Lewis & Mighdoll is a North Palm Beach law firm focused on estate planning, probate, trust and estate litigation, elder law, and guardianship for families throughout Palm Beach County. Managing partner Mitchell Ira Kitroser leads the firm with partners Kathryn Lewis and Preston Mighdoll, and its attorneys bring over 100 years of combined legal experience to clients in West Palm Beach, North Palm Beach, Palm Beach Gardens, Jupiter, and Jensen Beach, as well as former Florida residents and out-of-state owners of Florida property. The firm prepares wills and trusts, including special needs, pet, marital, and irrevocable life insurance trusts, and handles asset protection, business succession, incapacity planning, Medicaid planning, and guardian advocacy. On the litigation side, it represents clients in will contests, trust disputes, and claims involving undue influence, breach of fiduciary duty, and elder financial exploitation. Its attorneys have earned recognition including Super Lawyers, Avvo Top Rated Lawyer, and Martindale-Hubbell honors. The firm emphasizes long-term relationships, meeting families at each stage of life. Kitroser Lewis & Mighdoll is located at 631 US Hwy 1, Suite 406, North Palm Beach, FL 33408 and can be reached by phone at 561-721-0600.
