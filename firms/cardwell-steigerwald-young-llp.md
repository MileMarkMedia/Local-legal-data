# Cardwell Steigerwald Young LLP

Cardwell Steigerwald Young LLP is a California family law firm representing clients throughout the San Francisco Bay Area, with offices in San Francisco, Oakland, and San Mateo and clients in Marin County, Palo Alto, and San Jose. The firm's partners, Alex Cardwell, Michelle Steigerwald, Charlie Young, and Suzanne Ahlport Tong, handle divorce, legal separation, child custody, child and spousal support, property and retirement division, restraining orders, prenuptial and postnuptial agreements, and post-judgment matters, including high net worth, LGBTQ, and military divorce. Alex Cardwell, Charlie Young, and Suzanne Tong are Certified Family Law Specialists by the California Board of Legal Specialization, and Michelle Steigerwald, Charlie Young, and Suzanne Tong have been recognized by Super Lawyers. The team brings more than 50 years of combined legal experience to matters ranging from straightforward divorces to complex cases involving tech sector clients. The firm takes a team approach, bringing in forensic accountants and other experts as needed, and favors negotiated or mediated results while litigating in court when necessary. Cardwell Steigerwald Young LLP is located at 150 Post St, Suite 710, San Francisco, CA 94108 and can be reached by phone at 415-259-5885.
