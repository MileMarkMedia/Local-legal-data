# The Law Office Of Laurie R. Chane

The Law Office Of Laurie R. Chane is a Dade City, Florida law firm focused on divorce, family law, estate planning, elder law and criminal defense, serving Dade City, Wesley Chapel, Zephyrhills and the surrounding areas of Pasco, Hillsborough and Hernando counties. Laurie R. Chane began her career as an Assistant Public Defender in East Pasco County, handling cases ranging from DUI and petit theft to capital murder, and brings more than 25 years of experience to her clients. She holds an AV Preeminent rating from Martindale-Hubbell and has served on the Executive Board of the East Pasco Bar Association, including as its President. The firm handles divorce, collaborative divorce, mediation, alimony, child custody, child support, property division and domestic violence injunctions, as well as wills, trusts, powers of attorney, advance directives, probate, asset protection and Medicaid planning. It also has experience with same-sex divorce, custody and estate planning matters, and represents clients facing misdemeanor and felony charges from traffic offenses to homicide. Clients can communicate with the office through a mobile app. The Law Office Of Laurie R. Chane is located at 14206 5th St, Dade City, FL 33523 and can be reached by phone at 352-567-0055.
