# Law Office of Manning Zimmerman & Oliveira PLLC

The Law Office of Manning Zimmerman & Oliveira PLLC is a Manchester, New Hampshire law firm focused on personal injury and workers' compensation representation for clients in Manchester, Concord, Nashua, and throughout the state. Owner Anna Goulet Zimmerman, a past President of the NH Association for Justice, leads the firm with trial attorney Michaila Oliveira, while founder Maureen Raiche Manning, also a past NHAJ President and a faculty member at the Keenan Ball Trial College, now serves as of counsel. The named partners bring more than 50 years of combined legal experience. The firm handles car and truck accidents, distracted driving crashes, dog bites, slip and fall cases, sexual assault claims, and premises liability matters, along with workers' compensation claims, hearings, and appeals, and it also handles family law matters such as divorce, custody, and support. Recognition includes Super Lawyers, an AV Preeminent rating from Martindale-Hubbell, and Avvo 10.0 ratings. Injury matters are handled on a contingency fee basis, consultations are free, and evening, weekend, and off-site meetings are available by appointment. Law Office of Manning Zimmerman & Oliveira PLLC is located at 87 Middle Street, Manchester, NH 03101 and can be reached by phone at 603-624-7200.
