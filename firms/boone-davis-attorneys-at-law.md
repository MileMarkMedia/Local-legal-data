# Boone & Davis, Attorneys at Law

Boone & Davis, Attorneys at Law is a Fort Lauderdale personal injury firm serving Broward County and clients throughout Florida. Founded in 1981 by David Boone and Michael Davis, the firm has more than 40 years of experience representing victims of car, truck, and motorcycle crashes, cruise ship injuries, premises liability, product liability, medical malpractice, and wrongful death claims. Partner Michael Davis, admitted to the Florida Bar in 1981, began his career as a law clerk for David Boone before the two formed their partnership, and he holds an AV Preeminent rating from Martindale-Hubbell and recognition among The National Trial Lawyers Top 100. The firm reports verdicts and settlements over $1 million in more than 100 cases, including a $2.6 million product liability result and a $1.3 million neck surgery result, and its attorneys are members of the Million Dollar Advocates Forum. Michael Davis gives clients his direct line and cell phone number, and home or hospital visits are available for seriously injured clients. Cases are handled on a contingency fee basis. Boone & Davis, Attorneys at Law is located at 2311 North Andrews Ave., Ft. Lauderdale, FL 33311-3993 and can be reached by phone at 954-566-9919.
