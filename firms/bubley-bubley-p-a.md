# Bubley & Bubley, P.A.

Bubley & Bubley, P.A. is a Tampa law firm focused on family law, estate planning, probate, and business law, serving clients in Tampa and throughout Florida since 1992. The firm recognizes that legal issues in one of these areas often overlap with another. Partner Daniel B. Bubley practices marital and family law and sports law, and Martin A. Bubley has focused on business transactions, estate planning, and probate law for more than 30 years, working alongside attorney Lexy M. Bubley, whose practice centers on probate, estate planning, and trust administration. The firm handles divorce, child custody, adoption, paternity, prenuptial and postnuptial agreements, wills, trusts, powers of attorney, probate administration, business formation and succession planning, residential and commercial real estate, civil litigation, and mediation. Clients range from young couples and business owners to retiring grandparents, and the attorneys personally handle each matter from the initial meeting through trial or other resolution. Bubley & Bubley, P.A. is located at 12960 N Dale Mabry Hwy, Tampa, FL 33618 and can be reached by phone at 813-963-7735.
