# Flores, PLLC

Flores, PLLC is a business law and litigation firm based in Austin, serving companies across Texas including Houston, as well as clients with operations in Mexico and worldwide. Founded by attorneys Melina Flores and Rey Flores, the firm brings 40 years of accumulated experience in commercial litigation, corporate and business law, Texas and Mexico cross-border transactions, international litigation, outside general counsel services, and corporate immigration law. Its clients range from startups to multinational corporations, with matters including trade secret disputes, construction litigation, mergers and acquisitions, joint ventures, and work visa compliance. Representative matters include a $250 million industrial real estate sale in Latin America, a $60 million debt restructuring with a global lender, and an ICC arbitration seated in London for an African LNG company. Recognition includes Chambers USA 2026, Lawdragon 2026, Super Lawyers, and Best Lawyers Ones to Watch. The bilingual team works in English and Spanish, tailors its counsel to each client's business goals, and offers alternative fee arrangements. Flores, PLLC is located at 7500 Rialto Blvd, Suite 270, Austin, TX 78735 and can be reached by phone at 512-381-8874.
