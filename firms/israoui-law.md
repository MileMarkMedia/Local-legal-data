# Israoui Law

Israoui Law is a personal injury firm representing accident victims in Orlando, Kissimmee, and throughout Central Florida. Attorney Naji Israoui, a graduate of the University of Central Florida and Florida State University, began representing injury victims in 2003 and started his own practice in 2007, and has since represented thousands of injured clients and recovered millions of dollars on their behalf. He previously served as an Assistant General Counsel for the Florida Department of Health and is a Florida Supreme Court Certified Circuit Court Mediator. A serious car accident that left him with permanent injuries shapes how he approaches each client's case. The firm handles car, truck, bus, boating, pedestrian, and bicycle accidents, uninsured and drunk driver crashes, hit-and-run accidents, slip and fall and premises liability claims, dog bites, brain and spinal injuries, burn injuries, wrongful death, and insurance disputes. Honors include 10 Best recognition from 2015 through 2018 and an Avvo Client's Choice award. Clients speak directly with attorneys who handle every aspect of the case, Spanish is spoken, and cases are taken on a contingency fee basis. Israoui Law is located at 5301 E. Colonial Dr., Orlando, FL 32807 and can be reached by phone at 407-381-4529.
