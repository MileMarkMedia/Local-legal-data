# Pike & Lustig, LLP

Pike & Lustig, LLP is a West Palm Beach law firm focused on business litigation and personal injury, serving clients in Palm Beach, Broward, Martin, St. Lucie, and Miami-Dade counties and throughout Florida. Managing partner Michael J. Pike and partners Daniel Lustig, Talina Bidwell, Robert C. Johnson, and Timothy O'Neill handle shareholder and partnership disputes, commercial litigation, business fraud, employment, franchise, copyright and trademark matters, and legal malpractice, along with auto, truck, motorcycle, boating, aviation, pedestrian, and negligent security injury claims. The firm also practices family law, estate and trust litigation, and equine law. Its results include an $80 million severe personal injury jury verdict, a $67.25 million jury verdict in a Martin County case, and a $23.8 million construction accident jury verdict. Michael Pike holds an AV Preeminent rating from Martindale-Hubbell, and the firm's recognitions include Best Law Firms and Super Lawyers. Firm attorneys have appeared on CNN, Fox News, and local television news. The firm has offices in West Palm Beach, Palm Beach Gardens, Wellington, and Miami, and Spanish is spoken. Pike & Lustig, LLP is located at 1209 N Olive Ave, West Palm Beach, FL 33401 and can be reached by phone at 561-291-8298.
