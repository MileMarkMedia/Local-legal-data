# Dominko & Swaim Law Group, PLLC

Dominko & Swaim Law Group, PLLC (DSLG) is a boutique Sarasota law firm focused on personal injury and business litigation, serving clients in Sarasota, North Port, Bradenton, Venice, Englewood and across Florida. The firm was founded in 2025 by Shem L. Dominko and Yuliya V. Swaim. Mr. Dominko, who has practiced since 2010, is Board Certified in Business Litigation by The Florida Bar. Ms. Swaim brings more than a decade of experience in high-stakes commercial litigation, and both founders have been recognized as Super Lawyers Florida Rising Stars. Of Counsel Riccardo D. Arcaro, a trial attorney with nearly five decades of practice, has served as lead counsel in more than 200 jury trials and holds an AV Preeminent rating from Martindale-Hubbell. DSLG represents individuals and families in catastrophic injury, wrongful death, vehicle accident and premises liability claims, and handles shareholder and partnership disputes, business torts, breach of contract, trade secret and wills and trust litigation. Personal injury cases are taken on a contingency basis, and attorneys and staff speak English, Ukrainian, Russian, Polish and French. Dominko & Swaim Law Group, PLLC is located at 6571 Palmer Park Cir, Sarasota, FL 34238 and can be reached by phone at 941-394-0183.
