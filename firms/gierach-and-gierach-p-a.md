# Gierach and Gierach, P.A.

Gierach and Gierach, P.A. is a Winter Park law firm focused on estate planning, probate, and workers' compensation defense, serving Orlando, Orange County, and clients across Florida. The firm was founded in 1986 by Elroy and John Gierach and has more than 40 years of legal experience. Co-founder and Principal Attorney John R. Gierach is board certified in workers' compensation by the Florida Bar and has 43 years of estate planning experience and 35 years in workers' compensation defense, and the firm holds an AV Preeminent rating from Martindale-Hubbell. The estate planning practice covers wills, trusts, powers of attorney, living wills, guardianships, asset protection, and estate tax planning. On the probate side, the firm represents executors, administrators, and trustees, handles will contests and probate litigation, and assists with real estate in probate and estates without a will, including remote probate for out-of-state families. The workers' compensation practice defends employers against disputed or improper claims. Longtime staff members have each been with the firm for more than 25 years, and initial consultations are free. Gierach and Gierach, P.A. is located at 1201 S Orlando Avenue, Suite 460, Winter Park, FL 32789-7109 and can be reached by phone at 407-598-8013.
