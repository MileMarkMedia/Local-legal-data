# Costanzo Law Firm, APC

Costanzo Law Firm, APC is an employment law firm serving San Jose, Palo Alto, Los Gatos, Campbell, Santa Clara, and the greater Santa Clara County area. Founding partner Lori J. Costanzo leads a team that brings more than 25 years of experience litigating cases in state and federal courts throughout the Bay Area. The firm represents employees in wrongful termination, discrimination, retaliation, disability accommodation, equal pay, wage and hour, whistleblower, and sexual harassment matters, along with business litigation, personal injury, and mediation. Results include a $4.8 million executive separation from a public Silicon Valley technology company, a $4 million gender equal pay class action against Farmers Insurance, and a $3.4 million business litigation settlement involving an ousted founding member. The firm has been recognized by Super Lawyers, the Top 100 Trial Lawyers, and Avvo, and is affiliated with California Women Lawyers. It develops individualized strategies for each client, pursuing resolution through mediation or arbitration when possible while remaining prepared for trial, and offers services in Spanish and Portuguese. Costanzo Law Firm, APC is located at 111 W. St. John Street, Suite 700, San Jose, CA 95113 and can be reached by phone at 408-993-8493.
