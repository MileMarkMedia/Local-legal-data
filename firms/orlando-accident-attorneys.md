# Orlando Accident Attorneys

Orlando Accident Attorneys is a boutique personal injury law firm in downtown Orlando representing accident victims and their families throughout Orange, Seminole and Osceola counties, including Winter Park, Lake Nona, Dr. Phillips, Kissimmee, Sanford and Altamonte Springs. Led by attorney Rania Arwani, a trial lawyer and TEDx speaker on the impact of domestic violence, the firm handles car, truck and motorcycle crashes, pedestrian and bicycle accidents, Uber, Lyft, rental car and tourist accidents, theme park and hotel injuries, boating and jet ski accidents, construction accidents, premises liability and slip and fall claims, catastrophic injuries such as traumatic brain and spinal cord injuries, and wrongful death. Its attorneys work directly with each client and prepare every case for trial while negotiating with insurers. The firm has earned an Avvo rating and a 10 Best Attorney Client Satisfaction honor, and it works on a contingency fee basis with free consultations. Orlando Accident Attorneys is located at 55 E Washington St, Suite 200, Orlando, FL 32801 and can be reached by phone at 407-775-4775.
