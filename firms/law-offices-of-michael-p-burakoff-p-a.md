# Law Offices of Michael P. Burakoff, P.A.

The Law Offices of Michael P. Burakoff, P.A. is a New Jersey personal injury firm representing accident victims in Morristown, Morris County, Newton, Sparta, West New York and surrounding communities. Serving the injured since 1976, the firm is led by principal attorney Michael P. Burakoff, who handles every critical aspect of each case personally. The firm handles car, truck, motorcycle and pedestrian accidents, premises liability, construction accidents, workers' compensation, product liability, medical malpractice, UM/UIM and bad faith insurance claims, and catastrophic injury and wrongful death cases. Burakoff has served as a Superior Court arbitrator in automobile accident and personal injury cases, and was co-counsel for Essex County, Union County and a dozen New Jersey municipalities in mass tort litigation against the lead paint industry. The firm has achieved dozens of six and seven figure recoveries, including $3,600,000 for a construction site accident, $1,600,000 for an automobile accident and $1,415,000 in a product liability case. Cases are handled on a contingency fee basis and Spanish is spoken. The Law Offices of Michael P. Burakoff, P.A. is located at 89 Headquarters Plaza North, Suite 1402, Morristown, NJ 07960 and can be reached by phone at 973-455-1567.
