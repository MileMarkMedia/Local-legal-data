# Ira H. Weinstock, P.C.

Ira H. Weinstock, P.C. is a Harrisburg law firm that has represented injured workers and labor unions since 1967, serving clients in Harrisburg, Carlisle, Lancaster, Reading, York, and communities throughout Pennsylvania. Founder Ira H. Weinstock has practiced labor and employment law for more than 45 years and is joined by attorneys Jason M. Weinstock, John (Jack) B. Dougherty, and Louis J. Michelsen, a former staff attorney for Justice Michael Eakin of the Pennsylvania Supreme Court, with more than 100 years of combined legal experience. The firm includes Certified Specialists in Pennsylvania Workers Compensation Law and concentrates on workers compensation benefits, denials, hearings, and settlements, workplace injury, personal injury, Social Security Disability, and labor and employment matters for union and non-union employees. Its attorneys have earned recognition from Best Lawyers, including a 2014 Lawyer of the Year award for Jason M. Weinstock in Workers' Compensation Law, Claimants, in Harrisburg, along with Pennsylvania Super Lawyers, an AV Preeminent rating, and Avvo Rating 10.0 Superb. The firm offers free case evaluations and a team approach with personal attention from its attorneys. Ira H. Weinstock, P.C. is located at 800 N 2nd St, Harrisburg, PA 17102 and can be reached by phone at 717-238-1657.
