# Mark David Shirian P.C.

Mark David Shirian P.C. is a New York City law firm handling personal injury and employment discrimination cases for clients in Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, the Bronx, Staten Island, Long Island and Westchester. Founded in 2016 by attorney Mark D. Shirian and joined by attorney Shawn D. Shirian, the firm has recovered millions of dollars for clients in injury and civil matters. It represents people hurt in car, truck and motorcycle crashes, pedestrian and bicycle accidents, construction and worksite injuries under New York Labor Law, slip and fall cases, medical malpractice, defective products, dog bites, nursing home negligence and wrongful death, and it pursues workplace discrimination claims. Mark Shirian's work on high-profile cases has been covered by The New York Times, the New York Post, Fox News and CBS News. Every case is handled from start to finish by one of the firm's attorneys rather than a case manager, with early investigation and trial-ready preparation. Mark David Shirian P.C. is located at 228 East 45th Street, Suite 1700B, New York, NY 10017 and can be reached by phone at 212-931-6530.
