# Drew Fritsch Law Firm, P.A.

Drew Fritsch Law Firm, P.A. is a Punta Gorda criminal defense firm representing clients throughout Charlotte, Lee, Collier, and Sarasota Counties, including Port Charlotte, Cape Coral, Fort Myers and Estero. Founder Drew Fritsch has been a member of The Florida Bar since 1997 and has practiced criminal law for more than twenty years, including eight years as a prosecutor in Charlotte and Lee Counties. He is a former president of the Charlotte County Bar Association. The firm handles DUI and DUI refusal, drug possession and trafficking, theft, weapon charges, assault, domestic violence, probation violations, driver's license suspensions, traffic violations throughout Florida, and expungement and sealing. Results include a domestic battery charge dismissed on the eve of trial, a battery charge dismissed on a stand your ground motion, DUI charges dropped after the defense identified errors, and a felony battery charge reduced to breach of peace. Drew Fritsch is AV Rated by Martindale-Hubbell, received an Avvo Clients' Choice Award, and personally handles each client's case. Drew Fritsch Law Firm, P.A. is located at 265 E Marion Ave, Suite 118, Punta Gorda, FL 33950 and can be reached by phone at 941-205-3535.
