# Fisher Law LLC

Fisher Law LLC is a boutique estate planning and elder law firm in Norwood, Massachusetts, serving Westwood, Dedham and communities across the South Shore, Metro West and Greater Boston. The firm focuses on estate planning, elder law, and probate and trust administration, including wills, trusts, powers of attorney, advance directives, special needs planning, asset protection, MassHealth planning, guardianships and conservatorships, veterans benefits, and business succession planning. Founder and principal attorney Linda K. Fisher holds an LL.M. in Taxation from Boston University School of Law, a J.D. cum laude from the Catholic University of America Columbus School of Law, and an M.B.A. from George Washington University. Before turning her focus to individuals and families, she worked on corporate finance and securities matters as an associate at Peabody and Arnold LLP in Boston and in the commercial law department of Pepper Hamilton LLP in Philadelphia. She is a member of the National Academy of Elder Law Attorneys, WealthCounsel and the Norfolk and Plymouth Estate Planning Council. The firm provides customized plans designed to give clients clear structure for their loved ones. Fisher Law LLC is located at 128 Carnegie Row, Suite 204, Norwood, MA 02062 and can be reached by phone at 781-821-8800.
