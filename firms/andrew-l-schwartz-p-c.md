# Andrew L. Schwartz, P.C.

Andrew L. Schwartz, P.C. is a Marietta criminal defense firm representing people charged with DUI, drug crimes, and other Georgia misdemeanor and felony offenses throughout Cobb County, including Marietta, Roswell, Kennesaw, and Smyrna. The firm is led by Andrew L. Schwartz, a member of the State Bar of Georgia since 1997 who spent many years as a prosecutor before turning to defense work and who was named Trial Attorney of the Year by the Fulton County District Attorney in 2003. The firm handles DUI cases, including multiple-offense, commercial driver, and drug-related DUI, along with drug possession, distribution, and trafficking, theft and shoplifting, burglary, assault and battery, gun charges, probation violations, and reckless driving. Schwartz holds an AV Preeminent rating from Martindale-Hubbell, has been named to the Top 100 Trial Lawyers by The National Trial Lawyers, and is a member of the National College for DUI Defense. He draws on his prosecution experience to build each defense, explains every option directly, and offers a free consultation. Andrew L. Schwartz, P.C. is located at 191 Roswell Street, Marietta, GA 30060 and can be reached by phone at 678-853-2500.
