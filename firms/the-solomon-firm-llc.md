# The Solomon Firm LLC

The Solomon Firm LLC is an Atlanta law firm focused on Georgia family law and divorce, along with civil litigation, personal injury and criminal defense, serving metro Atlanta from its Buckhead-area office. The firm is led by attorney Tricia Solomon, a University of Georgia School of Law graduate admitted to the Georgia Bar in 2007, who worked as an associate at a boutique firm and at a criminal defense firm before running her own practice. She has represented clients in trials, hearings, arbitrations and mediations before agencies and federal, state and local courts, in matters including product liability, construction, medical malpractice and employment law. She is joined by attorney Vickie S. Poliard, also a UGA School of Law graduate, who founded Poliard Law Firm, LLC and practiced family law, estate planning and juvenile law for many years. The firm handles divorce, alimony, child custody, child support, legitimation, paternity, modifications, prenuptial agreements and property division, as well as car, truck and slip and fall injury claims and criminal matters such as DUI. Each case is evaluated individually, with the firm settling when that is the right course and litigating when a better outcome is possible. The Solomon Firm LLC is located at 1718 Peachtree St NW, Suite 195, Atlanta, GA 30309 and can be reached by phone at 404-565-0641.
