# Robert S. Norell, P.A.

Robert S. Norell, P.A. is a Florida employment law firm focused on unpaid overtime and wage claims, representing workers statewide with offices serving Broward and Miami-Dade Counties. Attorney Robert S. Norell has been a member of The Florida Bar since 1994, is admitted to practice in the United States District Courts throughout Florida, and has assisted hundreds of employees with pay problems over more than 30 years. He has also served as a Florida Supreme Court Certified Mediator since 2009. The firm handles overtime and unpaid wages, independent contractor misclassification, Equal Pay Act claims, retaliation, whistleblower claims, wrongful termination, employment contracts, non-compete and severance agreements, and discrimination based on age, disability, pregnancy, race, religion, and military status, along with sexual harassment, FMLA, and EEOC matters. Most wage claims are filed in federal district court, where the firm seeks unpaid wages, liquidated damages, interest, and costs. Cases are typically handled on a contingency basis, and clients pay nothing unless a recovery is obtained. Robert S. Norell, P.A. is located at 300 N.W. 70th Avenue, Suite 305, Plantation, FL 33317 and can be reached by phone at 954-617-6017.
