# Weir & Kestner Injury Lawyers

Weir & Kestner Injury Lawyers is a Tennessee personal injury law firm serving Murfreesboro, Smyrna, Nashville, Chattanooga, and the surrounding areas of Rutherford County and Middle Tennessee. The firm was founded in 2012 by Joe Weir and Tony Kestner, with roots in Rutherford County dating back to 2009 when Joe Weir began representing injured people there. Together the founding partners bring more than 45 years of combined legal experience, much of it gained working as insurance defense attorneys before they shifted their practice to representing injured people. The firm handles car and truck accidents, motorcycle crashes, slip and fall and other premises liability claims, workers' compensation, wrongful death, dog bites, and other personal injury matters. Weir & Kestner has reported more than $5 million in publicly shared case results, including a $1,800,000 negligence recovery, a $1,000,000 motorcycle accident recovery, an $850,000 truck accident recovery, a $500,000 truck accident recovery, and a $350,000 slip and fall recovery, along with many confidential settlements. The firm has been recognized among the National Top 100 Trial Lawyers and America's Top 100 Personal Injury Attorneys, and is Lead Counsel Verified. Every case is assigned a dedicated case manager, Spanish-speaking help is available, and clients pay no attorney's fee unless the firm recovers compensation for them. Weir & Kestner Injury Lawyers is located at 128 1/2 N Church St, Murfreesboro, TN 37130 and can be reached by phone at 615-220-4180.
