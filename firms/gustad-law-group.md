# Gustad Law Group

Gustad Law Group, PLLC is a Washington law firm focused on Social Security Disability, VA disability and personal injury claims, with offices in Seattle, Tacoma and Spokane and clients across the state, including Olympia, Yakima and Wenatchee. Founder John-Paul Gustad has practiced law for more than 25 years and has represented thousands of people seeking benefits. He is admitted to practice before the Social Security Administration and is a VA-accredited attorney. The firm handles SSDI and SSI applications, reconsiderations, hearings before administrative law judges and federal appeals, along with VA service connection claims, increased ratings, TDIU, Aid and Attendance and VA appeals, for clients in Washington and nationwide. It also represents people hurt in car, truck and motorcycle accidents and families facing nursing home neglect. John-Paul Gustad is a member of the National Organization of Social Security Claimants' Representatives and has received an Avvo Clients' Choice Award. The firm speaks Spanish and charges no fee unless it wins. Gustad Law Group is located at 7815 Greenwood Ave N, Seattle, WA 98103 and can be reached by phone at 206-533-2222.
