# Anthony Z. Vargas, Esq. Attorney at Law

Anthony Z. Vargas, Esq. Attorney at Law is a San Diego employment law firm representing employees throughout San Diego County in disputes with their employers. Anthony Vargas, a graduate of California Western School of Law, served as an attorney with the San Diego Office of the Public Defender, where he handled thousands of misdemeanor and felony cases and tried them in courtrooms in downtown San Diego, Vista, El Cajon, and Chula Vista. He now applies that trial experience to workplace harassment, employment discrimination, wrongful termination, whistleblower and workplace retaliation, wage and hour violations, employee misclassification, and disability accommodation. He is a two-time recipient of the American College of Trial Lawyers Medal for Excellence in Advocacy and was named a Super Lawyers Rising Star in 2025 and 2026. He also teaches trial advocacy at California Western School of Law and the National Institute of Trial Advocacy. Anthony handles each case personally, speaks English and Spanish, and takes most cases on a contingency fee, so clients owe no attorney fee unless money is recovered. Anthony Z. Vargas, Esq. Attorney at Law is located at 8880 Rio San Diego Dr, Suite 800, San Diego, CA 92108 and can be reached by phone at 858-285-4595.
