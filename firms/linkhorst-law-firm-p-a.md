# Linkhorst Law Firm, P.A

Linkhorst Law Firm, P.A. is a construction law and litigation firm based in Jupiter serving contractors, subcontractors, owners, condominium and homeowners associations, design professionals and sureties throughout Florida, with a concentration on the Atlantic Coast from Vero Beach to the Florida Keys. Managing partner Adam C. Linkhorst has focused on construction law since his admission to The Florida Bar in 1995 and was among the first class of Florida attorneys Board Certified in Construction Law in 2005. He is a Florida Supreme Court certified Circuit Court Mediator, holds an AV Preeminent rating from Martindale-Hubbell and serves as general counsel of the Florida East Coast Chapter of the Associated General Contractors. He practices alongside partner R. Scott Clayton. The firm handles bid protests, bond claims, construction contracts, lien law, delay claims, defective construction, default and convenience terminations, and design professional liability. Its reported appellate decisions include an affirmed zero liability judgment for ITT Industries as design-builder of a reverse osmosis water treatment plant. The firm emphasizes early dispute resolution to control litigation costs. Linkhorst Law Firm, P.A. is located at 4495 Military Trail, Suite 106, Jupiter, FL 33458 and can be reached by phone at 561-626-8880.
