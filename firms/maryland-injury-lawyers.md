# Maryland Injury Lawyers

Maryland Injury Lawyers is a personal injury law firm based in Baltimore that represents injured people across Maryland, from Annapolis, Bethesda and Rockville to Frederick, Hagerstown, Salisbury and Ocean City. Backed by the attorneys of Berman \| Sobin \| Gross LLP, including partner Craig Meyers, the team brings more than 30 years of legal experience to car, truck and motorcycle accidents, medical malpractice and birth injuries, defective products and drugs, premises liability, nursing home negligence, catastrophic injuries and wrongful death claims. Its results include a $44 million medical malpractice verdict, a $4 million surgical burn verdict and a $5.5 million negligence settlement. The firm has been recognized by Best Lawyers, Best Law Firms and The National Trial Lawyers Top 100, and it builds each case with Maryland's strict contributory negligence rule in mind. Clients work directly with the lawyer handling their case, and there is no fee unless the firm recovers compensation. Maryland Injury Lawyers is located at 233 E Redwood Street, Suite 400F, Baltimore, MD 21202 and can be reached by phone at 866-836-4878.
