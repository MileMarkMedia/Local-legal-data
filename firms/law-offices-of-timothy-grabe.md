# Law Offices of Timothy Grabe

The Law Offices of Timothy Grabe is a Portland personal injury firm that has represented accident victims throughout Oregon since 1983. Founding attorney Tim Grabe graduated from the University of Oregon and Lewis & Clark Law School, and he worked in criminal defense and represented large insurance companies before focusing on injured clients, giving him insight into how insurers evaluate claims. The firm handles car, truck, motorcycle, pedestrian and bicycle accidents, construction and railroad worker injuries, premises liability, product liability, catastrophic injuries and wrongful death. Reported results include $27 million in a drunk driving accident case and $1.2 million for an assault injury. Grabe has been appointed by the Oregon Supreme Court as a pro tem judge in Multnomah County Circuit Court. He was a contributing author to Courtroom Companion: Oregon Evidence, which received the Association of Continuing Legal Education's Award of Professional Excellence for 2024. The firm limits its caseload, works on a contingency fee basis, and brings in medical experts and co-counsel when needed. The Law Offices of Timothy Grabe is located at 909 SW St. Clair Ave, Suite 1C, Portland, OR 97205 and can be reached by phone at 971-645-7176.
