# Rinaldo Law Group

Rinaldo Law Group is a Tampa personal injury firm founded by Catherine M. Rinaldo, a member of The Florida Bar since 1985, serving clients throughout Tampa and Hillsborough County. The firm handles car, truck, motorcycle, bicycle, and pedestrian accidents, wrongful death, medical malpractice, premises liability, and uninsured motorist claims. Built on the principles of dignity and trust, the firm brings more than 30 years of experience representing injured clients against negligent parties and their insurance companies. Results include an $8.25 million wrongful death recovery, a $4 million truck accident recovery, and a $1.65 million recovery for rehab facility neglect. Catherine Rinaldo holds an AV Preeminent rating from Martindale-Hubbell and an Excellent rating from Avvo, is a member of the Multi-Million Dollar Advocates Forum and Million Dollar Advocates Forum, and is a graduate and faculty member of the Keenan Ball Trial College. She has been featured on ABC Action News and Tampa Bay's Morning Blend. The firm offers free consultations, works on a contingency basis, makes home and hospital visits, and serves Spanish speaking clients. Rinaldo Law Group is located at 3800 Bay to Bay Blvd, Tampa, FL 33629 and can be reached by phone at 813-831-9999.
