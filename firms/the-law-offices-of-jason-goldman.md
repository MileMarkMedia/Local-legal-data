# The Law Offices of Jason Goldman

The Law Offices of Jason Goldman is a New York City criminal defense firm representing people who are under investigation, arrested or facing charges in state and federal court. Founder Jason Goldman is a former Brooklyn prosecutor who has tried more than 40 cases to verdict, is admitted in New York and New Jersey and in the Southern and Eastern Districts of New York, and has been named a New York Super Lawyers Rising Star. The firm handles federal wire fraud and financial crimes, drug conspiracy, RICO, grand jury subpoenas and target letters, homicide, sexual assault, domestic violence, sentencing and appeals to the Second Circuit. Notable outcomes include a grand jury declining to indict in the J train subway self-defense case, dismissed murder charges, a rape conviction reversed on appeal, and below-guidelines sentences in federal fraud and drug cases. His work has been covered by the Associated Press, CNN, the New York Post and New York Magazine. The firm also represents survivors of sexual assault in a practice kept separate from its defense work. The Law Offices of Jason Goldman is located at 275 Madison Ave, 35th Floor, New York, NY 10016 and can be reached by phone at 212-466-6617.
