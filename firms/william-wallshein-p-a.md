# William Wallshein P.A.

William Wallshein P.A. is a West Palm Beach law firm focused on criminal defense and family law throughout Palm Beach County, Broward County and South Florida. Attorney William Wallshein has more than 38 years of legal experience, including five years as a prosecutor in Palm Beach County, and he is admitted to practice in federal court. His CPA background informs family law matters involving business valuation and property division. He defends clients in state and federal courts against charges including DUI, drug offenses, firearms and weapons charges, domestic violence, sex crimes, juvenile crimes, federal and white collar crimes, and violent crimes, and he also handles expungements, grand jury proceedings and criminal appeals. On the family law side, he handles contested, uncontested and collaborative divorce, child custody and parenting plans, child support, alimony, grandparents' rights and gestational surrogacy. He works directly with clients, offers a free initial consultation, and has received the Avvo Client's Choice Award. William Wallshein P.A. is located at 1601 Forum Pl #602, West Palm Beach, FL 33401 and can be reached by phone at 561-533-1221.
