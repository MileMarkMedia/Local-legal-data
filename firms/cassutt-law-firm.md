# Cassutt Law Firm

Cassutt Law Firm is an Alpharetta, Georgia law firm focused on business and real estate law. The firm is led by Attorney Kenneth Cassutt, who represents individuals, business owners, entrepreneurs, investors, and companies throughout Alpharetta and the surrounding communities. His practice covers business transactions, business litigation, real estate transactions, and real estate litigation, including business formation and entity selection, business purchases and sales, contract drafting and negotiation, operating and partnership agreements, shareholder and ownership agreements, and business succession matters. He also represents clients in business disputes involving breach of contract, partnership conflicts, and ownership interests, evaluating opportunities for negotiation and settlement while remaining prepared to litigate when necessary. On the real estate side, the firm assists with residential and commercial real estate transactions, commercial leases, land use and zoning, and real estate litigation such as title disputes and boundary or easement disputes. The firm serves clients in Alpharetta, Roswell, Sandy Springs, Cumming, Duluth, Johns Creek, and Suwanee. Kenneth Cassutt emphasizes clear communication, accessibility, and keeping clients informed throughout their legal matters. Cassutt Law Firm is located at 11175 Cicero Dr., Building 200, Suite 100, Alpharetta, GA 30022 and can be reached by phone at 678-733-1284.
