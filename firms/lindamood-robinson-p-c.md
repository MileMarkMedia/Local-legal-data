# Lindamood & Robinson, P.C.

Lindamood & Robinson, P.C. is a Houston family law firm focused on divorce and related matters, serving clients in Harris, Galveston, Fort Bend, Montgomery and Brazoria Counties. For over 40 years, the firm and its predecessor firm have guided clients through divorce, child custody, child support, alimony, property division and complex marital estates, along with modification and enforcement, relocation and paternity matters. Steven P. Lindamood, active in trial practice since 1984, is Board Certified in Family Law and in Civil Trial Law by the Texas Board of Legal Specialization and is a Fellow of the American Academy of Matrimonial Lawyers. Lorie M. Robinson is Board Certified in Family Law by the Texas Board of Legal Specialization, and Of Counsel Thomas R. Conner has been Board Certified in Family Law for 40 years and holds an AV rating from Martindale-Hubbell. Together the attorneys bring a combined 90 years of family law experience, and the firm has been rated by Super Lawyers for 15 years. The firm guides clients through difficult decisions rather than pressuring them. Consultations for Galveston, Fort Bend and Montgomery County clients are available in person or by Zoom. Lindamood & Robinson, P.C. is located at 1415 Louisiana St, Suite 3450, Houston, TX 77002 and can be reached by phone at 713-654-2112.
