# Law Offices of Carol M. Galloway, P.A.

The Law Offices of Carol M. Galloway, P.A. is a Jacksonville bankruptcy and debt relief firm representing individuals, families and small business owners throughout Northeast Florida and Central Florida. Attorney Carol M. Galloway, admitted to The Florida Bar in 2002, brings decades of experience with federal bankruptcy law and Florida debt relief statutes, handling Chapter 7 and Chapter 13 bankruptcy, business bankruptcy, foreclosure defense, mortgage modifications, wage garnishment, creditor harassment and asset protection. She is a Supreme Court of Florida Circuit Civil Mediator who regularly mediates mortgage loan modifications for bankruptcy debtors, and since 2015 she has also handled employment law matters involving workplace discrimination, harassment and retaliation. Galloway is a member of the Jacksonville Bankruptcy Bar Association and the National Association of Consumer Advocates. The firm serves Jacksonville, the Beaches, Orange Park, St. Augustine, Fernandina Beach and Palm Coast, with Orlando appointments available, and offers free consultations and payment plans. The Law Offices of Carol M. Galloway, P.A. is located at 301 West Bay Street, Suite 1451-52, Jacksonville, FL 32202 and can be reached by phone at 904-694-5489.
