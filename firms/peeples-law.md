# Peeples Law

Peeples Law is a Birmingham divorce and family law firm serving families in Jefferson County and throughout Alabama, including Hoover, Vestavia Hills, Mountain Brook, Homewood, Pelham, and Trussville. Divorce and family law are the firm's only areas of practice, covering contested, uncontested, collaborative, military, and high-asset divorce, alimony, property and retirement division, child custody and support, relocation, modifications, adoption, paternity, and prenuptial and postnuptial agreements. Attorney Candace B. Peeples, known as Candi, is a trial lawyer who graduated from the University of Georgia and earned her law degree cum laude from Samford University's Cumberland School of Law, where she competed on the school's trial teams. She has extensive experience in high net worth divorces involving complex asset division. Partner Julia G. Williams is admitted to practice in Alabama, Tennessee, and Mississippi. The firm's attorneys are certified mediators and take a team approach that favors negotiation and mediation over a scorched earth strategy, while remaining ready to go to court when necessary and keeping the focus on the children's interests. Peeples Law is located at 2956 Rhodes Cir S, Birmingham, AL 35205 and can be reached by phone at 205-403-5577.
