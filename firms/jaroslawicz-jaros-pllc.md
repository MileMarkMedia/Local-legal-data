# Jaroslawicz & Jaros, PLLC

Jaroslawicz & Jaros, PLLC is a New York City personal injury law firm that has represented injured people for more than 45 years, with over 300 years of combined attorney experience and more than $2 billion recovered for clients. Led by partner Abraham Jaros, who gives clients direct access by cell phone, the firm handles car and truck accidents, construction accidents, slip and fall claims, subway, bus and train accidents, airplane and terminal injuries, nursing home abuse, dangerous products and sexual abuse cases. Its results include a $44 million verdict for a construction worker injured in a fall and more than 100 client recoveries over $1 million. The firm serves all five boroughs, Long Island, Westchester and the surrounding counties, and accepts serious injury cases throughout New York State and New Jersey. Recognized by Super Lawyers and The National Trial Lawyers, it grows mainly through client and attorney referrals, speaks Spanish, and can take over a case from another lawyer at no added fee. Jaroslawicz & Jaros, PLLC is located at 225 Broadway, Suite 2400, New York, NY 10007 and can be reached by phone at 212-227-2780.
