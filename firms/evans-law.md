# Evans Law

Evans Law is an Atlanta law firm focused on real estate litigation, serving clients throughout Georgia. Led by attorney Andrew Evans, who has more than 20 years of experience, the firm handles wrongful and lender foreclosures, tax sale excess funds and surplus proceeds recovery, quiet title actions, adverse possession, eminent domain and condemnation, banking disputes, real property litigation, business and commercial litigation, insurance claims, probate, and landlord-tenant matters. Andrew Evans graduated summa cum laude and Phi Beta Kappa from the University of Texas at Austin and earned his law degree cum laude from the University of Georgia School of Law, where he served as an editor of the Journal of International Law. He has won high-dollar disputes against opponents including Citi Financial and USAA. The firm represents homeowners, heirs, real estate investors and private lenders, and helps individuals recover surplus funds from tax sales and foreclosure auctions. It serves Fulton, DeKalb, Cobb, Clayton, Gwinnett, Henry and Rockdale Counties, along with cities such as Athens, Augusta, Columbus, Macon, and Savannah, and offers a free consultation. Evans Law is located at 750 Piedmont Ave NE, Atlanta, GA 30308 and can be reached by phone at 404-276-0629.
