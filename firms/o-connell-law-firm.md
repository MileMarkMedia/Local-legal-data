# O'Connell Law Firm

O'Connell Law Firm focuses exclusively on Georgia workers' compensation and work injury claims, serving injured workers in Decatur, Atlanta, and communities across Georgia, including Brookhaven, Tucker, Stone Mountain, and Marietta. The firm is led by brothers Andrew M. O'Connell and Daniel F. O'Connell, who grew up in Decatur. Andrew worked for defense firms representing employers and insurers before founding the firm to represent injured workers only, and Dan, who has represented injured workers in Georgia for over a decade, previously served as a staff attorney and mediator for the Georgia State Board of Workers' Compensation. The firm handles back and neck injuries, catastrophic injuries, occupational disease, and chemical and asbestos exposure claims for workers in fields ranging from construction and trucking to healthcare and restaurant work. Reported results include a $1 million third party settlement for hip, neck, and back injuries and a $750,000 recovery for complex regional pain syndrome. The firm has been recognized by Super Lawyers. Consultations are free, fees are charged only if benefits are recovered, and clients speak directly with their attorney rather than a case manager. O'Connell Law Firm is located at 1455 Church St, Decatur, GA 30030 and can be reached by phone at 404-410-0034.
