# Hollander & Hanuka

Hollander & Hanuka is a criminal defense firm serving Fort Myers and Lee County, including Fort Myers Beach, North Fort Myers, Bonita Springs, Cape Coral, Estero, and Lehigh Acres, with an additional office in Naples serving Collier County. Attorney Dan Hanuka, a 1996 graduate of the University of Miami School of Law, has practiced criminal defense in Florida since 1997. He served three years as a Collier County public defender, including as Misdemeanor and Juvenile Division Supervisor, and was twice named Attorney of the Year for that office. He is licensed in all Florida state courts, is admitted to the U.S. District Court for the Middle District of Florida, and has served as Treasurer of the Lee County chapter of the Florida Association of Criminal Defense Lawyers since 2001. The firm handles state and federal misdemeanor and felony cases, including violation of probation, DUI, drug possession and trafficking, domestic violence, theft, burglary, robbery, violent crimes, firearm offenses, juvenile crimes, and traffic violations. Each prospective client receives a free, extensive initial consultation and step-by-step guidance with clear expectations. Hollander & Hanuka is located at 1375 Jackson Street, Suite 405, Fort Myers, FL 33901 and can be reached by phone at 239-332-3300.
