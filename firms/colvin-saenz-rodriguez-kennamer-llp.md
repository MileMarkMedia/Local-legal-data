# Colvin, Saenz, Rodriguez & Kennamer LLP

Colvin, Saenz, Rodriguez & Kennamer LLP is a South Texas civil litigation firm serving Brownsville, Edinburg, the Rio Grande Valley, and communities including Harlingen, McAllen, Laredo, and Corpus Christi. The firm traces its roots to 1950, when it was founded as Sharpe, Cunningham & Garza, and founding partner Reynaldo G. Garza later became the first Hispanic U.S. Federal District Court judge. Across more than 70 years, the firm has defended businesses, manufacturers, and insurers in products liability, insurance coverage and defense, trucking defense, toxic torts, commercial and construction litigation, professional negligence, labor and employment, and family law matters. Named partners Norton A. Colvin, Jaime A. Saenz, Joseph A. Rodriguez, and Alison Kennamer lead a team recognized by Best Lawyers and Best Law Firms. The firm serves as regional counsel for Ford Motor Company, has represented Missouri Pacific and Union Pacific Railroad since 1950, and represents manufacturers of vehicles, tires, construction equipment, and other products across the country. Colvin, Saenz, Rodriguez & Kennamer LLP is located at 1201 East Van Buren St., Brownsville, TX 78520 and can be reached by phone at 956-542-7441.
