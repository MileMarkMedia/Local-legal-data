# The Baez Law Firm

The Baez Law Firm is a criminal defense, civil rights and civil litigation firm with offices in Miami and Orlando, serving clients throughout Florida and in cases across the country. The firm is led by trial lawyer Jose A. Baez, who secured a full acquittal on first-degree murder charges in the Casey Anthony trial and teaches trial techniques as a faculty member at Harvard Law School. Results the firm reports include the acquittal of NFL player Aaron Hernandez on double homicide charges in Boston, an Ohio doctor acquitted of 25 counts of murder, and cardiologists acquitted of 50 federal health care fraud counts. Jose Baez was named Lawyer of the Year for 2011 by LawyersUSA and to The National Trial Lawyers Top 100 Trial Lawyers from 2011 through 2014. Managing Partner Michelle Medina, who began her career with the Office of the Public Defender in the 9th Judicial Circuit, has been with the firm for more than 15 years. The firm handles murder, federal crimes, white collar and health care fraud, drug and sex offenses, and appeals, and uses independent forensic testing of DNA, fingerprints and digital evidence. Jose Baez and Michelle Medina speak Spanish, and the firm offers free consultations and 24/7 availability. The Baez Law Firm is located at 1200 Brickell Avenue, Suite 1410, Miami, FL 33131 and can be reached by phone at 305-999-5100.
