# Bowman Law Firm

Bowman Law Firm is an estate planning and elder law practice in Norcross serving metro Atlanta and communities throughout Georgia, including Gwinnett, DeKalb, Cobb, Forsyth, Hall, and Barrow counties. Attorney Shireen Hormozdi Bowman has practiced law since 2003, bringing more than 20 years of experience. She previously tried cases for a national consumer law firm, served as a law clerk to a federal judge in Georgia, and handled personal injury defense for a large Florida firm before opening her own Norcross practice in 2012. The firm focuses on revocable living trusts, charitable and life insurance trusts, Medicaid asset protection trusts, wills and pour-over wills, powers of attorney, advance directives, trust funding and administration, and long-term care and Medicaid planning, helping families avoid probate, protect assets, and plan for incapacity, and it also handles probate. Services are available in English, Spanish, and Vietnamese. Each client receives personalized attention, from a free discovery call to an in-person signing meeting where the completed estate plan is presented. Bowman Law Firm is located at 1770 Indian Trail Lilburn Rd, Suite 320, Norcross, GA 30093 and can be reached by phone at 678-395-7795.
