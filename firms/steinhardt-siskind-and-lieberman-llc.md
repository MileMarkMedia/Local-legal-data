# Steinhardt, Siskind and Lieberman, LLC

Steinhardt, Siskind and Lieberman, LLC is a Glen Burnie law firm representing disabled and injured people throughout Maryland in Social Security disability, long-term disability, workers' compensation, and personal injury claims. Attorneys Michael D. Steinhardt, Keith R. Siskind, and Jordan E. Lieberman bring over 80 years of combined legal experience. Michael Steinhardt has practiced law for over 40 years, Keith Siskind has practiced since 1987 and handles most of the firm's litigation, and Jordan Lieberman concentrates on Social Security disability and SSI claims and regularly leads seminars for claimants. The firm represents injured workers including construction, hospital, and warehouse employees, police officers, and firefighters, and handles car and truck accidents, motorcycle accidents, slip and fall, medical negligence, and wrongful death. In addition to the Glen Burnie office, the firm meets clients by appointment in Owings Mills, Ellicott City, and Annapolis, and evening and weekend appointments can be arranged. Initial consultations are free, and there are no attorney fees or expenses unless the firm wins the case. Steinhardt, Siskind and Lieberman, LLC is located at 808 Landmark Dr, Suite 227, Glen Burnie, MD 21061 and can be reached by phone at 410-766-7630.
