# Lobo Law

Lobo Law is a Las Vegas criminal defense firm led by attorney Adrian Marie Lobo, who has more than twelve years of experience as a Las Vegas criminal lawyer and serves clients throughout Clark County and other areas of Nevada, including Washoe, Elko and White Pine counties. The firm represents individuals accused of offenses ranging from misdemeanors to serious felonies, including drug crimes, DUI, domestic violence, sex crimes, violent crimes, theft, weapons charges, white collar crimes and federal charges, and it also handles personal injury matters. Lobo's trial experience includes a medically complex murder case involving shaken baby syndrome, and reported results include a murder charge dismissed by the state and attempted murder charges dismissed in a case negotiated down to a firearm possession charge. A Franny Forsman Award recipient at the National Criminal Defense College, she has taught advanced trial skills and coached trial teams at California Western School of Law and the UNLV Boyd School of Law. She is a member of Nevada Attorneys for Criminal Justice and the National Association of Criminal Defense Lawyers. The firm stays involved through every stage of a case, and Spanish is spoken. Lobo Law is located at 8872 S Eastern Ave, Suite 250, Las Vegas, NV 89123 and can be reached by phone at 702-290-8998.
