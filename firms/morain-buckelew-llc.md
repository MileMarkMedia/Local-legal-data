# Morain & Buckelew, LLC

Morain & Buckelew, LLC is an Atlanta personal injury and workers' compensation law firm that has represented injured people and their families since 2011. Its four attorneys, Grant G. Morain, Brian J. Buckelew, Paul Allmon and Louis Schiappa, bring more than 90 years of combined legal experience to car, truck and motorcycle accidents, pedestrian, bicycle and rideshare crashes, premises liability and slip and fall claims, construction and workplace injuries, workers' compensation, nursing home abuse, catastrophic injuries and wrongful death. Results include a $1 million recovery for a construction worker injured when a ladder dislodged and a $500,000 recovery in a rear-end collision case. The firm is recognized by Super Lawyers, Best Lawyers and Avvo, and it serves clients in Buckhead, Midtown, Downtown, Decatur, Sandy Springs, Brookhaven and across metro Atlanta, with Spanish-language resources available. Every case is prepared as though it will go to trial in Fulton County or federal court. Morain & Buckelew, LLC is located at 1465 Northside Dr NW, Suite 211, Atlanta, GA 30318 and can be reached by phone at 404-448-3146.
