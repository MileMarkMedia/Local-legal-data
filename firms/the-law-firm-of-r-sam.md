# The Law Firm of R. Sam

The Law Firm of R. Sam is a personal injury law firm serving Modesto, Stockton and California's Central Valley, with offices in Modesto, Stockton, Sacramento, Fresno, Oakland and Milpitas. Attorney Roeuth Sam founded the firm after practicing at one of California's largest plaintiff personal injury firms. He speaks Cambodian (Khmer), and the firm's staff serves Spanish-speaking clients. The firm represents people hurt in car, truck, motorcycle, pedestrian and bicycle accidents, rideshare and drunk driving crashes, slip, trip and fall incidents, dog bites, and boating and jet ski accidents, along with brain injury, spinal cord injury and wrongful death claims. Its results include a $2.7 million wrongful death jury verdict and a $1.9 million truck accident jury verdict, and the firm has received American Institute of Trial Lawyers honors in 2020, 2023 and 2026. The firm meets clients after hours, on weekends, at home or in the hospital, and charges nothing unless it recovers compensation. The Law Firm of R. Sam is located at 1800 Tully Rd, Suite B-2, Modesto, CA 95350 and can be reached by phone at 209-222-3000.
