# TNL MIAMI, PLLC

TNL MIAMI, PLLC is a Florida family law and mediation firm based in Miami, serving families statewide through in-person and virtual sessions. The firm was founded by Daniel Umbert, its lead attorney and a Florida Supreme Court Certified Mediator in Marital and Family Law, whose career has focused on Florida family law since he earned his law degree from the University of Miami School of Law in 2014. He previously served as an Assistant Attorney General with the Florida Department of Revenue Child Support Program in Broward County and as an Assistant State Attorney handling child support enforcement in Miami-Dade County. The firm mediates divorce, child custody, child support, alimony, parenting plan, relocation, paternity, grandparent, high-conflict parenting and post-judgment disputes, and also handles prenuptial agreements, equitable distribution, domestic violence and post-divorce modifications. Its neutral, solution-focused sessions help parties reach private agreements outside court. Daniel Umbert and the firm's staff are fluent in Spanish. The firm also has an office in Delray Beach. TNL MIAMI, PLLC is located at 390 NE 191st St, Suite 8368, Miami, FL 33179 and can be reached by phone at 786-730-6444.
