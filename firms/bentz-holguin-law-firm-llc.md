# Bentz Holguin Law Firm, LLC

Bentz Holguin Law Firm, LLC is a Chicago bankruptcy and debt relief firm serving clients throughout the Chicago area, with additional work in IRS debt negotiation, foreclosure defense, and personal injury. Managing partner Jessica Bentz Holguin established the firm in 2009 after working at a large Chicago bankruptcy firm, has more than 15 years of bankruptcy experience, and is licensed in Illinois and Florida. She has litigated hundreds of cases and participated in a reported case in which the Supreme Court of Florida affirmed an award of social security benefits she obtained for a dependent client. Attorney Adam Holguin works almost exclusively on bankruptcy and personal injury matters, and together the attorneys bring more than 30 years of combined experience. The firm handles Chapter 7 and Chapter 13 bankruptcy, wage garnishment, debt collector harassment, IRS offers in compromise, and tax resolution. The attorneys have been recognized by Super Lawyers and Top Attorneys. Clients work directly with the attorneys rather than junior staff, and the firm offers free consultations, $0 down to start, and affordable payment plans. Bentz Holguin Law Firm, LLC is located at 27 N. Wacker Dr, Suite 310, Chicago, IL 60606 and can be reached by phone at 312-647-2116.
