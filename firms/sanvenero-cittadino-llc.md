# Sanvenero & Cittadino, LLC

Sanvenero & Cittadino, LLC is a Shrewsbury law firm focused on family law and divorce, serving Red Bank, Monmouth County, and clients across Ocean and Middlesex counties in New Jersey. The firm was founded by managing partner Richard Sanvenero and partner Joshua Cittadino. Richard Sanvenero is a New Jersey Supreme Court Certified Matrimonial Attorney, a former law clerk in the Family Part of the Superior Court in Middlesex Vicinage, and is trained in mediation, arbitration, and collaborative divorce. The firm handles divorce, alimony, child custody, child support, equitable distribution, domestic violence, high asset divorce, relocation, post-judgment matters, and prenuptial and postnuptial agreements, along with business law, criminal and municipal court defense, real estate, estate planning, civil litigation, and personal injury. Its attorneys have been recognized by Super Lawyers and Rising Stars and hold Avvo ratings. Clients in Rumson, Middletown, Holmdel, Freehold, Long Branch, and Toms River work with one coordinated team that explains options in plain language and prepares every matter for settlement or trial. Sanvenero & Cittadino, LLC is located at 740 Broad St, Suite 200, Shrewsbury, NJ 07702 and can be reached by phone at 732-743-9665.
