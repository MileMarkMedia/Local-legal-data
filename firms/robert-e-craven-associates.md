# Robert E. Craven & Associates

Robert E. Craven & Associates is a North Kingstown personal injury law firm representing accident victims throughout Rhode Island in car and truck accidents, drunk and distracted driving cases, uninsured driver claims, dog bites, slip and fall claims, and wrongful death matters. The firm has more than 30 years of experience representing injured people. Founder Robert E. Craven began his legal career in 1983 and served as a Rhode Island Assistant Attorney General from 1983 to 1992, where he was lead trial lawyer and Chief of the Public Corruptions Unit. He has served in the Rhode Island House of Representatives for North Kingstown District 32 since 2012 and as Town Solicitor in Charlestown since 2007. He is recognized by Rhode Island Super Lawyers in Plaintiff's Personal Injury Law, is a Rhode Island Bar Foundation Fellow, and received the Rhode Island Monthly Professional Excellence in Law Award. He works alongside attorney Nicholas A. Solitro. Cases are handled on a contingency fee basis, the firm advances case costs, and consultations are free, with legal procedures explained to clients throughout their case. Robert E. Craven & Associates is located at 7405 Post Road, North Kingstown, RI 02852 and can be reached by phone at 401-453-2700.
