# Moore Law

Moore Law is a Tampa personal injury law firm founded in 2019 by principal attorney Eric Moore, who has practiced law for nearly 30 years and spent 25 years representing both plaintiffs and defendants in personal injury cases before opening the firm. A 1993 honors graduate of Mercer University's Walter F. George School of Law, he began his career at a firm that defended lawsuits for insurers, giving him direct insight into insurance company tactics. The firm handles auto, truck, motorcycle, bicycle, and pedestrian accidents, Uber and Lyft crashes, slip and fall and premises liability claims, dog bites, defective products, school injuries, catastrophic injuries, and wrongful death. Moore Law serves clients in Hillsborough, Pinellas, Pasco, Polk, Manatee, and Sarasota Counties, including Tampa, Carrollwood, Land O' Lakes, Lutz, and Wesley Chapel. Cases are handled on a contingency fee basis with costs advanced by the firm, and clients can meet in the Tampa office, at their own location, or remotely, with evening and weekend appointments and a client portal for case documents. Moore Law is located at 14001 N Dale Mabry Hwy, Tampa, FL 33618 and can be reached by phone at 813-510-5400.
