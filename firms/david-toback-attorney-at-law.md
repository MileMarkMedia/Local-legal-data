# David Toback, Attorney At Law

David Toback, Attorney At Law is a Tampa estate planning and tax law firm with offices in Tampa and St. Petersburg, serving clients throughout Hillsborough and Pinellas Counties, including Lutz. Founder David S. Toback concentrates on estate planning and probate, with an emphasis on tax planning, asset protection, and business succession planning, and brings more than 20 years of legal experience centered on estate and tax matters. He earned a bachelor's degree in accounting magna cum laude from the University of South Florida and his law degree cum laude from Washington and Lee University School of Law. He began his career as a State and Local Tax Manager at PricewaterhouseCoopers before spending nearly ten years at a Tampa boutique tax firm. His work includes wills, trusts, powers of attorney, living wills, business formation and transactions, and resolving tax controversies before the IRS and state and local taxing authorities. The firm also assists with probate, elder law and Medicaid planning, Florida domicile issues, and international estate planning. David Toback, Attorney At Law is located at 1211 N. Westshore Blvd, Suite 315, Tampa, FL 33607 and can be reached by phone at 813-252-7529.
