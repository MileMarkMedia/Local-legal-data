# Joshi Law Firm, PA

Joshi Law Firm, PA is an Orlando criminal defense firm handling state and federal cases across Central Florida and the state. Founding partner Rajan Joshi, a University of Florida College of Law graduate, began his career as an Assistant State Attorney in Jacksonville, received a 2003 Jury Trial Award, and later became a partner at Nejame Law Firm before founding his own firm, bringing more than 20 years of criminal defense experience. The firm defends clients facing DUI, drug possession and trafficking, assault and battery, domestic violence, sex offenses, theft, robbery, murder and manslaughter, white collar and federal crimes, probation violations, and record sealing matters. Results include not guilty verdicts on charges of firearm possession by a convicted felon after the firm was hired six days before trial, sexual battery on a minor, and second and third DUI offenses. Rajan Joshi has been recognized with Super Lawyers, an Avvo Rating of 10.0 Superb, and The National Trial Lawyers Top 100 Trial Lawyers, and the firm has been featured on CNN, Fox, The Today Show, Dr. Phil, WESH 2, News6, and WFTV9. The firm offers free consultations and builds a personalized defense strategy for each case. Joshi Law Firm, PA is located at 5750 Major Blvd, Unit 530, Orlando, FL 32819 and can be reached by phone at 407-661-1109.
