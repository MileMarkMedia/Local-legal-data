# Yesner Law

Yesner Law is a Clearwater, Florida law firm focused on bankruptcy and real estate, serving Tampa and Hillsborough, Pasco, Pinellas, Polk and Manatee counties. Founder Shawn M. Yesner has practiced law since 1998 and began as an associate attorney at a large Florida lender foreclosure firm, representing lenders including Freddie Mac, the VA, Chase and Countrywide, before forming his own firm in 2004 and later founding Yesner Law. The firm handles Chapter 7 and Chapter 13 bankruptcy, debt alternatives such as loan modification, debt settlement, deed in lieu of foreclosure, debt lawsuit defense and creditor harassment, and foreclosure defense, along with commercial real estate, construction liens, landlord representation, mortgage and foreclosure creditor representation and short sales. Having represented thousands of consumers, lenders and investors, the firm works on both sides of foreclosure and bankruptcy matters. Clients work directly with Shawn Yesner at every step rather than being passed to associates or staff, and initial consultations are free. The firm also produces the Crushing Debt Podcast. Yesner Law is located at 2753 FL-580, Suite 106, Clearwater, FL 33761 and can be reached by phone at 727-261-0224.
