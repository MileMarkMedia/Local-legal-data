# Calhoun Law, PLC

Calhoun Law, PLC is a Nashville, Tennessee law firm focused on personal injury, medical malpractice, business litigation, and civil litigation, with offices in Nashville and Murfreesboro. Founder Colin B. Calhoun, a Tennessee trial lawyer admitted to practice in 2008, represents clients in medical malpractice, wrongful death, personal injury, products liability, and complex civil litigation, and works alongside attorney Joseph Swafford. The firm serves Nashville, Davidson County, and Middle Tennessee communities including Murfreesboro, Franklin, Brentwood, Hendersonville, and Mount Juliet, and also handles workers' compensation and Social Security disability claims. Results include $2.5 million in a commercial vehicle collision case, $1.25 million in a motor vehicle collision case, and $900,000 in a medical malpractice case. Colin Calhoun has been recognized by Super Lawyers, and the firm's attorneys have been recognized by the Million Dollar Advocates Forum. Clients work directly with an attorney rather than a rotating case management team, and injury cases are handled on a contingency fee basis with no fees unless compensation is recovered. Calhoun Law, PLC is located at 222 2nd Ave N, Suite 326, Nashville, TN 37201 and can be reached by phone at 615-250-8000.
