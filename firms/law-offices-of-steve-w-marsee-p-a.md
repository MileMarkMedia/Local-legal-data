# Law Offices of Steve W. Marsee, P.A.

The Law Offices of Steve W. Marsee, P.A. is an Orlando divorce and family law firm serving clients throughout Central Florida. Firm president Steve W. Marsee founded the firm in 2000 after a career in law enforcement, during which he worked as an undercover drug investigator, led intelligence and organized crime investigations for the Lake County Sheriff's Office, and became a police chief at age 29. He earned his J.D. from the University of Miami School of Law, is admitted in Florida and Washington, D.C., and is a Florida Supreme Court certified family law mediator. The firm handles contested and uncontested divorce, high net worth and business owner divorce, child custody, child support, alimony, property distribution, paternity, prenuptial and postnuptial agreements and domestic violence injunctions. Marsee received the Martindale-Hubbell Client Distinction Award in 2012 and was selected in 2015 by the National Association of Distinguished Counsel as a member of the nation's top one percent. The firm reports settling more than 95 percent of its cases at mediation and works with forensic accountants and business appraisers on complex asset cases. The Law Offices of Steve W. Marsee, P.A. is located at 4162 Edgewater Dr, Orlando, FL 32804 and can be reached by phone at 407-521-7171.
