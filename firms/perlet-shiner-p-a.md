# Perlet & Shiner, P.A.

Perlet & Shiner, P.A. is a West Palm Beach criminal defense firm representing clients in Palm Beach County and throughout Florida in state and federal court. Founded in 2000 by Heidi Perlet and Marc Shiner, the firm's team includes four former prosecutors. Heidi Perlet has 31 years of experience, has handled about 200 jury and non-jury trials, and was appointed by the Governor of Florida as a special prosecutor on a number of cases. Marc Shiner spent almost 13 years as a major crimes prosecutor with the State Attorney's Office and has handled 250 jury and non-jury trials, including high-profile sex crime and murder cases. The firm defends clients facing drug crimes and trafficking, DUI and BUI, sex crimes, violent crimes and homicide, theft and white collar offenses, health care fraud, firearms charges, federal crimes, juvenile matters, probation violations, and appeals. Marc Shiner holds an AV Preeminent rating from Martindale-Hubbell and has been named to The National Trial Lawyers Top 100. The firm offers free consultations and serves clients in English and Spanish. Perlet & Shiner, P.A. is located at 1801 Centrepark Drive East, Suite 110, West Palm Beach, FL 33401 and can be reached by phone at 561-721-0552.
