# Vanessa L. Prieto Law Offices, LLC

Vanessa L. Prieto Law Offices, LLC is a Fort Lauderdale firm focused on family law and civil litigation, serving clients throughout Broward, Miami-Dade and Palm Beach counties. Attorney Vanessa L. Prieto has more than 25 years of experience handling litigation across Florida, is a published author and has served as an Adjunct Professor at the Shepard Broad Law Center at Nova Southeastern University. The firm handles divorce, including high-conflict and military divorce, child custody and time-sharing, child relocation, alimony, child support, paternity, domestic violence, prenuptial and postnuptial agreements, marital settlement agreements, modifications and family law appeals, along with personal injury, civil appeals and post-judgment litigation. As a sole practitioner working closely with her staff, Ms. Prieto emphasizes communication, preparation and proactive strategy, aiming for a swift resolution with minimal court involvement. The firm offers a free initial consultation and also has an office in Boca Raton. Vanessa L. Prieto Law Offices, LLC is located at 1845 SE 4th Avenue, Fort Lauderdale, FL 33316 and can be reached by phone at 954-800-2362.
