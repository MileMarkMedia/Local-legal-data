# Zavala Law

Zavala Law is a Ventura personal injury law firm focused on car accidents, truck and motorcycle collisions, pedestrian and bicycle accidents, premises liability cases, dog bites and wrongful death claims. The firm serves clients throughout Ventura County, Santa Barbara County, San Luis Obispo County, Kern County, parts of Los Angeles County and the greater Southern California region. Founder Matt Zavala spent over a decade as an insurance defense attorney before starting the firm, and he has also served as a mediator and as an adjunct law school professor. He handles every case personally from the first call through settlement or trial and has recovered more than $10 million for injured clients, with reported results including a $5 million confidential settlement, a $1.3 million back surgery case and a $690,000 slip and fall recovery. Matt Zavala has been recognized by Super Lawyers and Rising Stars and is a member of the Million Dollar Advocates Forum, the Consumer Attorneys of California and the Consumer Attorneys Association of Los Angeles. He is fluent in Spanish. The firm works on a contingency fee basis and advances case costs. Zavala Law is located at 674 County Square Dr, Suite 207-A, Ventura, CA 93003 and can be reached by phone at 805-429-4292.
