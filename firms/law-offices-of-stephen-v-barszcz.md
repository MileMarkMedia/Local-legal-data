# Law Offices of Stephen V. Barszcz

The Law Offices of Stephen V. Barszcz is a national disability firm based in Pinellas Park, Florida, focused on OPM Disability Retirement for federal employees under FERS and CSRS, Social Security Disability, and Federal Workers' Compensation claims, including schedule awards. Founder Stephen V. Barszcz, a graduate of the University of Miami and Stetson University College of Law, began his career representing the government and large insurance companies before switching sides in the 1990s to represent only individuals and families. With more than 25 years of experience securing benefits for disabled and injured federal and private sector workers, he has obtained millions of dollars in medical treatment, lost wages and schedule awards for clients nationwide. Barszcz personally handles every case and limits the number of new cases he accepts each month, working directly with clients and with doctors and specialists across the country from the free consultation through resolution. He was recognized by Lawyers of Distinction in 2019, and under the firm's Peace of Mind Guarantee for OPM and Social Security claims, no fee is owed if the claim is not won. The Law Offices of Stephen V. Barszcz is located at 6251 Park Blvd., Suite 3, Pinellas Park, FL 33781 and can be reached by phone at 877-655-2667.
