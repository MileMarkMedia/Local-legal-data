# Franco Law Firm

Franco Law Firm is a bilingual Tampa law firm focused on workers' compensation, personal injury, and Social Security Disability claims for clients throughout the Tampa Bay area. The firm is led by Managing Partner Manny Franco, who was admitted to the Florida Bar in 1990 and to the United States District Court for the Middle District of Florida. He is a Certified Circuit Civil Mediator who conducts private mediations in workers' compensation and personal injury matters in West Central Florida and a frequent lecturer to attorney groups. He is a member of the Florida Bar Workers' Compensation Section, Florida Workers Advocates, and the Hillsborough County Bar Association and its Workers' Compensation Section. The workers' compensation practice covers construction accidents, first responder claims, CRPS, spine and brain injuries, occupational illness, and death benefits. The firm also handles auto, truck, motorcycle, rideshare, and pedestrian accidents, dog bites, and negligent security cases. Consultations are free, cases are taken on a contingency fee basis, and clients can meet in person, by Zoom, or after hours, in English or Spanish. Franco Law Firm is located at 800 W. Dr. M.L. King Jr. Boulevard, Suite 1, Tampa, FL 33603 and can be reached by phone at 813-872-0929.
