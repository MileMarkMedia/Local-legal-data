# Buckhead Family Law

Buckhead Family Law is an Atlanta family law firm handling divorce and related domestic relations matters throughout Atlanta and Fulton, Cobb, DeKalb, Gwinnett, and Forsyth counties, with offices in Atlanta and Marietta. Founder and principal attorney Melissa Davis has practiced family law at Atlanta firms since 2006, after beginning her career as a corporate litigation attorney at Powell Goldstein handling class action, product liability, and pharmaceutical cases in state and federal courts. The firm handles contested, uncontested, collaborative, high net worth, and military divorce, along with child custody, child support, alimony, property division, prenuptial and postnuptial agreements, paternity and legitimation, contempt, protective orders, grandparent visitation, and appeals. Recognition includes the Daily Report's Best of 2022, Client's Choice Awards and 10 Best honors. As a boutique practice, Buckhead Family Law offers mediation and collaborative options alongside courtroom litigation and works to protect family relationships both during and after a case. Buckhead Family Law is located at 3017 Bolling Way NE, First Floor, Atlanta, GA 30305 and can be reached by phone at 404-390-0000.
