# Larsen & Rico, PLLC

Larsen & Rico, PLLC is a Salt Lake City business litigation boutique with a national-level practice, led by attorneys Mark A. Larsen and Lisa C. Rico. Mark Larsen, who has practiced for more than four decades, holds an AV Distinguished rating from Martindale-Hubbell. The firm represents businesses and individuals, as both plaintiffs and defendants, in trials, appeals, arbitrations, and mediations involving breach of contract, business torts, shareholder disputes, real estate and construction litigation, financial fraud and embezzlement, professional malpractice, and intellectual property matters such as trade secrets and trademark disputes. Its attorneys have tried and argued cases in Utah, Idaho, Nevada, Arizona, California, Texas, Florida, Colorado, Wyoming, Minnesota, and New York, and other lawyers often refer their own business matters to the firm. Reported results include more than $3 million recovered in breach of contract litigation and $1.2 million recovered in an embezzlement and auditor malpractice case. The firm's partners have been recognized by Super Lawyers and Utah's Legal Elite. The firm serves Salt Lake, Davis, Utah, and Washington counties. Larsen & Rico, PLLC is located at 230 South 500 East, Suite 260, Salt Lake City, UT 84102 and can be reached by phone at 801-550-6500.
