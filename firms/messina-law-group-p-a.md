# Messina Law Group P.A.

Messina Law Group, P.A. is a probate, estate planning, and real estate law firm serving families throughout Hillsborough, Pasco, and Pinellas Counties and the greater Tampa Bay area, with offices in New Port Richey, Tampa, Palm Harbor, and Wesley Chapel. The firm was started by brothers Anthony J. Messina and Paul M. Messina, Jr., fourth generation Tampa natives, and has handled thousands of cases throughout Florida. Its work covers wills, trusts, revocable living trusts, special needs trusts, powers of attorney, Lady Bird deeds, formal and summary probate administration, ancillary and out of state probate, will challenges, trust administration, real estate closings, and business law. Paul Messina spent nearly eight years at Blank Rome, LLP, holds a Martindale-Hubbell AV Preeminent rating, and has been recognized by Best Lawyers: Ones to Watch and Super Lawyers. Anthony Messina focuses on real estate, business transactions, and probate and estate planning, and owns Keystone Title Agency. The firm offers in person and virtual consultations, along with mobile notary services for document signing. Messina Law Group, P.A. is located at 2550 Permit Pl, New Port Richey, FL 34655 and can be reached by phone at 813-492-7798.
