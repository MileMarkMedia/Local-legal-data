# Rosenbaum Law Group, PC

Rosenbaum Law Group, PC is a personal injury law firm serving Portland and clients throughout Oregon. Founded more than 40 years ago by Mike Rosenbaum, now retired, the firm represents injured people in car, truck, motorcycle, bicycle, pedestrian, and rideshare accidents, slip and fall and premises liability claims, brain, spinal, and other catastrophic injuries, workplace injuries, wrongful death, and first-party insurance disputes including PIP denials, UM/UIM coverage, and bad faith claims. The firm's attorneys include Ashley Rosenbaum-DePalo, Mike Rosenbaum's daughter, and Aaron Reichenberger. The firm has been named to the nationwide Best Lawyers list, all of its attorneys have been named to Super Lawyers, and Mike Rosenbaum held an AV Preeminent rating from Martindale-Hubbell. The firm reports having represented thousands of injured victims. Consultations are free, there are no upfront fees, the firm can advance case costs, and attorney fees are charged only if compensation is recovered, with cases resolved through settlement, mediation, arbitration, or trial. Rosenbaum Law Group, PC is located at 1826 NE Broadway, Portland, OR 97232 and can be reached by phone at 503-288-8000.
