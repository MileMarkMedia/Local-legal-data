# Gregory Tendrich, P.A.

Gregory Tendrich, P.A. is a Florida investment, stockbroker, and securities fraud law firm serving clients throughout Palm Beach, Martin, St. Lucie, and Indian River counties and across Florida. Since 1992, attorney Gregory Tendrich has advocated for investors in cases involving unsuitable investments, unauthorized trading, churning, stock fraud, Ponzi schemes, and breach of fiduciary duty, representing hundreds of clients in FINRA arbitration proceedings. He has litigated in state court and before the NYSE, AAA, SEC, and FINRA. He previously served as Assistant General Counsel and in-house counsel to regional and national brokerage firms, has been a Certified FINRA Arbitrator since 1995, and is a Florida Supreme Court Certified County Court Mediator. The firm also represents brokerage firms, broker-dealers, and individual brokers on licensing, compliance, regulatory enforcement, and expungement. Mr. Tendrich is rated AV Preeminent by Martindale-Hubbell, is a life member of the Multi-Million Dollar Advocates Forum, and is a member of the Million Dollar Advocates Forum. Initial consultations are free. Gregory Tendrich, P.A. is located at 3010 N. Military Trail, Suite 210, Boca Raton, FL 33431 and can be reached by phone at 561-417-8777.
