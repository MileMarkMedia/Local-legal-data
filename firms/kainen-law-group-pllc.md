# Kainen Law Group, PLLC

Kainen Law Group, PLLC is a Las Vegas family law firm handling divorce and related matters for clients in Las Vegas, Summerlin, Henderson, North Las Vegas, and Boulder City, as well as clients from across the country in high-asset, complex divorces. Senior and managing partner Edward L. Kainen is a Nevada Board Certified Specialist in Family Law, is Board Certified in Family Trial Law by the National Board of Trial Advocacy, and is an elected Fellow of both the American Academy of Matrimonial Lawyers and the International Academy of Family Lawyers. He leads the firm with partners Andrew L. Kynaston, who has practiced exclusively in family law since 2002, Racheal H. Mastel, and Dara L. Marias. The firm handles alimony, asset division and business valuation, military divorce, child custody and support, relocation, paternity, prenuptial and post-marital agreements, guardianship, grandparent visitation, modifications, appeals, and joint mediation. Edward Kainen has been named a Nevada Super Lawyer annually since 2007 and included in Best Lawyers in America for over 10 continuous years, and the firm holds an AV Preeminent rating from Martindale-Hubbell. Clients have telephone, email, and video access to their attorneys. Kainen Law Group, PLLC is located at 3303 Novat Street, Suite 200, Las Vegas, NV 89129 and can be reached by phone at 702-823-4900.
