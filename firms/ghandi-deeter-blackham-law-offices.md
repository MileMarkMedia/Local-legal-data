# Ghandi Deeter Blackham Law Offices

Ghandi Deeter Blackham Law Offices is a Las Vegas boutique firm focused on divorce and family law, along with guardianship, estate planning, and probate, serving Las Vegas, Henderson, and Clark County. Laura Deeter, who joined the firm in 2013, handles estate planning, guardianship, trusts, probate, and family law. Brian E. Blackham is a certified family law specialist, a credential held by less than 0.5 percent of Nevada attorneys, and focuses on complex custody disputes and business issues in divorce. Partner Meghan Lonardo holds an LLM in Taxation and concentrates on high-asset, high-conflict divorce and custody cases, partner Leah M. Blakesley has practiced exclusively in family law for over a decade, and litigator Shara L. Larson brings over twenty years of experience. The firm handles contested and uncontested divorce, child custody, child support, spousal support, property division, military divorce, prenuptial agreements, and adoption. Recognition for the firm includes Super Lawyers, Avvo 10.0 ratings, and Desert Top Lawyers. The firm keeps a deliberate caseload, offers mediation and collaborative options, and provides video consultations. Ghandi Deeter Blackham Law Offices is located at 725 S 8th St, Suite 100, Las Vegas, NV 89101 and can be reached by phone at 702-878-1115.
