# Law Offices of Larry E. Bray, P.A.

The Law Offices of Larry E. Bray, P.A. is a West Palm Beach probate, estate planning, real estate and business law firm serving individuals, families and businesses throughout Palm Beach County and Florida. Founder Larry E. Bray earned his J.D. with honors from Nova Southeastern University Law Center and has been admitted to practice in Florida and New York since 1981, bringing more than 30 years of experience. Before entering private practice, he trained in transactional work at RCA Records in New York. The firm handles wills, trusts, probate and trust administration, asset protection, residential and commercial real estate, title insurance, foreclosure defense and loan modifications, business formation, succession planning and mergers and acquisitions, and business and real estate litigation, and it runs a consumer and commercial collections department. In addition to West Palm Beach, the firm has offices in Boynton Beach, Lake Worth and Boca Raton. Bray was recognized as a Top Rated Lawyer in 2020 and holds Avvo ratings in probate, real estate and business law. The firm builds plans tailored to each client and keeps clients informed at every stage. The Law Offices of Larry E. Bray, P.A. is located at 801 Northpoint Parkway, West Palm Beach, FL 33407 and can be reached by phone at 561-571-8970.
