# Young, Marr, Mallis & Associates

Young, Marr, Mallis & Associates is a Pennsylvania and New Jersey law firm focused on bankruptcy, criminal defense and disability law. Founding partner Paul H. Young leads the firm with senior partners Gail Marr, who began her legal career at the Bucks County District Attorney's Office, and Paul Mallis, a former prosecutor who handled hundreds of criminal cases ranging from DUI to homicide. With more than 40 years of experience, the firm serves Philadelphia, Bucks, Montgomery, Delaware, Chester, Lehigh, Northampton and Dauphin counties in Pennsylvania and Burlington, Mercer and Middlesex counties in New Jersey. Its attorneys have handled more than 15,000 Chapter 7 and Chapter 13 bankruptcy cases, defend clients facing charges from DUI and drug crimes to homicide, and pursue Social Security Disability and long-term disability benefits. The firm has earned an Avvo Superb rating, Avvo Clients' Choice and the American Institute of Bankruptcy Attorneys 10 Best Client Satisfaction Award, and its attorneys have been featured in The New York Times, USA Today, The Philadelphia Inquirer, BBC News and ABC News. Young, Marr, Mallis & Associates is located at 3554 Hulmeville Ave, Suite 102, Bensalem, PA 19020 and can be reached by phone at 215-639-5297.
