# Baginski Brandt & Brandt

Baginski Brandt & Brandt is a Port St. Lucie family law and divorce firm that also handles criminal defense, personal injury, and estate planning matters throughout St. Lucie and Martin counties, with appointment offices in Stuart and Fort Pierce. Partner Arthur B. Brandt, a University of Miami School of Law graduate and Florida Bar member since 1997, has owned his own firm since 2000, and partner Julia G. Baginski Brandt, who led a solo firm for ten years, joined him in 2016. Together they bring more than 30 years of legal experience to divorce, high-net-worth and international divorce, alimony, child custody and time-sharing, child support, adoption, and paternity, as well as DUI, drug, felony, and misdemeanor defense and car accident, slip and fall, and wrongful death claims. Arthur Brandt holds an AV Preeminent rating from Martindale-Hubbell, and the firm has been recognized among the National Trial Lawyers Top 100. Reported criminal results include not guilty verdicts on DUI and cocaine possession charges. The attorneys aim for practical resolutions while remaining ready to litigate, offer services in Spanish, and take injury cases on contingency. Baginski Brandt & Brandt is located at 8483 S US Highway 1, Port St. Lucie, FL 34952 and can be reached by phone at 772-466-0707.
