# Spencer Morgan Law

Spencer Morgan Law is a Miami personal injury firm representing accident victims throughout Miami-Dade County and Florida, including Pinecrest, Kendall, and Coral Gables. Managing attorney Spencer G. Morgan was admitted to The Florida Bar in 1996 after graduating from the University of Miami School of Law. He began his career defending personal injury claims for insurance companies and corporations, and since 2001 he has represented injured individuals exclusively. The firm handles car, truck, motorcycle, and rideshare accidents, slip, trip and fall and premises liability claims, workplace and construction accidents, negligent security, defective products, maritime injuries, and wrongful death. Results include a $1,000,000 semi-truck crash recovery, a $1,000,000 auto accident settlement, an $850,000 slip and fall settlement, and an $800,000 maritime accident recovery. Consultations are free and available at any hour, clients pay no fees or costs unless there is a recovery, and the firm can meet clients at home, in the hospital, or at the accident scene. Services are offered in English and Spanish. Spencer Morgan Law is located at 9155 S Dadeland Blvd, Suite 1104, Miami, FL 33156 and can be reached by phone at 305-423-3800.
