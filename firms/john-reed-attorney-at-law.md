# John Reed, Attorney at Law

John Reed, Attorney at Law is a criminal defense firm serving League City and the Galveston Bay area, including Clear Lake City, Texas City, Dickinson, Friendswood, La Marque, Galveston, Kemah, Seabrook, Houston, and Harris and Galveston Counties. Attorney John Reed, a graduate of the University of Texas School of Law, brings more than 35 years of experience in Texas criminal law, including four years as an Assistant District Attorney, and has been in private practice since 1996. He handles DWI and ALR hearings, drug crimes, assault, domestic violence, homicide, robbery, sex offenses, theft, weapons charges, white collar crime, probation violations, expunctions, and non-disclosure orders. Recent results include jury acquittals in DWI, aggravated assault with a deadly weapon, and continuous sexual abuse of a child cases, along with dismissals of murder, sexual assault of a child, and aggravated robbery charges. He is a member of the Texas Criminal Defense Lawyers Association and the Galveston County Criminal Defense Lawyers Association. Clients speak directly with John Reed, receive his cell phone number, and can arrange free consultations, including evenings and weekends. John Reed, Attorney at Law is located at 1500 Marina Bay Dr, Bldg 133, Clear Lake Shores, TX 77565 and can be reached by phone at 281-928-8228.
