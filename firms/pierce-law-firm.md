# Pierce Law Firm

Pierce Law Firm is a Houston, Texas law firm focused exclusively on legal malpractice, representing clients harmed by a former attorney's negligence, breach of fiduciary duty, fraud, or mishandling of a case. Attorney Nicholas Pierce has represented clients in hundreds of legal malpractice lawsuits against their lawyers and is licensed in all Texas courts and the Southern District of Texas. The firm pursues claims involving missed deadlines, failure to investigate, conflicts of interest, lack of communication, improper withdrawal, overbilling, settlements made without client consent, and mismanaged personal injury cases, as well as barratry and improper solicitation. The firm has received the Avvo Clients' Choice Award, and Nicholas Pierce is a member of the Houston Bar Association and the American Bar Association. Each case begins with a damages analysis and is prepared as though it may go to trial. Cases are handled on a contingency fee basis, clients can reach Nicholas Pierce directly by phone, text, or email, and Spanish is spoken. The firm represents clients throughout Houston, Harris County, and Texas, including Austin, Dallas, and San Antonio. Pierce Law Firm is located at 24 Greenway Plz, Suite 500A, Houston, TX 77046 and can be reached by phone at 832-866-2861.
