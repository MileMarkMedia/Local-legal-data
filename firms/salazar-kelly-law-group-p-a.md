# Salazar & Kelly Law Group, P.A.

Salazar & Kelly Law Group, P.A. is a Kissimmee based firm handling criminal defense, personal injury, and bankruptcy matters for clients across Orlando and Central Florida. It was founded in 2003 by Juan A. Salazar and James B. Kelly, III, former Florida prosecutors who built their practice through trial experience, and the firm has litigated thousands of cases in the Florida courts. With more than 20 years of legal work, its attorneys handle DUI, drug crimes, sex offenses, assault, theft, weapons charges, homicide, federal crimes, probation violations, and expungements, as well as car and truck accidents, motorcycle accidents, wrongful death, premises liability, medical malpractice, and property damage claims. Personal injury cases are taken on a contingency basis so clients pay no fees until compensation is received, and consultations are free. The firm serves Spanish speaking clients and communities including Kissimmee, St. Cloud, Poinciana, Haines City, Buenaventura Lakes, Celebration, and Alafaya, with additional offices in Downtown Orlando and Altamonte Springs. Salazar & Kelly Law Group, P.A. is located at 603 Verona Street, Kissimmee, FL 34741 and can be reached by phone at 407-483-0500.
