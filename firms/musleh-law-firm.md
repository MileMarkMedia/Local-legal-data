# Musleh Law Firm

Musleh Law Firm is a personal injury firm serving Ocala and North Central Florida, led by attorneys Victor J. Musleh Jr. and Kelly H. Musleh. The Musleh family has represented injured people in Ocala since 1949, and Victor Musleh, a third generation Ocala resident, has been the firm's founder and owner since 1999. Both attorneys earned their law degrees from Stetson University College of Law. The firm handles car, truck, and motorcycle accidents, workers' compensation, premises liability, medical malpractice, defective products, insurance disputes, and wrongful death claims. It has obtained over $115 million in settlements for clients, with reported results including $2.4 million and $1.4 million motor vehicle accident recoveries and a $1.05 million medical negligence and work accident result. Victor Musleh has represented more than 2,500 injured people, is a member of the Million Dollar Advocates Forum, and was selected for Florida Trend's Legal Elite. Clients work directly with the attorneys from start to finish, the firm advances litigation costs, and there is no fee unless the firm recovers compensation. Musleh Law Firm is located at 907 SE 3rd Avenue, Ocala, FL 34471 and can be reached by phone at 352-732-0600.
