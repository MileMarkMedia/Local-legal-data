# Carroll Law Firm LLC

Carroll Law Firm LLC is an Atlanta law firm focused on medical malpractice, personal injury, and business litigation for clients throughout Atlanta and the surrounding Georgia area. Led by trial attorney Stacey Carroll, the firm draws on more than 15 years of legal practice and hundreds of litigated civil lawsuits for individuals and businesses, including cases involving millions of dollars in damages. Stacey Carroll has been recognized by Georgia Super Lawyers. The firm handles surgical errors, birth injuries, misdiagnosis, medication and hospital errors, auto and truck accidents, defective products, unsafe premises, wrongful death, and business disputes such as partner and shareholder conflicts, breach of contract, and unfair competition. Results include a $2 million malpractice verdict against a cardiologist who failed to properly monitor a pacemaker, a $1.2 million insurance bad faith verdict, and a $1 million slip and fall recovery that secured the policy limits. The firm works with a network of medical experts, prepares every case as though it is going to court, and offers free consultations with evening and weekend meetings by appointment. Carroll Law Firm LLC is located at 4200 Northside Pkwy NW, Suite 300, Atlanta, GA 30327 and can be reached by phone at 404-816-4555.
