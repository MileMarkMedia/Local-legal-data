# Neuman Law, PA

Neuman Law, PA is a boutique Boca Raton law firm focused on construction litigation, real estate litigation, construction project administration, community association matters, and business litigation. The firm represents property owners, developers, general contractors, subcontractors, design professionals, and specialty trades throughout Florida, including Palm Beach, Broward, Miami-Dade, Martin, Monroe, and Hillsborough Counties. Managing Partner Eric J. Neuman, a Fordham University School of Law graduate admitted in Florida and New York, has been recognized by Super Lawyers, including as a Rising Star from 2010 to 2016, and has been quoted in the Palm Beach Post, Sun-Sentinel, and Daily Business Review. He is joined by Partner Devin T. Moore. The firm handles construction defects, mechanic's liens, payment and contract disputes, delay claims, change orders, landlord and tenant disputes, evictions, quiet title actions, partnership disputes, and condominium and homeowners' association issues. Operating as a concierge practice, the firm gives clients direct access to an attorney and gets involved early through contract review and project administration to help avoid disputes. Neuman Law, PA is located at 1001 W Yamato Rd, Suite 401, Boca Raton, FL 33431 and can be reached by phone at 561-571-4500.
