# Sotolongo Law

Sotolongo Law is a Miami, Florida law firm focused on medical malpractice and serious personal injury cases. The firm is led by Pedro P. Sotolongo, who brings more than 20 years of medical malpractice experience to representing people seriously injured by negligent healthcare providers. Before representing injured patients, Sotolongo spent five years defending medical malpractice claims, giving him insight into how healthcare providers, hospitals, insurers, and defense attorneys approach these cases. The firm handles medical malpractice matters including surgical errors, birth injuries, misdiagnosis, nursing and hospital errors, and anesthesia errors, as well as car, truck, and boating accidents, premises liability cases such as slip and fall and negligent security, nursing home abuse and neglect, catastrophic injuries, and wrongful death claims. Sotolongo Law serves clients in Miami Beach, Miami-Dade, Palm Beach, Boca Raton, Fort Lauderdale, and Orlando. The firm has obtained results including a $10,000,000 car accident settlement, a $6,000,000 trip and fall settlement, and a $1,000,000 maritime claim settlement. Services are offered in English and Spanish. The firm offers a free consultation and emphasizes a personal approach to each case while being prepared to take on large hospital systems and insurance companies. Sotolongo Law is located at 1000 5th St., Suite #402, Miami Beach, FL 33139 and can be reached by phone at 305-415-0073.
