# Draper Law Office

Draper Law Office is a Kissimmee law firm focused on personal injury and family law, serving clients throughout Osceola County and greater Orlando from offices in Kissimmee and Orlando. Managing partner Charles B. Draper founded the firm in 1984 and has represented injured clients for more than 40 years. He previously practiced with Magavern and Magavern in Buffalo, New York before moving to Florida. Attorney Michelle Incandela began her career in 1997 at a New York insurance defense firm before focusing on personal injury and wrongful death in Florida, and attorney Linda Gruszynski, licensed in Florida and Georgia, concentrates on family law. The firm handles car, truck, motorcycle, bicycle and pedestrian accidents, drunk and distracted driving crashes, uninsured driver claims, insurance disputes, dog bites, slip and fall, and wrongful death, along with divorce, custody and time-sharing, child support, alimony, paternity, equitable distribution and domestic violence injunctions. Personal injury consultations are free and cases are handled on a contingency fee basis, and several staff members speak Spanish. Draper Law Office is located at 705 W. Emmett Street, Kissimmee, FL 34741 and can be reached by phone at 407-846-0075.
