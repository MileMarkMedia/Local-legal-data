# McBride Legal Group, P.A.

McBride Legal Group, P.A. is a Stuart, Florida family law firm built for contested, high-conflict and high-asset divorce and family law cases on the Treasure Coast, serving Martin, St. Lucie, Indian River, Brevard and Okeechobee counties. Managing Attorney Luisa McBride is a former prosecutor with more than 15 years of experience in Florida family law, and she prepares every case as though it will go to trial. She founded the firm with her husband, Chief Executive Officer Patrick McBride, a former Martin County Fire Rescue captain who oversees the client experience. The firm handles divorce, property division, alimony, child support, child custody and timesharing, military divorce, fathers' rights and paternity, relocation, modifications and enforcement of final judgments, prenuptial and postnuptial agreements, and domestic violence injunctions. Results include eliminating a non-modifiable alimony award, a directed verdict that defeated a modification action, and a full timesharing award for a father that was upheld on appeal. McBride Legal Group, P.A. is located at 34 SE Osceola St, Stuart, FL 34994 and can be reached by phone at 772-236-0234.
