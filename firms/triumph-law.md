# Triumph Law

Triumph Law is a boutique corporate law firm in the Washington, D.C. area serving founders, high-growth companies and investors in Washington, D.C., Northern Virginia and Maryland, with clients and transactions nationwide. The firm was founded by Max Moyer, who has more than a decade of corporate law experience, previously practiced at Cooley LLP and Holland & Knight LLP, and has served as a startup executive. Its attorneys bring backgrounds from national law firms, in-house legal departments and operating businesses. Triumph Law handles startup formation, founder agreements, equity allocation and outside general counsel services, seed, venture capital, strategic and debt financings for companies and investors, and mergers and acquisitions ranging from small roll-up acquisitions and acquihires to billion-dollar mergers. The firm also advises on technology transactions, commercial contracts, data privacy, AI, patents, trademarks and intellectual property strategy, and it provides supplemental support to in-house legal teams on specific projects. Triumph Law is located at 145 Church St NW, Suite 301, Vienna, VA 22180 and can be reached by phone at 301-205-5895.
