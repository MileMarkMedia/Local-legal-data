# Law Offices of Gary E. Gerson

The Law Offices of Gary E. Gerson is a Pittsburgh criminal defense firm serving clients throughout Allegheny County and Western Pennsylvania. Attorney Gary E. Gerson, a graduate of the University of Pittsburgh and Duquesne University School of Law, began his career at the Pittsburgh firm Rosenberg-Kirshner before opening his own practice in 2001, and he brings more than 30 years of legal experience to state and federal cases. The firm defends clients facing drug and drug trafficking charges, DUI, sex offenses and internet crimes, violent crimes, theft and white collar crime, weapons charges, and juvenile matters, along with expungements and pardons. Reported results include all charges withdrawn after suppression was granted in a Mercer County case involving 100 pounds of marijuana, not guilty verdicts on all counts in a Clarion County case involving 208 counts of sex offenses, and not guilty verdicts in an Allegheny County attempted homicide case and a Washington County homicide by vehicle case. Gerson holds an Avvo Rating of 10.0 Superb and received 2016 Clients' Choice Awards for criminal defense, federal crime and DUI. The Law Offices of Gary E. Gerson is located at 304 Ross Street, Suite 600, Pittsburgh, PA 15219 and can be reached by phone at 412-219-6875.
