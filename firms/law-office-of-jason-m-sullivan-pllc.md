# Law Office of Jason M. Sullivan, PLLC

The Law Office of Jason M. Sullivan, PLLC provides attorney-led immigration representation to individuals, families, professionals, and employers throughout New Hampshire, with a Coral Gables office serving select clients in Miami and South Florida. Attorney Jason M. Sullivan has been licensed in New Hampshire since 2000 and has focused exclusively on U.S. immigration law for more than two decades, representing clients from more than 75 countries before USCIS, the Department of Labor, the Board of Immigration Appeals, and federal courts. His practice covers marriage-based green cards, K-1 fiance visas, adjustment of status, consular processing, removal of conditions, naturalization, PERM labor certification, EB-2 National Interest Waivers, and selected EB-1 cases, along with responses to Requests for Evidence, Notices of Intent to Deny, and prior denials. He has served as an expert witness in court. Clients work directly with Attorney Sullivan rather than through paralegals or case managers, with fixed legal fees, no hourly billing, and secure video consultations. Spanish is spoken, and a discount is offered to active military and veterans. The Law Office of Jason M. Sullivan, PLLC is located at 1 New Hampshire Ave, Suite 125, Portsmouth, NH 03801 and can be reached by phone at 603-433-1325.
