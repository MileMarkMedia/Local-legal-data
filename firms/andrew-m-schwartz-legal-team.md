# Andrew M. Schwartz Legal Team

The Andrew M. Schwartz Legal Team is a Boca Raton business litigation firm founded in 1994, serving clients throughout Palm Beach County and Broward County. Founder Andrew M. Schwartz has 37 years of litigation experience and has practiced in South Florida since his admission to The Florida Bar in 1989. He is joined by Senior Counsel Sundeep Nath, a Northwestern-trained litigator focused on business and real estate cases. The firm concentrates on business divorce, partnership and LLC member disputes, conflicts between family members in business, complex contract litigation, and disputes over money owed or missing funds. Its real estate practice covers partition actions and title disputes for co-owners who need to separate from a property. The firm also obtains temporary and permanent injunctions and provides outside general counsel services. In May 2024, after a five-day bench trial, the firm obtained a final judgment in Broward County granting specific performance of a commercial real estate contract. The firm regularly represents clients from New York, New Jersey, and Connecticut with business or real estate interests in South Florida and accepts referrals from other attorneys. The Andrew M. Schwartz Legal Team is located at 4755 Technology Way #103, Boca Raton, FL 33431 and can be reached by phone at 561-347-6767.
