# Law Office of Wilfredo O. Allen

The Law Office of Wilfredo O. Allen is a Miami immigration law firm led by founding attorney Wilfredo O. Allen, serving Miami's immigrant community with a particular focus on the city's Cuban and Venezuelan populations, along with clients nationwide and worldwide. For more than three decades, the firm has handled asylum, refugee status, Temporary Protected Status, family-based petitions, employment-based immigration from EB-1 through EB-5, business immigration including H-1B and PERM matters, immigrant and nonimmigrant visas, adjustment of status, green cards, citizenship and naturalization, criminal immigration issues, bond hearings, and removal and deportation defense. Asylum is a central focus of the practice. All six of the firm's attorneys are bilingual in Spanish and English, and the firm maintains an additional office in Louisville, Kentucky. The firm builds a strategic plan for each case and emphasizes personalized, culturally sensitive communication, with free consultations available by phone or through online scheduling. The Law Office of Wilfredo O. Allen is located at 2250 SW 3rd Ave, Suite 301, Miami, FL 33129 and can be reached by phone at 305-854-5955.
