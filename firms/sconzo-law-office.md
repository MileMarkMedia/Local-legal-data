# Sconzo Law Office

Sconzo Law Office is a West Palm Beach area law firm focused on employment law and disability rights, representing workers throughout Florida. Attorney Gregory S. Sconzo founded the firm in 2017 after five years at Kaplan & Sconzo, P.A., where he handled civil litigation, discrimination, and civil rights cases, and he practices alongside Attorney Andrea Sconzo, with the team bringing decades of combined experience. Gregory Sconzo graduated magna cum laude from the University of Miami School of Law, has tried numerous cases to verdict, has argued appeals before the Fourth District Court of Appeal and the Eleventh Circuit, and has been recognized as a Super Lawyers Rising Star. The firm handles wrongful termination, age, disability, gender, pregnancy, race, national origin, and religious discrimination, harassment and sexual harassment, wage theft, FMLA violations, whistleblower claims, and employment contract and non-compete disputes. Most cases are handled on a contingency fee basis, and consultations are available in person, by phone, or by video. Sconzo Law Office is located at 300 Avenue of the Champions, Suite 260, Palm Beach Gardens, FL 33418 and can be reached by phone at 561-279-6114.
