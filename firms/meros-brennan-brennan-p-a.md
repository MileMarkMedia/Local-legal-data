# Meros, Brennan & Brennan, P.A.

Meros, Brennan & Brennan, P.A. is a St. Petersburg family and divorce law firm that has served clients in St. Petersburg and Pinellas County since 1954. Partners Peter N. Meros, Kevin D. Brennan and Christin C. Brennan bring more than 89 years of combined legal experience. Peter Meros has been Board Certified in Civil Trial Law since 1983 and belongs to the Thirty Year Club for 30 years of continuous board certification, Kevin Brennan is Board Certified in Marital and Family Law, and Christin Brennan has practiced exclusively in marital and family law since 2009. The firm handles divorce, high net worth divorce and complex litigation, alimony, child support, child custody and time-sharing, equitable distribution, paternity, domestic violence, modifications, relocations and enforcement, prenuptial and postnuptial agreements, mediation, collaborative divorce and family law appeals, along with commercial litigation, real estate, wills, trusts and probate, and personal injury. The firm holds an AV Preeminent rating from Martindale-Hubbell and Super Lawyers recognition, and both Brennans were named Young Lawyer of the Year by the Canakaris Family Law Inn of Court. Meros, Brennan & Brennan, P.A. is located at 415 1st Ave N, St. Petersburg, FL 33701 and can be reached by phone at 727-822-4929.
