# Whitfield Crosby & Flynn

Whitfield Crosby & Flynn is a personal injury and wrongful death trial firm serving Madisonville, Hopkins County and Western Kentucky, with additional offices in Marion, Illinois, Chattanooga, Tennessee and Indianapolis, Indiana. Founding attorney John Whitfield leads the trial team from Madisonville, bringing more than 40 years of practice and certification as a National Board of Trial Advocacy Trial Specialist. He practices alongside Tim Crosby in Chattanooga and Chuck Flynn in Indianapolis, a member of the Multi-Million Dollar Advocates Forum recognized by the Tennessee Supreme Court as an Attorney for Justice. With more than 90 years of combined experience, the attorneys handle car, truck and motorcycle accidents, medical malpractice, nursing home abuse, products liability, coal mine injuries and select criminal defense matters. Reported results include a $165 million class action settlement over defective heart valves, a $5.78 million jury verdict for a law enforcement officer and a $4.8 million settlement for the families of five coal miners. Every case is prepared for trial from the start, and the firm has been recognized by Best Lawyers: Ones to Watch. Whitfield Crosby & Flynn is located at 19 N Main St, Madisonville, KY 42431 and can be reached by phone at 270-821-0656.
