# Scriven Law, P.A.

Scriven Law, P.A. is a Tampa criminal defense firm founded in 2007 by Bryant A. Scriven, who has practiced law in the Tampa Bay area for more than 17 years. He began his career as a prosecutor with the Hillsborough County State Attorney's Office, where he served as lead trial counsel on thousands of criminal cases. He has tried over 100 cases to verdict and has obtained not guilty verdicts in both state and federal courts. The firm handles DUI, drug crimes, domestic violence, sex crimes, violent crimes, theft, weapons offenses, probation violations, white collar and federal fraud charges, and traffic offenses, along with personal injury claims, throughout Hillsborough County, Pinellas County, and Florida. Results include no charges filed in a counterfeit bills and forgery case carrying 10 years of exposure, and probation with the minimum mandatory waived in a cocaine delivery near a school case carrying 30 years. Bryant holds an AV Preeminent rating from Martindale-Hubbell and has been named to The National Trial Lawyers Top 100 Criminal Lawyers. Free consultations are available 24 hours a day, with evening and weekend meetings. Scriven Law, P.A. is located at 402 East 7th Avenue, Tampa, FL 33602 and can be reached by phone at 813-226-8522.
