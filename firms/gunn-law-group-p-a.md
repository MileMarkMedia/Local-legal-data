# Gunn Law Group, P.A.

Gunn Law Group, P.A. is a Tampa law firm serving Florida since 2005, focused on medical malpractice, catastrophic personal injury, and insurance bad faith claims. The firm is led by Lee D. Gunn IV, who has practiced law since 1983, has been a Florida Bar Board Certified Civil Trial Lawyer since 1990, and spent 17 years defending hospitals, corporations, and insurance carriers before turning to plaintiff representation in 2000. Attorneys Ryan A. Lopez, L. Delton Gunn V, and Christopher A. Cazin are also part of the team, which has more than 75 years of combined experience. The firm handles surgical errors, birth injuries, misdiagnosis, medication errors, wrongful death, and insurance coverage and bad faith disputes. Results include a $13.8 million confidential settlement, $10.5 million in an improper surgery case, and $10 million in a case of improper brain stenting with stroke. Recognition includes Super Lawyers, Best Lawyers, Best Law Firms, an AV Preeminent rating from Martindale-Hubbell, and The National Trial Lawyers Top 100. Each client works with a specific attorney as the main point of contact, and cases are handled on a contingency fee basis. Gunn Law Group, P.A. is located at 401 East Jackson St, Suite 3600, Tampa, FL 33602 and can be reached by phone at 813-228-7070.
