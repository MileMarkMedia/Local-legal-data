# Berman \| Sobin \| Gross LLP

Berman \| Sobin \| Gross LLP is a Maryland workers' compensation and personal injury firm founded in February 1991 in Gaithersburg by Ken Berman, Cliff Sobin and Alan Gross, who together bring more than a century of combined experience. Over 35 years the firm has grown from three attorneys in one office to more than 20 attorneys, with offices in Gaithersburg, Lutherville, Baltimore, Frederick, Hagerstown, Largo and Cumberland, serving injured workers throughout Maryland and Washington, D.C. The firm concentrates on workers' compensation, along with workplace injury, personal injury, medical malpractice and wrongful death, and many of its clients are union members and their families. Cliff Sobin is the author of the two-volume treatise Maryland Workers' Compensation. The firm has handled hundreds of workers' compensation jury trials and appeals and has been recognized by Best Lawyers, Best Law Firms, AV Preeminent, the Million Dollar Advocates Forum and The National Trial Lawyers. Attorneys and staff speak Spanish, and workers' compensation cases are handled on a contingency basis. Berman \| Sobin \| Gross LLP is located at 481 N Frederick Ave, Suite 300, Gaithersburg, MD 20877 and can be reached by phone at 301-670-7030.
