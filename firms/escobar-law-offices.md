# Escobar Law Offices

Escobar Law Offices is a Northern Virginia immigration law firm led by attorney Janet A. Escobar, serving clients in Annandale, Alexandria, Arlington and throughout Virginia, as well as the Washington DC area. The firm brings nearly a decade of experience to immigration matters, including family-based and employment-based immigration, green cards, visas, T-visas and U-visas, and defensive immigration and deportation defense. Escobar Law Offices also provides criminal defense for misdemeanor and felony charges, including DUI, drug crimes, domestic violence, federal crimes and white collar offenses such as bank, healthcare, mortgage and securities fraud. Janet Escobar also serves as a mediator and arbitrator for civil and business disputes. The firm has been recognized by 10 Best. Its client centered approach uses different resolution methods to reach practical outcomes, and it guides individuals, families and professionals through each stage of the immigration process. Consultations are confidential and available in person or virtually. Escobar Law Offices is located at 7010 Little River Turnpike, Suite 240, Annandale, VA 22003 and can be reached by phone at 866-846-7730.
