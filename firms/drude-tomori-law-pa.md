# Drude Tomori Law, PA

Drude Tomori Law, PA is an estate planning, probate and elder law firm serving clients in St. Petersburg and throughout the Greater Tampa Bay Area, including Bradenton, Lakewood Ranch and Sarasota. The firm was founded by Rachel Drude-Tomori, Esq., LL.M., who has more than 15 years of legal experience. She graduated summa cum laude from Loyola University New Orleans and earned her J.D. with honors and an LL.M. in Estate Planning from the University of Miami School of Law. The firm handles wills, trusts, advance directives, supplemental needs trusts, asset protection, Medicaid and long-term care planning, probate administration, guardianships, probate and trust litigation, business succession planning, and Deferred Sales Trusts. Rachel has been recognized by Super Lawyers since 2017 and has chaired the Probate and Guardianship Section of the St. Petersburg Bar Association. The firm focuses on helping individuals and families protect their assets and plan for the future through tailored estate planning and elder law strategies. Drude Tomori Law, PA is located at 2051 1st Avenue North, St. Petersburg, FL 33713 and can be reached by phone at 727-300-8900.
