# Fox, Farley, Willis & Burnette

Fox, Farley, Willis & Burnette is a Tennessee personal injury law firm with offices in Clinton, Knoxville, Sevierville, LaFollette, and Maryville, serving clients across East Tennessee including Gatlinburg and Pigeon Forge. The firm's named attorneys are Bruce D. Fox, Michael S. Farley, John Willis, and Bradley C. Burnette. Co-founder Bruce D. Fox has more than 45 years of experience and received the Tennessee Trial Lawyers Association Lifetime Achievement Award in 2025 and its Paladin Award in 2011. For over thirty years, the firm has represented people hurt in car, truck, and motorcycle accidents, along with wrongful death, premises liability, dangerous drug, and product liability claims. It also handles family law and criminal defense matters. Reported results include a $3.4 million auto case, a $2.3 million jury verdict for a Knox County motorcyclist who lost his leg, and a $1.6 million trucking settlement. Recognition includes Super Lawyers, an AV Preeminent rating from Martindale-Hubbell, and America's Top 100 High Stakes Litigators. Cases are handled on a contingency fee basis, attorneys will visit clients at home or in the hospital, and Spanish is spoken. Fox, Farley, Willis & Burnette is located at 310 North Main St, Clinton, TN 37716 and can be reached by phone at 865-457-6440.
