# The Probate Guy

The Probate Guy is the Buena Park, California practice of attorney Robert L. Cohen, who focuses on probate, wills and trusts throughout Southern California, including Los Angeles, Orange, Riverside and San Bernardino counties and cities such as Anaheim, Fullerton, Cerritos, Downey and Whittier. Robert L. Cohen has more than 30 years of probate experience and handles probate administration, probate litigation including will and trust challenges, estate administration, and estate planning with wills, trusts, powers of attorney and living wills, along with related real estate matters such as probate home sales and reverse mortgage foreclosures. He advances all probate fees, costs and court filing expenses so clients pay nothing out of pocket, with repayment from the inheritance at the end of the case, and some probate litigation may be handled on contingency. He personally handles the work on each case rather than relying on paralegals, appears in court for clients so they rarely need to attend, and explains the process in plain English. Clients can work with him entirely by phone, email and mail without visiting the office, and the initial consultation is free. The Probate Guy is located at 8081 Orangethorpe Avenue, Buena Park, CA 90621 and can be reached by phone at 714-522-8880.
