# Bruce S. Rosenwater & Associates, P.A.

Bruce S. Rosenwater & Associates, P.A. is a West Palm Beach law firm focused on family law and divorce, along with bankruptcy, foreclosure, and real estate matters. Led by attorney Bruce S. Rosenwater, the firm has served Palm Beach County residents for more than 30 years, guiding clients through divorce, alimony, child custody, child support, paternity, and property division cases with a combination of sensitivity and aggressive advocacy. The firm also helps individuals facing financial hardship through Chapter 7, Chapter 11, and Chapter 13 bankruptcy, debt settlement, and foreclosure alternatives. Bruce Rosenwater holds an AV Preeminent rating from Martindale-Hubbell and is recognized as an Avvo 10.0 Top Attorney. The firm offers services in Spanish and works to help clients resolve difficult legal challenges efficiently. Bruce S. Rosenwater & Associates, P.A. is located at 1601 Forum Place, Suite 602, West Palm Beach, FL 33401 and can be reached by phone at 561-688-0991.
