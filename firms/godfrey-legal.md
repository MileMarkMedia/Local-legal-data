# Godfrey Legal

Godfrey Legal is an Orlando business law firm led by B.F. "Biff" Godfrey, who has more than 40 years of experience counseling companies on business, corporate, and commercial real estate matters throughout Orlando and Central Florida. Admitted to the Texas Bar in 1974 and the Florida Bar in 1994, he served as in-house counsel for Zale Corporation, TGI Fridays Inc., and Darden Restaurants, Inc., handling leasing, real estate, domestic and international franchising, licensing, trademarks, and purchasing. The firm's practice covers business start-ups, contracts, asset and stock purchases, mergers and acquisitions, franchising, non-compete agreements, commercial real estate, retail and commercial leasing, easements and zoning, liquor licensing, and trademarks. He lectures across Florida on easement law and real estate development and has been a member of the American Bar Association Forum on Franchising since 1992. Clients work directly with him, and he focuses on efficient, cost-effective resolutions. Godfrey Legal is located at 1000 Legion Place, 10th Floor, Orlando, FL 32801 and can be reached by phone at 407-890-0023.
