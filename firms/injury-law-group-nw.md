# Injury Law Group NW

Injury Law Group NW is a personal injury firm based in Issaquah serving King County, the greater Seattle area, and Eastside communities including Bellevue, Sammamish, Redmond, and Kirkland. Founding partner Troy Hunter spent 16 years in insurance defense, mostly as a partner at Floyd, Pflueger & Ringer, giving the firm insight into how insurers evaluate claims, and has argued cases at every level in Washington, from district courts to the State Supreme Court. Attorney David D. Mordekhov spent two decades handling wrongful death, catastrophic injury, and product liability cases and serves on the faculty of the National Institute for Trial Advocacy. The firm handles car, truck, motorcycle, and bus accidents, boat and construction accidents, medical malpractice, retail liability, slip and fall injuries, traumatic brain injury, and wrongful death, and its results include more than $1 million for parents whose newborn died after a hospital failed to adequately test for Group B Strep. Troy Hunter and David Mordekhov hold AV Preeminent ratings from Martindale-Hubbell, and David Mordekhov has been selected to Super Lawyers. The firm serves Russian, Ukrainian, and Spanish-speaking clients and works on a contingency fee basis. Injury Law Group NW is located at 1180 NW Maple Street, Suite 310, Issaquah, WA 98027 and can be reached by phone at 425-313-1184.
