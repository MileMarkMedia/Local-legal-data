# The Reep Law Firm

The Reep Law Firm is a Clearwater, Florida criminal defense and personal injury practice led by Justin Reep, a former Assistant State Attorney in the Sixth Judicial Circuit who handled hundreds of criminal cases as a prosecutor. The firm represents clients throughout Pinellas County, including Clearwater, Clearwater Beach, Seminole, Largo and Pinellas Park, in cases ranging from DUI and BUI, drug possession, domestic battery and domestic injunctions to theft, sexual battery, juvenile delinquency, probation violations, white collar crime and homicide charges, and also assists with sealing and expungement. Drawing on his background as a prosecutor, Justin Reep applies an insider's understanding of how local law enforcement and the courts operate to build defenses for clients facing misdemeanor and felony charges, and may negotiate alternatives such as probation, diversion programs or community service. The personal injury practice covers auto, motorcycle, truck, boat, pedestrian, rideshare and dog bite cases, premises liability and wrongful death. The firm has been recognized with an Avvo Rating of 10.0 and a Top 40 Under 40 honor, and Justin Reep is a member of the Clearwater Bar Association. Free consultations are available. The Reep Law Firm is located at 2274 State Rd 580, Suite B, Clearwater, FL 33763 and can be reached by phone at 727-330-6502.
