# Killian, Davis, & Richter, P.C.

Killian, Davis, & Richter, P.C. is a personal injury and accident law firm serving Grand Junction, Montrose, Delta, and the greater Western Slope of Colorado, including Mesa, Delta, Montrose, and Garfield counties. Founded in 1991 by managing shareholder J. Keith Killian, a former U.S. Air Force Judge Advocate and Colorado State Public Defender, the firm is led by shareholders J. Keith Killian, Damon J. Davis, and Christopher H. Richter and brings more than 34 years of experience. The firm handles car, truck, and motorcycle accidents, including crashes on I-70 and US-50, along with workplace injuries, workers' compensation, wrongful death, and cases tied to mining, oilfield work, and propane and natural gas incidents. Reported results include a $6,267,717 judgment for injuries caused by a semi-truck collision and a $2,438,051 settlement for a propane explosion. Recognition includes Super Lawyers, an AV Preeminent rating from Martindale-Hubbell, The National Trial Lawyers Top 100, and Best Law Firms in Colorado. The firm speaks Spanish, offers free consultations, and works on a contingency fee basis, with clients communicating directly with their attorney. Killian, Davis, & Richter, P.C. is located at 202 N 7th St, Grand Junction, CO 81501 and can be reached by phone at 970-241-0707.
