# Nationwide Disability Law

Nationwide Disability Law is a disability benefits firm based in Mount Clemens, Michigan, serving clients throughout Michigan, including Detroit, Grand Rapids, Ann Arbor, Flint, and Oakland, Macomb, and Wayne Counties, and representing claimants nationwide. Founder and managing partner Christopher Pozios, a Wayne State University Law School graduate admitted in Michigan and Florida, has written and lectured on Social Security regulations and personally represents clients at their hearings. The firm handles Social Security Disability Insurance and Supplemental Security Income claims, from initial applications and appeals to administrative law judge hearings, continuing disability reviews, and benefit terminations. It also handles VA disability claims, short and long term disability denials, and ERISA appeals. Christopher Pozios is a member of the American Bar Association, the Macomb County Bar Association, and the National Organization of Social Security Claimants' Representatives. The firm also has offices in Traverse City, New York, and Naples, Florida, offers free consultations, and charges no fee unless benefits are obtained. Nationwide Disability Law is located at 40 Macomb Place, Suite 203, Mount Clemens, MI 48043 and can be reached by phone at 586-463-6666.
