# Law Offices of Lauren H. Kane

The Law Offices of Lauren H. Kane is a Philadelphia family law firm focused on divorce, child custody, support and related domestic relations matters in Pennsylvania and New Jersey. Founded and led by attorney Lauren H. Kane, a graduate of Yale University and Villanova University School of Law licensed in Pennsylvania, New Jersey and Florida, the firm draws on more than 40 years of family law experience in the courts of Philadelphia, Montgomery, Bucks, Chester and Delaware counties and the South Jersey counties of Atlantic, Burlington, Camden and Gloucester. Matters range from uncontested and collaborative divorce to high net worth, military and same-sex divorce, contested custody and relocation, alimony, pension division, prenuptial and postnuptial agreements, adoption, paternity and protection from abuse cases. In addition to its Philadelphia office, the firm serves Main Line clients from an office in Bala Cynwyd. As a solo practice, clients work directly with Ms. Kane from the first consultation through final resolution rather than being passed to associates or staff. The Law Offices of Lauren H. Kane is located at 3900 Ford Road, Suite 7L, Philadelphia, PA 19131 and can be reached by phone at 215-918-9453.
