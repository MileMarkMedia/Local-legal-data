# Knudsen Law

Knudsen Law is a Tampa law firm focused on estate planning, trusts, and probate for families throughout Hillsborough, Pinellas, and Pasco counties. Attorney Paul Knudsen, a native Floridian with more than ten years of legal practice, began his career as a prosecutor and later practiced family law before focusing solely on trusts, estate planning, and probate. He holds degrees from Florida State University and Florida Coastal School of Law. The firm prepares wills, revocable living trusts, irrevocable and dynasty trusts, powers of attorney, living wills, healthcare surrogate designations, and Lady Bird deeds, and assists with asset protection, high net-worth planning, and business succession. On the probate side, Knudsen Law guides personal representatives and beneficiaries through formal and summary administration, ancillary and snowbird probate, intestate estates, and probate litigation such as will contests and breach of fiduciary duty claims, using mediation when possible. The firm serves Tampa, South Tampa, Westchase, Brandon, Clearwater, St. Petersburg, Wesley Chapel, and surrounding communities. Knudsen Law is located at 4006 S MacDill Ave, Suite G, Tampa, FL 33611 and can be reached by phone at 727-398-3600.
