# Phillip J. Murphy, Attorney at Law

Phillip J. Murphy, Attorney at Law is a New City, New York criminal defense practice serving clients in Rockland, Westchester, and Orange counties and the Lower Hudson Valley, as well as Bergen County, New Jersey. Phillip J. Murphy has practiced law since 1989 and brings more than 25 years of experience in criminal and civil matters. He is admitted in New York, New Jersey, Connecticut, and Pennsylvania, as well as several federal district courts and the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Third Circuit. He handles cases in Rockland County Court, Clarkstown Justice Court, and other Rockland County justice courts, along with Bergen County Superior and Municipal Courts. His practice covers DWI and DUI, drug offenses, assault and violent crimes, theft and shoplifting, traffic violations, juvenile offenses, and probation violations. He is a member of the New York State Association of Criminal Defense Lawyers and serves on the Criminal Law Committee of the Rockland County Bar Association. Rather than rushing to a plea, he reviews the facts and law in each case and pursues alternatives such as diversion or treatment programs where appropriate. Phillip J. Murphy, Attorney at Law is located at 10 Esquire Road, Suite 10, New City, NY 10956 and can be reached by phone at 845-639-6600.
