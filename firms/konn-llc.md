# Konn LLC

Konn LLC is a boutique debt settlement law firm serving clients throughout metro Atlanta and across Georgia, including Alpharetta, Augusta, Decatur, Dunwoody, Macon, Marietta, Roswell, Sandy Springs, Savannah, and Smyrna. Founded by attorney Neil J. Konn, who was admitted to the State Bar of Georgia in 2010 and has more than 15 years of experience, the firm concentrates exclusively on settling consumer debt, including credit card debt and personal loan debt. Neil Konn began his career as a creditors' rights attorney representing financial institutions in collection lawsuits, garnishments, and foreclosures before moving to represent individual debtors, giving him direct insight into how creditors approach collection. He has settled more than 1,500 cases for individual debtors. He earned his economics degree from Emory University and his law degree from Thomas M. Cooley Law School, is admitted in Georgia's Middle and Southern federal districts, and is a member of the Atlanta Bar Association and the South Asian Bar Association. The firm offers free initial consultations by phone, email, or virtual meeting. Konn LLC is located at 6075 Barfield Rd, Suite 411, Atlanta, GA 30328 and can be reached by phone at 678-820-8704.
