# Nowack & Olson, PLLC

Nowack & Olson, PLLC is a bankruptcy, foreclosure defense, and real estate law firm serving clients throughout South Florida, with offices in Plantation, Doral, Boca Raton, Jupiter, and Port St. Lucie. Partners Mitchell J. Nowack and Christian J. Olson each have more than 20 years of experience in Florida's bankruptcy courts, giving the firm more than 40 years of combined experience. The firm handles Chapter 7, Chapter 11, and Chapter 13 bankruptcy for individuals, families, and small businesses, along with foreclosure defense, loan modifications, lien stripping, short sales, creditor harassment, repossession, wage garnishment, and real estate closings and contracts. Clients come from Broward, Palm Beach, Martin, and St. Lucie Counties and communities such as Fort Lauderdale, Miami, Delray Beach, Palm Beach Gardens, and West Palm Beach. Each client's finances are reviewed before a solution is recommended, and the attorneys attend the First Meeting of Creditors with their clients. The firm offers free consultations and payment plans, and Spanish speaking clients are served in their own language. Nowack & Olson, PLLC is located at 8551 West Sunrise Blvd, Suite 208, Plantation, FL 33322 and can be reached by phone at 954-349-2265.
