# Daniel T. Fleischer, Attorney at Law

Daniel T. Fleischer, Attorney at Law, focuses on estate planning and probate for clients in Plantation, Boca Raton, and Aventura, Florida and throughout Broward, Palm Beach, and Miami-Dade Counties. Attorney Daniel Todd Fleischer has practiced estate planning and probate law since 2001 and opened his own firm in 2012. He earned a combined JD/MBA from the University of Miami and is also a Certified Financial Planner, which allows him to coordinate legal planning with clients' broader financial goals. Before opening his practice, he served corporate fiduciaries as an Estate Tax Officer and Regional Probate Administrator at Northern Trust and as a Trust and Fiduciary Specialist at Wells Fargo Private Bank. The firm handles wills, trusts, elder law, Medicaid planning, asset protection, guardianship, business succession, and estate tax planning, along with probate, trust administration, and probate and trust litigation. Daniel holds an AV Preeminent rating from Martindale-Hubbell and was named to Florida Trend's Legal Elite for Wills, Trusts and Probate. Daniel T. Fleischer, Attorney at Law is located at 7805 SW 6th Ct, Plantation, FL 33324 and can be reached by phone at 954-888-1747.
