# Daniel M. Copeland, Attorney at Law, P.A.

Daniel M. Copeland, Attorney at Law, P.A. is a Jacksonville, Florida law firm focused on construction law and real estate litigation, serving individuals, families and businesses throughout northeast Florida. Founder and principal attorney Daniel M. Copeland is a lifelong Jacksonville resident who grew up in the Mandarin area, graduated from The Bolles School, and earned a B.S. in Accounting from Jacksonville University. He is also an experienced contractor and developer who has built 1,400 homes, which gives him practical insight into construction contracts and documents, construction defects, construction liens and disputes involving unlicensed contractors. The firm handles real property matters including quiet title actions, partitions, tax deeds, adverse possession, encroachment and boundary disputes, and landlord and tenant cases. It also assists with business formation, including choosing and forming corporations, partnerships and LLCs, along with family law and wills and trusts. Clients are offered a free case evaluation and are guided through each step of their legal matter with clear explanations of the process. Daniel M. Copeland, Attorney at Law, P.A. is located at 9310 Old Kings Road South, Suite 1501, Jacksonville, FL 32257 and can be reached by phone at 904-482-0616.
