# Justin Spizman

The Spizman Firm is a criminal defense and personal injury practice led by Managing Partner Justin Spizman, serving clients in Atlanta, Georgia, including DeKalb and Fulton Counties and throughout the state. The firm handles criminal matters such as DUI, traffic offenses, drug charges, assault, and other felonies and misdemeanors, along with personal injury cases involving vehicle accidents, medical malpractice, nursing home abuse, catastrophic injuries, and wrongful death. Justin Spizman brings more than 19 years of experience to his clients and has been recognized by Super Lawyers, the National Trial Lawyers as a Top 40 Under 40 and Top 100 Trial Lawyer, Georgia Trend Magazine's Legal Elite, and carries an AV Preeminent rating from Martindale-Hubbell. The Spizman Firm is located at 200 Ashford Center North, Suite 200, Atlanta, GA 30338 and can be reached by phone at (770) 685-6400.
