# The Law Office of Laura A. Olson, P.A.

The Law Office of Laura A. Olson, P.A. is a Tampa law firm focused on divorce and family law, serving South Tampa and the surrounding bay area. Founder and principal attorney Laura Anne Olson is a South Tampa native with more than 25 years of experience as a trial and appellate lawyer. She earned an accounting degree from the University of South Florida and her law degree from Stetson University College of Law, and clerked during law school for Chief Judge Dennis Alvarez of the 13th Judicial Circuit and Judge Elizabeth Kovachevich of the U.S. District Court for the Middle District of Florida. She is AV rated by Martindale-Hubbell. The firm handles child custody, child support, alimony, property division, high net worth, same-sex, military, collaborative and uncontested divorce, prenuptial agreements, paternity, relocation, enforcement, post-judgment modifications and domestic violence matters, along with personal injury and civil litigation cases. Clients work one on one with Ms. Olson, and the firm offers a free 30-minute phone consultation, flexible evening and weekend appointments, and hourly, flat or contingency fee options where appropriate. The Law Office of Laura A. Olson, P.A. is located at 1313 S. Howard Avenue, Tampa, FL 33606 and can be reached by phone at 813-222-0888.
