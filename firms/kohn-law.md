# Kohn Law

Kohn Law is a Tampa firm focused on representing victims of nursing home abuse and neglect, along with assisted living facility, hospital, and rehab center neglect, medical malpractice, personal injury, and wrongful death claims for clients across Florida. Attorney Kimberley M. Kohn began her career as an Assistant Public Defender in Tampa and has focused since 1999 on representing people harmed by individuals, insurance companies, institutions, and nursing homes, bringing more than 25 years of experience. She is admitted to practice in Florida, California, Pennsylvania, and New Jersey, as well as the Middle, Northern, and Southern Districts of Florida. The firm handles cases involving bed sores, malnutrition, dehydration, falls, infections and sepsis, physical and sexual abuse, financial abuse, and nursing home deaths. Kim Kohn has obtained more than $50 million for clients, with reported results including a $6 million nursing home abuse recovery involving pressure sores, weight loss, dehydration, and infections, and an auto accident settlement of over $4 million. She has spoken on elder law topics before the Collier, Osceola, and Orange County Bar Associations. The firm offers free consultations. Kohn Law is located at 3004 W. Cypress Street, Tampa, FL 33609 and can be reached by phone at 813-428-8504.
