# Cairns Law

Cairns Law is a family law firm serving Clearwater, Largo, and St. Petersburg, Florida. The firm was founded by husband and wife attorneys Joseph P. Cairns and Jarica Cairns, who focus their practice on family law and bring 16 years of experience. Joseph Cairns graduated cum laude from Stetson University College of Law, joined The Florida Bar in 2007, and previously worked as a senior associate attorney in family law, while Jarica Cairns attended the University of Tulsa College of Law, received the Order of Barristers award, and practiced exclusively family law before the firm opened. The firm handles divorce, child custody, alimony, child support modification, relocation, parenting plans, paternity, adoption, domestic violence injunctions, prenuptial and postnuptial agreements, enforcement of final judgments, and mediation. The attorneys combine trial advocacy and litigation skill with an approach geared toward amicable resolution, creating a specific plan with each client and explaining possible solutions and their risks. Evening and weekend appointments are available. Cairns Law is located at 801 West Bay Drive, Suite 713, Largo, FL 33770 and can be reached by phone at 727-683-1472.
