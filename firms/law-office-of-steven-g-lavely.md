# Law Office of Steven G. Lavely

The Law Office of Steven G. Lavely is a personal injury and auto accident firm serving Bradenton, St. Petersburg, and surrounding Gulf Coast communities, including Lakewood Ranch, Palmetto, and Longboat Key, with offices in Bradenton and St. Petersburg. Steven G. Lavely opened the firm in June 1986 and has practiced in Florida since 1984, bringing more than 30 years of experience as lead trial counsel for thousands of accident victims, including cases involving catastrophic injuries. A graduate of the University of South Florida and the University of Miami School of Law, he served as an Assistant State Attorney in Bradenton and Sarasota and has been Florida Bar Board Certified in Civil Trial Law since 2009. He has been recognized as a Florida Super Lawyer since 2010 and holds an AV rating from Martindale-Hubbell. The firm handles car, truck, motorcycle, bicycle, pedestrian, boat, and rideshare accidents, slip and falls, dog bites, nursing home abuse, wrongful death, and other injury matters, along with criminal defense. Spanish is spoken, consultations are free, cases are handled on a contingency fee basis, and home or hospital visits are available. The Law Office of Steven G. Lavely is located at 527 Manatee Ave. East, Bradenton, FL 34208 and can be reached by phone at 941-747-7994.
