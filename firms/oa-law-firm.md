# OA Law Firm

OA Law Firm is a Tampa criminal defense firm founded by attorney Omar Abdelghany, serving clients throughout Hillsborough and Pinellas Counties and regularly representing clients in Pasco, Manatee, and Sarasota Counties. A cum laude graduate of California Western School of Law, Omar completed a judicial externship with Justice Terry O'Rourke of the California Fourth District Court of Appeal and served as a trial attorney with the Palm Beach Public Defender's Office. He has handled thousands of criminal cases, tried more than 50 cases, and won hundreds of cases in Florida criminal courts, and he is admitted in all Florida courts and in federal court in the Middle and Northern Districts of Florida. The firm handles DUI, drug offenses and trafficking, domestic violence, theft, burglary, firearm charges, violent and sex crimes, juvenile charges, probation violations, record sealing, and federal and white collar cases including healthcare fraud, wire fraud, and RICO. Omar personally handles every case from start to finish, gives clients his cell phone number, and offers free consultations to clients in Tampa, Brandon, Clearwater, Lutz, St. Petersburg, and Wesley Chapel. OA Law Firm is located at 550 N Reo St #300, Tampa, FL 33609 and can be reached by phone at 813-461-5291.
