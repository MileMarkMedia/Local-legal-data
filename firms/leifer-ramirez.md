# Leifer & Ramirez

Leifer & Ramirez is a personal injury law firm based in Boca Raton serving clients across South Florida and the Treasure Coast, with offices in Boca Raton, Fort Lauderdale, West Palm Beach and Port St. Lucie. Led by attorneys Corey Leifer and Fernando Ramirez, who have more than 25 years of combined experience, the firm handles car, truck, motorcycle, pedestrian and rideshare accidents, slip and fall and premises liability claims, medical malpractice, nursing home abuse, defective products, catastrophic injuries and wrongful death. Reported results include a $1,000,000 slip and fall recovery in a case where liability was disputed and the claim was originally denied, $837,500 for a client injured in a multiple vehicle crash involving an Uber, and a $350,000 policy limits settlement in a pedestrian wrongful death case. The firm prepares every case as if it will go to trial and holds an Avvo Superb rating and a Martindale-Hubbell peer rating for ethical standards and legal ability. Cases are handled on a contingency fee basis, home and hospital visits are available, and the team serves clients in English, Spanish and Portuguese. Leifer & Ramirez is located at 7251 West Palmetto Park Road, Suite 201, Boca Raton, FL 33433 and can be reached by phone at 561-660-9421.
