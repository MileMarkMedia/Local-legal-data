# Brian G. Miller Co., L.P.A.

Brian G. Miller Co., L.P.A. is a personal injury law firm based in Worthington, serving clients throughout Columbus, the rest of Ohio, and the Huntington area of West Virginia. Founded in 2004 by attorney Brian G. Miller, the firm brings more than 25 years of trial experience to auto, truck, motorcycle, bicycle, and pedestrian accidents, along with wrongful death, catastrophic injury, premises liability, workplace accident, and wage and hour claims. Brian Miller has argued before the Supreme Court of Ohio and multiple appellate courts, and attorney Adam L. Slone, who joined the firm in 2018, is also licensed in West Virginia. Results include a $34,317,636 jury verdict for a traumatic brain injury caused by a delayed electric company response and a $9,825,000 recovery in an Ohio truck crash. Recognition includes Super Lawyers, Best Lawyers, Best Law Firms, AV Preeminent, and the Multi-Million Dollar Advocates Forum. The firm keeps a selective, low-volume caseload, emphasizes diligent investigation and close attorney involvement, and handles injury cases on a contingency fee basis. Brian G. Miller Co., L.P.A. is located at 250 West Old Wilson Bridge Road, Suite 270, Worthington, OH 43085 and can be reached by phone at 614-221-4035.
