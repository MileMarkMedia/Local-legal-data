# Louis S. Schwartz, Attorneys at Law

Louis S. Schwartz, Attorneys at Law is a Philadelphia consumer law firm founded in 1996 to advocate for consumers subjected to unfair business, industry and sales practices. Attorney Louis S. Schwartz, a graduate of The George Washington University and Western New England College School of Law, served as a law clerk to a Philadelphia Municipal Court judge. The firm handles debt collection abuse, credit card and student loan debt, mortgage foreclosure defense, loan modifications, deficiency judgments and sheriff's sales, predatory and subprime lending, unlawful credit reporting, insurance claim denials, home contractor fraud and used car fraud, with bankruptcy considered only as a last resort. It pursues both individual cases and class actions, including matters involving Equifax and Specialized Loan Servicing. Schwartz has been featured in a television news segment and on a 610 AM Sports Radio podcast. The firm serves individuals and families throughout the Philadelphia metropolitan area, including Montgomery, Delaware, Berks and Lehigh Counties, and offers free, confidential consultations. Louis S. Schwartz, Attorneys at Law is located at 1500 John F Kennedy Blvd, Suite 1825, Philadelphia, PA 19102 and can be reached by phone at 215-790-1800.
