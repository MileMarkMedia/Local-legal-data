# Halpern Santos & Pinkert, P.A.

Halpern Santos & Pinkert, P.A. is a Coral Gables personal injury law firm representing people harmed by negligence, defective products, and corporate misconduct from Miami to Fort Lauderdale and in every county in Florida. Partners Jay Halpern, Ernesto L. Santos, Jr., and Ian D. Pinkert lead the firm, which has more than 60 years of combined experience, and Jay Halpern has exclusively represented injured plaintiffs for over 32 years. The firm handles car, truck, rideshare, and motorcycle accidents, tire defect and rollover cases, premises liability, medical malpractice, cruise ship injuries, wrongful death, and product liability claims involving airbags, lithium batteries, and goods sold through Amazon. The firm has recovered over $200 million for its clients, including a $37,800,000 verdict against Hankook Tire Company, described as the largest compensatory damage award in Virginia history, and an $11,550,000 settlement against an automobile manufacturer, a tire dealership, and a van owner. Its attorneys have earned recognition including Super Lawyers, an AV Preeminent rating from Martindale-Hubbell, and Florida Legal Elite. Consultations are free, and cases are handled on a contingency fee basis. Halpern Santos & Pinkert, P.A. is located at 150 Alhambra Cir, Suite 1100, Coral Gables, FL 33134 and can be reached by phone at 305-445-1111.
