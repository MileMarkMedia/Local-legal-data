# Halpern Santos & Pinkert, P.A. (Tire Defects)

Halpern Santos & Pinkert, P.A. is a Coral Gables, Florida law firm whose tire practice focuses on tire defect and rollover litigation for clients nationwide. Led by attorney Jay Halpern, the firm has handled cases against almost every major tire manufacturer in the world, having tried or settled cases with Bridgestone, Continental, Cooper, Dunlop, Firestone, General Tire, Goodyear, Hankook, Michelin, Sumitomo, and Yokohama. It handles claims involving tire design and manufacturing defects, failure to warn, tread and belt separation, sidewall failures, tire aging and recalls affecting cars, trucks, motorcycles, ATVs, and RVs. The firm has resolved tire and rollover cases in excess of $100 million, including a $37,800,000 verdict against Hankook Tire Company, described as the largest compensatory damage award in Virginia history, and a $6,800,000 verdict against General Tire Co. Recognition includes Super Lawyers, AV Preeminent, and Florida Legal Elite. The firm offers free, confidential consultations, works on a contingency fee basis, and accepts referrals and co-counsel arrangements with local attorneys. Halpern Santos & Pinkert, P.A. is located at 150 Alhambra Cir, #1100, Coral Gables, FL 33134 and can be reached by phone at 305-445-1111.
