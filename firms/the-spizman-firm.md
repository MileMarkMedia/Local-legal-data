# The Spizman Firm

The Spizman Firm is an Atlanta DUI and criminal defense law firm led by Managing Partner Justin Spizman, a former Fulton County prosecutor who has tried more than 200 cases to verdict in Georgia's State and Superior Courts, Court of Appeals and Supreme Court. The firm defends DUI charges, including DUI per se, less safe driving, drug, commercial and underage DUI and BUI, as well as drug, theft, weapons, white collar, sex crime and homicide charges, and handles personal injury cases involving impaired drivers on a contingency fee basis. It serves Atlanta, Fulton, DeKalb, Cobb and Gwinnett counties and clients across Georgia. Justin Spizman has been named a Georgia Super Lawyer every year since 2014 and to Georgia Trend's Legal Elite every year since 2013, and has been recognized by The National Trial Lawyers Top 40 Under 40 and Top 100 Trial Lawyers. The firm holds a 2021 AV Preeminent rating from Martindale-Hubbell. Reported results include dismissed felony murder charges and not guilty verdicts in DUI cases involving a .23 blood test and a .18 breath test. Several full-time staff members speak Spanish, and a live person answers calls at any time. The Spizman Firm is located at 200 Ashford Center N, Suite 200, Atlanta, GA 30338 and can be reached by phone at 770-685-6400.
