# Jennifer Kane Waterway

Jennifer Kane Waterway, P.A. is a Fort Lauderdale marital and family law firm serving clients throughout Broward, Palm Beach, and Miami-Dade Counties. Attorney Jennifer Kane Waterway earned her J.D. magna cum laude from Nova Southeastern University's Shepard Broad Law Center and was admitted to The Florida Bar in 2000. She served as a judicial law clerk at the Fourth District Court of Appeal and as a litigator at Akerman Senterfitt before focusing on family law. She handles divorce, alimony and division of assets, child support, child custody and timesharing, relocation, paternity, domestic violence injunctions, dependency matters, prenuptial and postnuptial agreements, and post-judgment modification, contempt, and enforcement. Ms. Waterway also serves as a guardian ad litem and attorney ad litem for children in need and provides pro bono services through Legal Aid. She holds an AV Preeminent peer review rating from Martindale-Hubbell and has been recognized by Super Lawyers and Avvo. The firm works with psychologists and accountants when needed, brings trial experience to litigated cases, and works to resolve matters amicably with the least amount of conflict. Jennifer Kane Waterway, P.A. is located at 500 E. Broward Blvd., Suite 900, Fort Lauderdale, FL 33394 and can be reached by phone at 954-332-2410.
