# Mainor Ellis Injury Lawyers

Mainor Ellis Injury Lawyers is a personal injury law firm serving accident victims and their families in Las Vegas, Henderson, and communities throughout Clark County and Southern Nevada. Founding partners Bradley S. Mainor and Adam Ellis, both Las Vegas natives, combine decades of experience in large law firms and high stakes litigation. The firm handles car, truck, motorcycle, bicycle, pedestrian, and rideshare accidents, premises liability, casino and hotel injuries, nursing home negligence, medical malpractice, defective products, catastrophic injury, and wrongful death claims. Reported results include a $40 million negligent security settlement, a $12.1 million product defect result, and an $11 million shuttle bus accident settlement. Bradley Mainor, who is fluent in Danish, is a member of the Million Dollar Advocates Forum, was named one of the Top 100 Trial Lawyers by the American Trial Lawyers Association, and has been recognized as Legal Elite by Nevada Business Magazine. Adam Ellis began his career as a law clerk to the Hon. Nancy Allf. Cases are handled on a contingency fee basis, and the firm advances case costs. Mainor Ellis Injury Lawyers is located at 8367 W. Flamingo Rd, Suite 200, Las Vegas, NV 89147 and can be reached by phone at 702-450-5000.
