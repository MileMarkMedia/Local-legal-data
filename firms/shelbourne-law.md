# Shelbourne Law

Shelbourne Law is a locally owned Summerville, South Carolina law firm serving Summerville, Charleston, and the surrounding Lowcountry, including North Charleston, Mt. Pleasant, Goose Creek, Ladson, Hanahan, Moncks Corner, St. George, and Ridgeville. Founder and lead attorney Brandt Shelbourne began practicing law in Charleston in 1991, spent six years with a Charleston litigation firm, and moved his practice home to Summerville after 1997. He is a certified mediator appointed by Circuit and Probate Courts, serves part time as Assistant Chief Magistrate for Dorchester County, and earned his law degree cum laude from Cumberland School of Law. He practices with attorney Colin Hamilton. The firm handles personal injury and auto accidents, product liability, professional malpractice, estate planning, estate administration, probate litigation, conservatorships and guardianships, corporate law, business litigation, contract and real estate disputes, criminal defense, and mediation. The firm holds AV Preeminent ratings from 2017 and 2023. It offers free consultations, flat rate, hourly, and contingency fee options, and evening and weekend appointments. Shelbourne Law is located at 131 E Richardson Ave., Summerville, SC 29483 and can be reached by phone at 843-871-2210.
