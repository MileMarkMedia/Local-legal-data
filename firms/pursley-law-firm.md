# Pursley Law Firm

Pursley Law Firm is a personal injury firm serving Carlsbad, Oceanside, San Marcos, and the North County region of San Diego County, California. The firm was founded in 2020 by Jared R. Pursley, its managing attorney, who has been a member of the State Bar of California since 2013. Pursley Law Firm represents clients in car, truck, bus, motorcycle, bicycle, pedestrian, and rideshare accidents, slip and fall incidents, dog bites, brain, burn, and catastrophic injuries, nursing home abuse, medical malpractice, and wrongful death claims. Results include a $1,000,000 policy limits recovery for a ladder fall from a condo roof, a $265,000 mediation settlement for a grocery store slip and fall in Orange County, and a $200,000 settlement for an intersection collision with a box truck in Los Angeles. The firm has been recognized by The National Trial Lawyers Top 40 and Top 100. Jared Pursley is a member of the San Diego County Bar Association, Consumer Attorneys of San Diego, and the Consumer Attorneys Association of Los Angeles. Cases are handled on a contingency basis and consultations are free. Pursley Law Firm is located at 701 Palomar Airport Road, Suite 300, Carlsbad, CA 92011 and can be reached by phone at 760-678-1884.
