# Gillette Law, P.A.

Gillette Law, P.A. is a personal injury firm with offices in Jacksonville, Florida and Brunswick, Georgia, serving Northeast Florida and Southeast Georgia, including Duval, St. Johns, Clay, Nassau, Glynn, and Camden counties. Charlie J. Gillette, Jr. founded the firm in 1995 after serving as an Assistant Public Defender in the Fourth Judicial Circuit and as a shareholder of Brannon & Gillette, P.A. He has practiced law since 1989, is admitted in Florida and Georgia, and has helped thousands of clients and handled hundreds of Jacksonville personal injury cases. The firm handles car, truck, motorcycle, bicycle, and pedestrian accidents, catastrophic injuries, medical malpractice, nursing home negligence, slip and fall claims, dog bites, wrongful death, and workers' compensation. Attorney Gillette takes a personal, hands-on approach in which clients speak with him directly, and the firm can meet clients at home or in the hospital when they cannot reach an office. He is a member of the National Bar Association, the D.W. Perkins Bar Association, the Florida Justice Association, and the Jacksonville Bar Association. Consultations are free and cases are handled on a contingency fee basis. Gillette Law, P.A. is located at 603 N Market St, Jacksonville, FL 32202 and can be reached by phone at 904-600-4758.
