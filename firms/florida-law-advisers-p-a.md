# Florida Law Advisers, P.A.

Florida Law Advisers, P.A. is a Tampa law firm handling family law and divorce, bankruptcy, personal injury, and immigration matters for clients throughout Tampa Bay, Orlando, and Central Florida, with offices in Tampa, Orlando, and Dade City. Its attorneys include Helbert Lopez, Michael R. Barnett, Martin B. Schwartz, Judy L. Gorman, and Lakeisha Simms Dunbar. The family law practice covers contested, uncontested, collaborative, military, and high net worth divorce, along with child custody, child support, alimony, paternity, and prenuptial agreements. The firm also helps clients with Chapter 7 and Chapter 13 bankruptcy, wage garnishment, foreclosure defense, and debt relief, and handles car accident, medical malpractice, and wrongful death claims. Helbert Lopez was recognized as a 2026 Rising Star, the firm carries an AV Preeminent rating from Martindale-Hubbell, and it holds an A+ rating from the Better Business Bureau. The firm aims to provide high-quality legal representation at a reasonable cost, with flat-fee options, payment plans, free consultations, evening appointments, and services in Spanish. Florida Law Advisers, P.A. is located at 1120 E Kennedy Blvd, Unit 231, Tampa, FL 33602 and can be reached by phone at 800-990-7763.
