# The Law Office of Adam R. Weaver, Esq.

The Law Office of Adam R. Weaver, Esq. is a general practice law firm in Lehighton, Pennsylvania, with an emphasis on bankruptcy and debt relief for individuals, families and small business owners in Carbon County and communities throughout Lehigh and Schuylkill counties, including Slatington and Tamaqua. Attorney Adam R. Weaver has more than 15 years of experience representing debtors and creditors in eastern Pennsylvania and has presented continuing legal education courses to other attorneys. The firm handles Chapter 7 and Chapter 13 bankruptcy, debt settlement, student loan debt, small business debt relief and mortgage foreclosure defense, along with civil litigation, criminal defense, family law, and wills and probate, including a Wills for Heroes program for military veterans. Its reported cases include In re Excel Storage Products, L.P., in which the court affirmed jurisdiction over an out-of-state defendant in an avoidable preference action while the firm represented the bankruptcy trustee. Clients speak directly with Attorney Weaver about their case and options, and evening and weekend meetings are available by appointment. The Law Office of Adam R. Weaver, Esq. is located at 1407 Blakeslee Blvd Dr E, Lehighton, PA 18235 and can be reached by phone at 570-818-4888.
