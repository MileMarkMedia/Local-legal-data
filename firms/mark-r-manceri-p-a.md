# Mark R. Manceri, P.A.

Mark R. Manceri, P.A. is a Pompano Beach law firm focused on estate, trust, guardianship, and probate litigation for clients throughout Broward County and nearby communities, including Fort Lauderdale, Coral Springs, Deerfield Beach, Parkland, and Plantation. Attorney Mark R. Manceri brings more than 40 years of experience, is Board Certified in Wills, Trusts and Estates by The Florida Bar, holds an AV Preeminent rating from Martindale-Hubbell, and is also a certified public accountant admitted to practice before the U.S. Tax Court. The firm handles will and trust contests, undue influence and lack of capacity claims, breach of fiduciary duty, beneficiary and trustee disputes, elective share claims, guardianship litigation, accounting disputes, and estate and trust administration. Notable appellate wins include Steuerwald v. Qualls and Jervis v. Tucker in Florida's Fourth District Court of Appeal. The firm represents both fiduciaries and beneficiaries, including corporate trust companies and professional guardians, and works toward resolution through mediation, settlement, or litigation. Services are available in English and Spanish. Mark R. Manceri, P.A. is located at 1600 South Federal Hwy Suite 900, Pompano Beach, FL 33062 and can be reached by phone at 954-491-7099.
