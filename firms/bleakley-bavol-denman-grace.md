# Bleakley Bavol Denman & Grace

Bleakley Bavol Denman & Grace (BBDG Law) is a Tampa law firm focused on estate and business litigation for clients throughout Florida. Founded in 2000, the firm brings more than 25 years of litigating experience to commercial and business disputes, shareholder and partnership disputes, trust, probate and guardianship litigation, will contests, fiduciary litigation, construction litigation, real estate and land use litigation, and liability defense. It also advises on estate planning, business formation and transactions, and labor and employment matters. The firm is led by managing partner J. Ronald Denman along with named partners Robert W. Bleakley, Charles D. Bavol, and Robert J. Grace, heading a team of 19 attorneys that includes trial lawyers, transactional attorneys, and counselors. BBDG Law is an AV rated firm. Its attorneys work as an extension of each client's business, looking for practical solutions and personalized, client-centered representation. Bleakley Bavol Denman & Grace is located at 15316 North Florida Ave, Tampa, FL 33613 and can be reached by phone at 813-221-3759.
