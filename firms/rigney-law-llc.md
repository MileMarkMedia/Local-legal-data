# Rigney Law LLC

Rigney Law LLC is an Indianapolis criminal defense firm founded in 2017 by Jacob Rigney and Kassi Rigney, a husband and wife team of former Marion County deputy prosecutors with more than 30 years of combined criminal law experience, including extensive jury trial work. Jake supervised a major felony court and handled cases such as murder, robbery, and burglary, while Kassi spent eight years in the major felony drug courts and trained officers on narcotics and firearm search warrants. The firm defends clients facing OWI and DUI, drug, gun, and violent crime charges, along with theft, fraud, sex offense allegations, juvenile cases, probation violations, appeals, and federal prosecutions in the Southern District of Indiana. It also handles civil asset forfeiture, expungement, license rehabilitation, red flag firearm seizures, protective orders, and parole matters. Rigney Law serves Indianapolis and Marion County as well as Hamilton, Hendricks, Johnson, Boone, Hancock, Madison, and Morgan counties. The attorneys work to get involved early, often before charges are filed, and give clients frank assessments starting with their goals. Rigney Law LLC is located at 250 E 38th St, Indianapolis, IN 46205 and can be reached by phone at 317-623-0989.
