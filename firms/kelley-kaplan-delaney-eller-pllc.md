# Kelley Kaplan Delaney & Eller, PLLC

Kelley Kaplan Delaney & Eller, PLLC is a West Palm Beach law firm concentrating on bankruptcy, foreclosure defense, and business and commercial litigation for individuals and businesses throughout South Florida, with an additional office in Stuart by appointment. Managing Partner Craig I. Kelley founded the firm in 2001 and has taught bankruptcy and foreclosure as a professor for the last 15 years. He practices alongside attorneys Dana Kaplan, C. Craig Eller, and Suzanne Delaney. The firm handles Chapter 7, 11, and 13 bankruptcy, asset protection, creditor harassment, wage garnishments, student loans, debt settlement, foreclosure defense and alternatives, loan modification, breach of contract, business torts, debtor and creditor rights, shareholder and partner disputes, business formation, and commercial and residential leases. Its recognitions include an AV Preeminent peer review rating from Martindale-Hubbell, Super Lawyers honors for Craig I. Kelley, and Avvo 10.0 Top Attorney ratings in bankruptcy and foreclosure. The firm tailors counsel to each client's needs and budget, makes attorneys available after hours, and does not push anyone into bankruptcy. Kelley Kaplan Delaney & Eller, PLLC is located at 1555 Palm Beach Lakes Blvd, Suite 1000, West Palm Beach, FL 33401 and can be reached by phone at 561-264-6850.
