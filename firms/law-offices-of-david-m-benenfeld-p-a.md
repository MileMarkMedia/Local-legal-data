# Law Offices Of David M. Benenfeld, P.A.

The Law Offices of David M. Benenfeld, P.A. is a South Florida workers' compensation and personal injury law firm with its main office in Sunrise and appointment offices in Fort Lauderdale and West Palm Beach, serving clients throughout Broward, Miami-Dade and Palm Beach counties. Attorney David M. Benenfeld, a member of the Million Dollar Advocates Forum and an Avvo Top Attorney in personal injury, leads the firm alongside attorney Lisa T. Blank, who has handled thousands of injury and workers' compensation claims over more than 20 years. The firm represents injured workers in construction, healthcare, retail, service and industrial jobs through benefit claims, denials, appeals and settlements, and it handles car, truck, motorcycle and bicycle accidents, slip and fall cases, nursing home abuse and wrongful death. Results include workers' compensation recoveries of $1.8 million and $1.5 million and a $1.3 million car accident recovery. The team speaks Spanish, works on a contingency fee basis and will travel to clients who are homebound or in the hospital. The Law Offices of David M. Benenfeld, P.A. is located at 7800 W Oakland Park Blvd, Building F, Suite 216, Sunrise, FL 33351 and can be reached by phone at 954-677-0155.
