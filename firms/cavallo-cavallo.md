# Cavallo & Cavallo

Cavallo & Cavallo is a family-owned law firm serving the Bronx, Westchester County, Putnam County, and the five boroughs of New York City, with offices in the Bronx and New Rochelle. Since 1989, the firm has focused on estate planning, elder law, Medicaid planning, real estate transactions, and personal injury matters, drawing on more than 35 years of combined experience. Founding partners Vincent A. Cavallo, Orlando A. Cavallo, Lawrence Fisher, and Gregory Stone help individuals and families with wills, trusts, powers of attorney, health care proxies, probate, guardianships, special needs planning, asset protection, long-term care planning, and nursing home care, as well as residential and commercial real estate purchases, sales, and refinancings. The personal injury practice covers car accidents, medical malpractice, product liability, slip and fall, and workplace injuries. The firm offers free consultations and a written legal game plan, meets clients in the office or at home, and makes house calls for elderly and disabled clients. Bilingual staff serve clients in English and Spanish. Cavallo & Cavallo is located at 3453 E. Tremont Avenue, Bronx, NY 10465 and can be reached by phone at 718-822-2203.
