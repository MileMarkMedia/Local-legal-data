# Medura Family Law

Medura Family Law is a boutique, all-female family law firm serving Salt Lake City, Utah. The firm focuses exclusively on family law matters, including divorce, child custody, child support, property division, alimony, high-asset and high-conflict divorce, mediation, prenuptial and postnuptial agreements, parentage, adoption, guardianship, and protective orders. The firm is led by Cassie Medura, Owner and Founder, who started her own family law practice fifteen years ago after working as a commercial litigator and shareholder at VanCott, Bagley, Cornwall and McCarthy. Cassie is an active member and executive board member of the American Family of Conciliation Courts, a member of the American Academy of Matrimonial Lawyers, and previously served on the Utah Supreme Court's Lawyer Wellness Committee and the Utah Supreme Court Ethics and Discipline panel. One of the firm's attorneys is a Utah State Court Rostered Domestic Mediator. The firm brings decades of combined experience and emphasizes a collaborative, trauma-informed, settlement-first approach to resolving family law disputes, including a proprietary mediation process. The firm also created Modern Family Foundations to help families build new structures after divorce. It serves Salt Lake City and surrounding communities including Park City, West Valley City, West Jordan, Sandy, South Jordan, Millcreek, Herriman, Taylorsville, Draper, Riverton, Tooele, Magna, Midvale, Cottonwood Heights, and Holladay. Medura Family Law is located at 68 S Main St, 5th Fl, Salt Lake City, UT 84101 and can be reached by phone at 801-883-8325.
