# Empowerment Legal, PLLC

Empowerment Legal, PLLC is a Jacksonville area law firm focused on veterans' claims, military law, immigration and family law, with offices in Orange Park and downtown Jacksonville. Founder and principal attorney Sidonie Parr is a disabled U.S. Navy veteran who served aboard the USS Iwo Jima, and the child of Haitian immigrants. She began her legal career as an Assistant State Attorney in Florida's 4th Judicial Circuit before practicing criminal defense. The firm assists veterans with VA claims and appeals before the Board of Veterans' Appeals and the Court of Appeals for Veterans Claims, and service members with non-judicial punishment, administrative separation and discharge review matters. It guides immigrants through family-based petitions, green cards, naturalization, asylum and deportation defense, and represents families in divorce, custody, support and adoption. The firm serves Duval County and neighboring communities and handles federal immigration and veterans' matters nationwide. Ms. Parr is a member of the American Immigration Lawyers Association, speaks Haitian Creole, and the firm offers free initial consultations. Empowerment Legal, PLLC is located at 330 Crossing Blvd, Suite 116, Orange Park, FL 32073 and can be reached by phone at 904-601-5673.
