# Poulos LoPiccolo PC

Poulos LoPiccolo PC is a New Jersey litigation firm in Ocean serving clients in Monmouth, Ocean, and Middlesex counties and throughout New Jersey and New York, with a focus on employment law. Led by attorneys Joseph LoPiccolo and John Poulos, the firm handles discrimination, harassment, retaliation, wage and hour, and family leave claims, along with personal injury, class actions, real estate, and wills, trusts, and estate planning. Its attorneys bring over 30 years of combined commercial litigation experience gained at a Manhattan law firm ranked first in the United States in the American Lawyer's A-List. Class action results include a $20 million result in Kuzian et al. v. Electrolux Home Products, Inc., a $7 million result in Caruso, et al v. Work Out World, and a $2 million result in Vitale v. US Gas & Electric Inc. The firm is recognized by Super Lawyers, is AV Peer Review Rated and Lead Counsel Rated, and is a member of the New Jersey Association for Justice. Clients speak directly with the lawyer handling their case, and the firm offers free initial consultations, hourly, flat, and contingency fee options, and after-hours appointments. Poulos LoPiccolo PC is located at 1305 South Roller Rd., Ocean, NJ 07712 and can be reached by phone at 732-757-0165.
