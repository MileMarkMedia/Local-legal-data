# Madonna Law Group

Madonna Law Group is a full service law firm serving Dade City, Zephyrhills, and communities throughout Pasco, Hernando, and Sumter Counties, including Brooksville, Bushnell, and The Villages. The firm has served Dade City residents since 1976, and its attorneys bring more than 75 years of combined experience to personal injury, criminal defense, family law, estate planning, probate, and business law matters. Managing Partner Bobbi Madonna is a former staff attorney for the Innocence Project of Florida and a former public defender in Hillsborough and Pinellas Counties. Partner Will Anderson is a former Tampa public defender, a Marine Corps Reserve veteran who served in Iraq, and president of the East Pasco Bar Association. Of Counsel Daniel Dwyer has been with the firm or its predecessor since 1991 and has been certified by the Supreme Court of Florida as a Family Law Mediator since 1997. The veteran owned firm has been recognized with honors including Super Lawyers, AV Preeminent, and The National Top 100 Trial Lawyers. Spanish language services are available in house, and evening, weekend, and off site meetings can be arranged by appointment. Madonna Law Group is located at 37837 Meridian Ave, Suite 311, Dade City, FL 33525 and can be reached by phone at 352-567-0411.
