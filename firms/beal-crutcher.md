# Beal \| Crutcher

Beal \| Crutcher is a Franklin, Tennessee law firm serving Brentwood, Franklin, Williamson County and the surrounding Middle Tennessee counties, with a core focus on divorce and family law. Attorney Robbie T. Beal is a former Circuit Court Judge for the 21st Judicial District, a former Juvenile Court Magistrate and a former Assistant District Attorney, and he is a Tennessee Supreme Court Rule 31 Family Law Mediator. Attorney Shannon Crutcher has practiced law for more than 12 years and previously served as Vice Mayor of the Fairview City Commission. The firm handles divorce, including uncontested, complex and high-asset cases, child custody and child support and their modification, alimony, property division and dividing a business, QDROs, paternity, relocation, grandparents' rights, prenuptial agreements, adoption, legal separation, annulment, domestic violence, juvenile court, enforcement and contempt, mediation and appeals. It also handles criminal defense and DUI, personal injury, business services and litigation, and estate planning and probate. The firm has been recognized by Super Lawyers and holds an Avvo Rating. Beal \| Crutcher is located at 103 Forrest Crossing Blvd, Suite 201A, Franklin, TN 37064 and can be reached by phone at 615-861-2304.
