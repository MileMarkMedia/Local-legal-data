# Foster Hsu, LLP

Foster Hsu, LLP is a San Jose family law firm serving clients throughout Santa Clara County, San Mateo County, Silicon Valley, the greater Bay Area, and the Northern Central Valley. The firm is led by partners Lydia E. Hsu and Kara N. Foster, both Certified Family Law Specialists through the State Bar of California Board of Legal Specialization and Fellows of the American Academy of Matrimonial Lawyers. Kara N. Foster is also a certified AAML mediator and volunteers as a settlement officer for the Family Court. The firm handles divorce and legal separation, child custody and visitation, child support, spousal support, property division, relocation, domestic violence and restraining orders, guardianship, stepparent and second parent adoption, mediation, and prenuptial and postnuptial agreements. Both partners have been named to Super Lawyers, and Kara N. Foster has also received Northern California Super Lawyers Rising Stars recognition. The firm takes a settlement-oriented approach, working toward amicable resolutions wherever possible while remaining prepared for trial when a case requires it. Services are offered in English and Mandarin. Foster Hsu, LLP is located at 84 W Santa Clara St, Suite 789, San Jose, CA 95113 and can be reached by phone at 408-841-7200.
