# Iamele & Iamele, LLP

Iamele & Iamele, LLP is a Baltimore litigation firm handling personal injury, medical malpractice, wrongful death, police misconduct, crime victim claims, and criminal defense throughout the Baltimore-Washington Metropolitan area, including Baltimore, Howard, Anne Arundel, and Prince George's Counties. The firm was founded by Domenic Iamele, who brings more than five decades of legal experience, including service as an Assistant State's Attorney in Baltimore City from 1970 to 1975, and is now led alongside Anton L. Iamele, who began his career clerking for Chief Judge Joseph F. Murphy, Jr. of the Maryland Court of Special Appeals. Both remain personally involved in most aspects of each case. The firm's results include a $2,500,000 wrongful death recovery in Sulton v. Baltimore County, a $2,000,000 negligent security settlement for a client pushed under a Light Rail train during a crowd surge, and a $500,000 police misconduct settlement. Honors include an AV Preeminent rating from Martindale-Hubbell, Super Lawyers, and The National Trial Lawyers. Injury cases are handled on a contingency basis, with free consultations and evening and weekend meetings available. Iamele & Iamele, LLP is located at 201 N Charles St, Suite 1802, Baltimore, MD 21201 and can be reached by phone at 410-779-6160.
