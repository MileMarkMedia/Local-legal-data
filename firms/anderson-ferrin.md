# Anderson & Ferrin

Anderson & Ferrin is an Orlando family law and bankruptcy firm serving clients throughout Orange County and Central Florida. The firm was co-founded by attorneys Victoria Lyn Anderson and Michael Ferrin, a married couple who practice together, bring more than 20 years of combined experience, and have provided counsel in family law and bankruptcy since 2007. Both are graduates of Barry University School of Law, and Victoria advanced in the legal field from secretary and paralegal to associate and partner before co-founding the firm. Family law matters include contested and uncontested divorce, child custody and time sharing, child support, spousal support, paternity, relocation, modifications, prenuptial and postnuptial agreements, step-parent adoption, and domestic violence injunctions. The bankruptcy practice covers Chapter 7, Chapter 13, foreclosure, and mortgage modification. The attorneys have been recognized by Super Lawyers, hold an AV Preeminent rating from Martindale-Hubbell, and received Avvo Clients' Choice awards in 2014 and 2016. They work directly with every client on a customized legal strategy and offer services in Spanish. Anderson & Ferrin is located at 1115 E. Livingston Street, Orlando, FL 32803 and can be reached by phone at 407-412-7041.
