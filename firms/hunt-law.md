# Hunt Law

Hunt Law, also known as the Law Office of Clifford J. Hunt, P.A., is a securities and business law firm in Seminole, in the Tampa Bay area, serving clients throughout Florida, across the United States, and abroad. Attorney Clifford J. Hunt brings more than 35 years of experience representing business entities in corporate and securities matters and has served as an arbitrator in approximately 100 securities sales arbitration cases. He practices alongside attorney Nancy W. Hunt, and both are AV Preeminent rated by Martindale-Hubbell. The firm counsels public and private companies on Regulation D, Rule 506, and Regulation A+ offerings, registration statements on Forms S-1, S-3, S-8, and 10, Blue Sky filings, Rule 144 and legend removal opinions, EDGAR filings, Rule 15c2-11 and Form 211 matters, and regulatory issues before the SEC, FINRA, and state regulators, as well as mergers, reverse mergers, acquisitions, entity formation, and outside corporate counsel services. Clients speak directly with a lawyer rather than support staff and can expect prompt responses to calls and emails. Hunt Law is located at 8200 Seminole Blvd., Seminole, FL 33772 and can be reached by phone at 727-471-0444.
