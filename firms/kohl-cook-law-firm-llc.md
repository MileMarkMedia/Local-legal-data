# Kohl & Cook Law Firm LLC

Kohl & Cook Law Firm LLC is an Ohio law firm focused on foreclosure defense, consumer law, civil litigation, and small business representation, with offices in Worthington, near Columbus, and Dayton serving clients statewide. The firm is led by partners Sean M. Kohl, Timothy J. Cook, and Andy Gerling. It helps homeowners defend foreclosures, negotiate loan modifications, and pursue RESPA claims, and represents consumers under more than 20 Ohio and federal consumer protection laws, including the FDCPA, FCRA, TCPA, Ohio Consumer Sales Practices Act, and lemon law. It also handles contract, construction, and commercial disputes for businesses and contractors. Reported results include multiple judgments exceeding $900,000 in damages and attorney's fees for victims of a Bitcoin pyramid investment scheme, a $65,000 judgment against a contractor for Consumer Sales Practices Act violations, and a successful foreclosure defense decision from Ohio's Second District Court of Appeals. Recognition includes Super Lawyers, Rising Stars, and an AV Preeminent rating from Martindale-Hubbell. The firm offers free consultations and a range of fee options, including contingency fees in appropriate cases. Kohl & Cook Law Firm LLC is located at 438 E Wilson Bridge Rd, Suite 200, Worthington, OH 43085 and can be reached by phone at 614-763-5111.
