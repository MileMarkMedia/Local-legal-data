# Cohan Law Firm

Cohan Law Firm is a New York City personal injury law firm serving clients throughout the Bronx and Brooklyn, as well as Manhattan, Queens, Staten Island, Long Island, and Westchester County. Founded in 2016 by managing partner Michael Cohan, who has been licensed in New York since 2012, the firm handles motor vehicle, rideshare, and pedestrian accidents, premises liability, construction accidents, medical malpractice, product liability, catastrophic injury, nursing home neglect, and wrongful death cases. Michael Cohan earned his law degree from St. John's University School of Law, clerked for a federal judge, and worked at the U.S. Department of Labor, and he has been recognized by Super Lawyers, the Million Dollar and Multi-Million Dollar Advocates Forums, and The National Trial Lawyers Top 40 Under 40. The firm has recovered more than $100 million for clients, including a $4,000,000 global settlement with a multinational company and a $2,500,000 recovery for a client struck by a car dealer delivery vehicle. The firm represents injured individuals and their families on a contingency fee basis and serves clients in English and Spanish. Cohan Law Firm is located at 401 Park Ave S, 10th Floor, New York, NY 10016 and can be reached by phone at 212-601-2693.
