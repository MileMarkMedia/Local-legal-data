# Law Office of Joseph Linnehan, Jr.

The Law Office of Joseph Linnehan, Jr. is a Boston personal injury firm representing accident victims throughout the greater Boston area. Founder and principal attorney Joseph R. Linnehan, Jr., a graduate of Boston College and New England School of Law, earned his law degree in 1992 and opened his own office in 2001, bringing over 30 years of legal experience. He is a member of the Massachusetts Bar Association. The firm handles car, truck, motorcycle, bicycle, pedestrian, and Uber and Lyft accidents, uninsured motorist claims, premises liability cases such as slip and falls, dog bites, and negligent security, workplace and construction accidents, products liability, medical malpractice, and catastrophic injuries including traumatic brain and spinal cord injuries. The firm has represented thousands of injured people and their families, with reported results including $250,000 recoveries for pedestrians with leg and hip injuries. Cases are handled on a contingency fee basis with no fee unless there is a recovery, the firm advances litigation costs, and free confidential consultations are available remotely or in person. The Law Office of Joseph Linnehan, Jr. is located at 15 Court Square, Suite 240, Boston, MA 02108 and can be reached by phone at 617-275-4200.
