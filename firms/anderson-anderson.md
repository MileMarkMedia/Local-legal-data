# Anderson & Anderson

Anderson & Anderson is a Tampa personal injury law firm focused on auto accidents, slip and fall cases, and other injury claims throughout the Tampa Bay area. The firm was founded in 1983 by Timothy G. Anderson Sr., a Florida board-certified civil trial attorney for more than 35 years who tried hundreds of cases, and is now led by his son, managing partner Timothy G. Anderson Jr., who practiced alongside his father for over a decade. The firm handles car, truck, motorcycle, bus, Uber and Lyft, hit and run, and uninsured motorist claims, along with premises liability, trip and fall, dog bite, wrongful death, medical malpractice, and construction injury cases. It represents clients across Hillsborough and Pinellas counties and nearby communities, including St. Petersburg, Clearwater, Brandon, Bradenton, Riverview, and Wesley Chapel. The attorneys have been recognized by Super Lawyers and are members of The National Trial Lawyers, the American Association for Justice, and the Florida Justice Association. As a two-lawyer firm, Anderson & Anderson meets face to face with every client, offers free consultations, works on a contingency fee, and has Spanish-speaking staff. Anderson & Anderson is located at 213 S Brevard Ave, Tampa, FL 33606 and can be reached by phone at 813-251-0072.
