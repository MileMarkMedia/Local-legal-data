# Bundza & Rodriguez, P.A.

Bundza & Rodriguez, P.A. is a Daytona Beach law firm focused on personal injury, estate planning, probate and guardianships, family law, and criminal defense, serving Volusia County, Flagler County, and clients throughout Florida. Founded in 2007 by trial attorneys Corey A. Bundza and Michael Rodriguez, the firm brings more than 15 years of combined experience. Corey Bundza, a University of Florida law graduate, handles the firm's first-party insurance claims and personal injury cases. Personal injury work covers car, truck, motorcycle, pedestrian, and boating accidents, wrongful death, medical malpractice, and nursing home abuse, while the estate practice includes wills, trusts, probate, will contests, and guardianships. The firm has been recognized by The National Top 100 Trial Lawyers. Every case is handled by an attorney rather than a legal assistant or case manager, and the firm aims to settle matters efficiently while remaining ready for trial. The attorneys, long-time Volusia County residents, offer free consultations at the office or a client's home, including evenings and weekends, and handle injury cases on a contingency fee basis. Bundza & Rodriguez, P.A. is located at 444 Seabreeze Blvd, Suite 750, Daytona Beach, FL 32118 and can be reached by phone at 386-252-5170.
