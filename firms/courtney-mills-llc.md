# Courtney & Mills, LLC

Courtney & Mills, LLC is a Springfield, Missouri law firm focused on family law and divorce, along with business law, wills and trusts, personal injury, and workers' compensation. Partners Chad T. Courtney and Ann Littell Mills handle the full range of Missouri family law matters, including agreed and contested divorce, child custody, child support, spousal support, property division, legal separation, parenting plan modifications, contempt, and mediation, as well as business formation, contracts, and litigation. Chad Courtney has served as Secretary and later Vice-Chair of the Missouri Bar Family Law Section, and Ann Mills earned her law degree with distinction from the University of Nebraska and received the Missouri Bar Association's Lon O. Hocker Award for outstanding young litigators in 2007. Both attorneys are admitted to practice in Missouri state and federal courts and have a long history of litigation in family law and other areas. The firm emphasizes personal service, client education, and practical, solution-oriented representation, recognizing that not every case requires litigation. Courtney & Mills, LLC is located at 725 West Battlefield Rd, Suite A, Springfield, MO 65807 and can be reached by phone at 417-869-9888.
