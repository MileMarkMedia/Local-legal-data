# Cessna & George Law Firm

Cessna & George Law Firm is a London, Kentucky law firm representing injured victims and individuals facing criminal charges throughout Southeast Kentucky, including Laurel, Knox, Whitley, Clay, Jackson, Bell, and Pulaski counties and communities such as Corbin, Manchester, and Somerset. Founding partners Conrad Cessna and Tyler D. George were both born and raised in London and are joined by attorney Chase F. McWhorter. Tyler George focuses on personal injury, along with Social Security Disability, divorce, bankruptcy, and workers' compensation, while Conrad Cessna leads the criminal defense practice and has tried criminal cases ranging from DUI and drug charges to white-collar crimes in Kentucky state and federal courts. The firm handles car, truck, and motorcycle accidents, medical malpractice, nursing home negligence, wrongful death, divorce, and custody cases. Results include a $1.3 million breach of contract judgment, a $1 million truck accident result, and a $950,000 motor vehicle accident settlement, along with a murder acquittal at trial and a double murder charge dismissed before trial. Consultations are free, with contingency fees in appropriate cases. Cessna & George Law Firm is located at 506 S Main St, London, KY 40741 and can be reached by phone at 606-770-5400.
