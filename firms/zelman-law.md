# Zelman Law

Zelman Law is a Tallahassee criminal defense and family law firm serving Leon, Wakulla and Jefferson counties and clients across Florida. Founder and principal attorney Joshua D. Zelman has more than 20 years of experience and is Board Certified by The Florida Bar in Criminal Trial law. He handles cases from misdemeanors in Leon County Court to felony prosecutions in Florida circuit courts and federal matters in the Northern District of Florida, including DUI, drug trafficking and possession, violent offenses, sex offenses, theft, juvenile crimes, probation violations, white collar and federal crimes, and sealing and expungement. The firm's family law practice covers divorce, including contested, uncontested, collaborative and high net worth cases, child custody and parenting plans, child support, alimony, relocation, paternity and property division. Joshua Zelman holds an AV Preeminent rating from Martindale-Hubbell and Avvo Superb 10.0 ratings in criminal defense and DUI, and he is a member of the National College for DUI Defense. The firm offers free consultations and evening or weekend meetings by appointment. Zelman Law is located at 1030 E Lafayette St, Suite 9, Tallahassee, FL 32301 and can be reached by phone at 850-765-5948.
