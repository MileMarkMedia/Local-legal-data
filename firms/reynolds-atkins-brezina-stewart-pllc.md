# Reynolds, Atkins, Brezina, & Stewart, PLLC

Reynolds, Atkins, Brezina, & Stewart, PLLC is a Knoxville, Tennessee law firm focused on business and commercial litigation, along with personal injury, medical malpractice, construction law, real estate, and civil litigation matters. The firm's founders and principal attorneys, Dallas T. Reynolds, III, Michael K. Atkins, E. Michael Brezina, III, and Keith D. Stewart, are long-time Knoxville residents who together offer clients over 100 years of legal experience. Dallas Reynolds has practiced law since 1994 and is an Associate Member of the American Board of Trial Advocates, Michael Atkins has practiced since 1996 and has tried over 100 cases to verdict in state and federal courts, and Keith Stewart has a career of more than 30 years. The firm assists businesses with entity formation, contract drafting and negotiation, and LLC, corporate, and shareholder disputes, and represents individuals in car accident, slip and fall, and nursing home negligence claims. Clients receive strategic counsel and representation in negotiation, litigation, mediation, arbitration, trial, or appeal, with free consultations and contingency fees in appropriate cases. Reynolds, Atkins, Brezina, & Stewart, PLLC is located at 606 W Main St, Suite 225, Knoxville, TN 37902 and can be reached by phone at 865-500-3121.
