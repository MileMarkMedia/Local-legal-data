# Murray & Murray Personal Injury Attorneys

Murray & Murray Personal Injury Attorneys is a family owned, fourth generation law firm in Sandusky, Ohio, representing injured people and families in Erie, Huron, and Ottawa Counties and across Ohio since 1931. Its attorneys include Dennis E. Murray Sr., who has more than 50 years of plaintiff trial experience, Dennis E. Murray Jr., Charles M. Murray, and Margaret M. Murray, who previously worked in the Erie County Prosecutor's Office. The firm handles car, truck, and motorcycle accidents, slip and fall injuries, wrongful death, medical malpractice, nursing home abuse, and brain and spinal cord injuries, along with insurance bad faith, class actions, and commercial litigation. Reported results include a $49 million civil RICO and public corruption recovery, a $34 million bad faith insurance result, and a $30 million toxic tort and environmental pollution result. The firm and attorneys Charles Murray, Dennis Murray Sr., and Margaret Murray were recognized by Best Lawyers in 2026, and the firm has also earned recognition from Super Lawyers and The National Trial Lawyers Top 100. The firm represents only individuals and families, never insurers or corporate defendants. Murray & Murray Personal Injury Attorneys is located at 111 E Shoreline Dr, Sandusky, OH 44870 and can be reached by phone at 419-624-3000.
