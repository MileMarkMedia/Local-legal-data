# Hammad S. Matin, P.A.

The Law Office of Hammad S. Matin, P.A. is a criminal defense and personal injury law firm serving La Plata, Waldorf, and Charles County, Maryland. Founder Hammad S. Matin was admitted to the Maryland bar in 2000, served as a judicial law clerk in Charles County, and worked as an Assistant State's Attorney for Charles County from 2001 to 2007, including two years as a lead prosecutor in juvenile matters. The firm has been practicing in Charles County for 25 years and handles felonies and misdemeanors, DUI and DWI, DMV hearings, drug crimes, assault, domestic violence, robbery, homicide, weapons offenses, juvenile defense, protective and peace orders, expungement, and traffic violations, along with auto accident, truck accident, and slip and fall injury claims. Hammad S. Matin is a past President of the Charles County Bar Association and was selected to the Super Lawyers Rising Stars list in 2013. The firm offers free initial consultations, evening and weekend meetings by appointment, and Spanish-speaking service. The Law Office of Hammad S. Matin, P.A. is located at 204 Washington Ave, Suite 300, La Plata, MD 20646 and can be reached by phone at 301-259-3488.
