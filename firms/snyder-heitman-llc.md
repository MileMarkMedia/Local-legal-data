# Snyder Heitman LLC

Snyder Heitman LLC is a Greenville personal injury law firm serving the Upstate of South Carolina, including Spartanburg and Anderson, and also defends physicians and healthcare professionals in medical malpractice cases. Founding partner Steven A. Snyder has practiced law for almost 40 years, including more than 30 years in medical malpractice defense, and practices alongside attorney Andrew K. Heitman. The firm's attorneys bring over 100 years of combined experience and have obtained favorable results in over 150 jury trials, along with many pre-trial settlements. The firm handles car, truck, motorcycle, pedestrian, and rideshare accidents, premises liability, dog bites, negligent security, product liability, catastrophic injuries such as brain and spinal cord injuries, and wrongful death. Steven Snyder has been recognized by Super Lawyers, Best Lawyers in America, Legal Elite of the Upstate, and TALK Greenville Top Lawyers, and holds an AV Distinguished rating from Martindale-Hubbell. Injury cases are handled on a contingency fee basis, consultations are free, and the firm limits its caseload to keep regular communication with each client. Snyder Heitman LLC is located at 50 Parkins Mill Road, Suite A, Greenville, SC 29607 and can be reached by phone at 864-335-3500.
