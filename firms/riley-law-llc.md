# Riley Law, LLC

Riley Law, LLC is a Bridgeport, Connecticut criminal defense firm led by attorney Michael Riley, who began his legal career inside the Connecticut Judicial Branch and opened his own practice in 2014. The firm represents people accused of crimes in Bridgeport, Fairfield, New Haven, Stamford, and communities throughout Fairfield and New Haven counties, handling DUI, drug and weapons charges, assault, theft, sex crimes, domestic violence, and homicide and other serious felony prosecutions. Michael Riley is admitted in Connecticut, the U.S. District Court for the District of Connecticut, and the Second Circuit Court of Appeals. Results include not guilty verdicts on murder charges in State v. Hamilton, State v. Carlson, and State v. Ramos, a not guilty verdict on five counts of sexual assault in State v. Whittaker, and the dismissal of a conspiracy to commit murder for hire charge in State v. Kevin B. The firm prepares every case for trial while also pursuing negotiated resolutions and diversionary programs when appropriate, and it handles personal injury and civil rights matters, including police brutality claims. Spanish is spoken. Riley Law, LLC is located at 1087 Broad St, Bridgeport, CT 06604 and can be reached by phone at 203-818-1888.
