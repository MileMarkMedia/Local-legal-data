# Mickelsen Dalton LLC

Mickelsen Dalton LLC is a personal injury and wrongful death law firm based in Charleston, South Carolina, with additional offices in Charlotte, North Carolina, and Edmonds, Washington. Founded in January 2019 by attorneys Brian Mickelsen and Danny Dalton, the firm represents victims of negligent security and shootings, catastrophic injury, traumatic brain injury, medical malpractice, nursing home abuse, premises liability, car and truck accidents, sexual assault, human trafficking, and insurance bad faith. Its attorneys have litigated in 10 jurisdictions, including South Carolina, Georgia, Alabama, and Massachusetts. Reported results include a $22 million wrongful death shooting recovery, an $11.25 million result for a shooting at an apartment complex, and a $10 million personal injury shooting recovery. Brian Mickelsen has been recognized by Best Lawyers, and the firm's cases have been covered by The New York Times, The Washington Post, ABC News, and Reuters. The firm serves Charleston, North Charleston, Mount Pleasant, Summerville, and nearby communities, prepares each case for trial from the start, and gives clients direct access to their attorney. Mickelsen Dalton LLC is located at 25 Society St., Charleston, SC 29401 and can be reached by phone at 843-804-0428.
