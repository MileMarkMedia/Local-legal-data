# Law Offices of Kisha M. Hebbon, LLC

The Law Offices of Kisha M. Hebbon, LLC is a family law and divorce firm serving Somerset County and clients throughout central and northern New Jersey, including Middlesex, Hunterdon, Mercer, Morris and Union Counties. Kisha M. Hebbon founded the firm in 2005 and has nearly 25 years of experience. A graduate of Rutgers University and Rutgers School of Law in Newark, she began her career in the Appellate Section of the Bergen County Prosecutor's Office and later served with the U.S. Department of Justice, Federal Bureau of Prisons, as Assistant Regional Counsel for the Northeast Region and as a staff attorney at FCI Fort Dix. The firm handles divorce, mediation, high net worth divorce, child custody and parenting time, child support, alimony, property division, prenuptial agreements and domestic violence, along with personal injury, estate planning, criminal defense and municipal court matters. Hebbon has appeared as a legal commentator on Fox News, CNN, HLN and MSNBC, and she has received the Martindale-Hubbell Client Champion Platinum recognition. The firm offers flexible scheduling and home or hospital visits when needed. The Law Offices of Kisha M. Hebbon, LLC is located at 19 Clyde Rd, Suite 202, Somerset, NJ 08873 and can be reached by phone at 732-873-6464.
