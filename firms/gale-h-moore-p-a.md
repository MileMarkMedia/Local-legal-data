# Gale H. Moore, P.A.

Gale H. Moore, P.A. is a family law firm in Largo serving clients in Clearwater, St. Petersburg, and throughout Pinellas County. Attorney Gale H. Moore has practiced family law exclusively for more than 30 years and is Board Certified in Marital and Family Law by the Florida Bar. She served as an Assistant Public Defender in the Sixth Judicial Circuit from 1993 to 1998 before entering private practice, and has been certified by the Florida Supreme Court as a family law mediator since 2009. The firm handles divorce, alimony, child custody and parenting plans, child support and enforcement, modifications, paternity, and property division, along with high-asset, medical professional, and military divorces, including for service members at MacDill Air Force Base and Coast Guard Air Station Clearwater. Ms. Moore has been a Florida Super Lawyers honoree since 2020, holds an Avvo Rating of 10, and received the Canakaris Inn of Court John C. Lenderman Award for Excellence in Family Law in 2015. The firm emphasizes mediation and collaborative divorce as private, non-adversarial options, while taking high-conflict and complex property cases to trial when necessary. Gale H. Moore, P.A. is located at 801 West Bay Drive, Suite 509, Largo, FL 33770 and can be reached by phone at 727-584-2528.
