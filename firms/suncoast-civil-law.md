# Suncoast Civil Law

Suncoast Civil Law is a Sarasota law firm handling business and civil litigation, wills and probate, foreclosure defense, and business transactions for individuals, families, and small businesses across Sarasota County, Bradenton, and Florida's Central Gulf Coast. Owner and principal attorney Jerry Sanchy has 40 years of experience in real estate, probate administration, estate tax planning, and civil litigation, and earned his law degree cum laude from the University of Connecticut School of Law. He practices with attorney Karla Lopez, and the firm has served the Sarasota area for over 20 years with more than 50 years of combined attorney experience. The firm represents plaintiffs and defendants in breach of contract, business torts, consumer fraud and consumer protection, homeowners' association, landlord and tenant, probate, and real estate disputes. Foreclosure defense results include a short payoff of $1,900,000 on a $2,516,000 loan balance and a loan modification that reduced a balance from $317,934 to $185,000. Jerry Sanchy has been featured on ABC 7 WWSB Suncoast. Consultations are free, and the firm offers contingency, flat fee, and hourly arrangements. Suncoast Civil Law is located at 6981 Curtiss Avenue #3, Sarasota, FL 34231 and can be reached by phone at 941-366-1800.
