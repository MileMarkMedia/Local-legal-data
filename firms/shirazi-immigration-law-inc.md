# Shirazi Immigration Law, Inc.

Shirazi Immigration Law, Inc. is an Atlanta immigration firm serving clients throughout Georgia, with additional offices in Moultrie, Dalton, and Chattanooga, Tennessee. Owner and attorney Amna Shirazi leads a team with more than 20 years of immigration experience. She earned her JD from Georgia State University College of Law and an LLM from Emory University School of Law, and has been a member of the American Immigration Lawyers Association since 2001. The firm handles family immigration, deportation defense, asylum and refugee cases, consular cases and waivers, citizenship and naturalization, employment-based immigration, non-immigrant visas, Temporary Protected Status, immigration for crime victims, crimmigration, and BIA and federal court litigation. Amna Shirazi has been recognized by Super Lawyers for over a decade and by The National Trial Lawyers Top 40 Under 40 and Best Lawyers, and holds a Martindale-Hubbell AV Preeminent rating. Of counsel attorneys include Elizabeth Garvish and Aimee Clark. The firm offers services in multiple languages and explains each step in plain language. Shirazi Immigration Law, Inc. is located at 303 Peachtree St NE, Suite 4100, Atlanta, GA 30308 and can be reached by phone at 404-523-3611.
