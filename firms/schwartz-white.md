# Schwartz \| White

Schwartz \| White is a Boca Raton law firm focused on divorce and family law, serving clients throughout Palm Beach County, with additional work in elder law and probate litigation. The firm is led by partner Philip L. Schwartz, who earned his law degree from Vermont Law School and was admitted to The Florida Bar and the U.S. District Court for the Southern District of Florida in 1989, with Gary L. White serving as Of Counsel. The firm handles contested, uncontested, and collaborative divorce, high net worth and complex divorce, alimony, child custody, child support, paternity, relocation, domestic violence, property division, prenuptial and postnuptial agreements, and post-judgment modifications and enforcement. Philip L. Schwartz holds an AV Preeminent rating from Martindale-Hubbell and an Avvo Rating of 10.0 Superb, and is a member of the Palm Beach County Bar Association and South Palm Beach County Bar Association. The firm has also been recognized by Expertise.com. Clients are encouraged to take part in strategy and preparation, and the firm draws on negotiation, mediation, and trial experience to build a plan for each case. Schwartz \| White is located at 2000 Glades Road, Suite 208, Boca Raton, FL 33431 and can be reached by phone at 561-391-9943.
