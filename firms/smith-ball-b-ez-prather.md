# Smith, Ball, Báez & Prather

Smith, Ball, Báez & Prather is a Florida law firm devoted entirely to personal injury law, with offices in Palm Beach Gardens, Port St. Lucie and Boca Raton and clients throughout Palm Beach County, the Treasure Coast, and Naples and Fort Myers. Its trial attorneys, Scott B. Smith, Marci Ball Elordi, Nelson E. Báez and David C. Prather, bring more than 65 years of combined experience and have recovered over $100 million for injured clients, including a $6.5 million truck accident wrongful death settlement and a $5 million medical malpractice verdict. The firm handles car, truck, motorcycle and boating accidents, medical malpractice, premises liability, negligent security, product liability, dog bites, work injuries, catastrophic injuries and wrongful death. Its honors include a Martindale-Hubbell AV Preeminent rating, The Best Lawyers in America, U.S. News Best Law Firms and the Multi-Million Dollar Advocates Forum. Clients work directly with their attorney, consultations are free, and the firm speaks Spanish. Smith, Ball, Báez & Prather is located at 4400 PGA Blvd, Suite 800, Palm Beach Gardens, FL 33410 and can be reached by phone at 561-500-4357.
