# Arnoux Sharma Standeford, LLC

Arnoux Sharma Standeford, LLC is a Chicago family law firm focused on divorce and related matters, including property division, maintenance, child custody, prenuptial agreements, and high-net-worth divorce. Partner Matthew C. Arnoux, admitted to the Illinois bar in 1984, has nearly 40 years of experience in Illinois family law, handles appellate work, and is certified by the Cook County courts to represent children in family law disputes. Partner Molshree "Molly" A. Sharma, licensed since 2004, concentrates on complex financial issues, international custody disputes, and Hague Convention cases, lectures at international family law conferences, and speaks Hindi and Urdu. Partner Jonathan R. Standeford handles child abduction cases, business valuations, and asset division. The firm's attorneys have been recognized by Super Lawyers, Best Lawyers and Leading Lawyers and hold an Avvo Rating of 10.0, and Molly Sharma was named to Crain's Notable Women in Law 2024. The firm handles mediation, collaborative practice, litigation, and appeals throughout Chicago and Cook, DuPage, Lake, Will, and Kane counties. Arnoux Sharma Standeford, LLC is located at 200 N LaSalle St, Suite 2310, Chicago, IL 60601 and can be reached by phone at 312-863-2800.
