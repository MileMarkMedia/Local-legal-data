# Scott Law Team

Scott Law Team is a Florida employment law firm with offices in Jupiter, West Palm Beach, and Jacksonville, representing both employees and employers throughout the state. Founder and managing partner Cathleen Scott has led the firm for 25 years. She was admitted to The Florida Bar in 1998, has been Board Certified by the Florida Supreme Court in Labor and Employment Law since 2006, and is a certified mediator. Gabe Roberts serves as the firm's Jacksonville partner. For employees, the firm handles discrimination, harassment, retaliation, wrongful termination, unpaid wages, whistleblower claims, and non-compete matters. For employers, it handles EEOC charges, employment contracts, handbooks, workplace investigations, and training, along with healthcare law and employment mediation. Cathleen Scott has been recognized by Super Lawyers, holds an AV Preeminent rating from Martindale-Hubbell, and has chaired the Executive Committee of The Florida Bar Labor and Employment Law Section. The firm litigates in Florida's federal district courts and handles appeals before the Fourth District Court of Appeal and the Eleventh Circuit. Scott Law Team is located at 250 South Central Blvd, Suite 205, Jupiter, FL 33458 and can be reached by phone at 561-653-0008.
