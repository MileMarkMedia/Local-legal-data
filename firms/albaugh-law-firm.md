# Albaugh Law Firm

Albaugh Law Firm is a St. Augustine and Jacksonville law firm focused on bankruptcy and debt relief, criminal defense, family law, and personal injury, serving clients across Northeast and Central Florida. The firm was founded by Ryan M. Albaugh, a former criminal prosecutor admitted to the California Bar in 1999 and the Florida Bar in 2005, who has tried more than 50 jury trials and has been named among the Top 100 Trial Lawyers. Attorney Thomas F. Walker joined the firm in 2015, and together the attorneys bring more than 70 years of combined experience. The firm handles Chapter 7 and Chapter 13 bankruptcy, foreclosure defense, and debt settlement in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the Middle District of Florida, along with DUI, drug, theft, and probation violation defense and divorce, custody, and support matters in the Seventh and Fourth Judicial Circuits. Reported results include a foreclosure sale stopped hours before auction through Chapter 13, a Jacksonville DUI reduced to reckless driving, and over $50,000 in debt discharged in Chapter 7. The firm is small by design, and the attorney a client meets at consultation handles the case. Albaugh Law Firm is located at 1301 Plantation Island Dr. S., Suite 302B, Saint Augustine, FL 32080 and can be reached by phone at 904-471-3434.
