# Lunsford, Baskin & Priebe PLLC

Lunsford, Baskin & Priebe PLLC is a workers' compensation law firm founded in 2013 that represents injured workers throughout Mississippi and Louisiana. Most lawyers who take comp cases are personal injury attorneys handling them on the side. This firm is different because workers' compensation is the center of its practice. Its attorneys bring over 75 years of combined experience and have recovered more than $100 million in settlements for their clients. Founding partner Rob Lunsford has represented injured workers for his entire career. Partners Brad Baskin and Jennifer Priebe have been recognized by Best Lawyers, Super Lawyers, and The National Trial Lawyers. The firm takes on denied and terminated claims involving back and spine injuries, fractures, shoulder and knee injuries, and the occupational conditions firefighters and first responders face. Consultations are free, the firm advances case costs, and it is paid only if it recovers benefits. Spanish is spoken. Lunsford, Baskin & Priebe is located at 317 E Capitol St, Suite 600, Jackson, MS 39201 and 5333 Magazine St, New Orleans, LA 70115, and can be reached by phone at 601-488-3975 or 504-788-2994.
