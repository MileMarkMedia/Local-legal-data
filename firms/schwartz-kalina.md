# Schwartz Kalina

Schwartz Kalina, PLLC is a Leesburg law firm serving clients throughout Loudoun County, Fairfax County, and Northern Virginia. Led by attorneys Peter J. Schwartz and Kristen M. Kalina, the firm focuses on family law and divorce, including child custody, child support, spousal support, equitable distribution, and mediation, while also handling criminal and traffic defense, personal injury, and legal malpractice and fee disputes. Kristen Kalina is a Virginia Supreme Court certified mediator. The firm brings more than 35 years of combined litigation experience to negotiations, mediation, and courtroom representation, working to protect client rights and interests across all aspects of family law matters. Schwartz Kalina is located at 118 Edwards Ferry Rd NE, Suite 100, Leesburg, VA 20176 and can be reached by phone at 703-667-0954.
