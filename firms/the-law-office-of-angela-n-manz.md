# The Law Office of Angela N. Manz

The Law Office of Angela N. Manz is an estate planning and elder law firm in Virginia Beach, Virginia, serving clients in Virginia Beach, Chesapeake, Norfolk, Portsmouth, Suffolk, Hampton and Newport News. Founding attorney Angela N. Manz has practiced for over a decade and focuses on estate planning and on helping families obtain Medicaid and VA benefits for long-term care. She holds the Virginia Law Foundation's Qualified Master Counselor designation in Trusts and Estates Practice, which requires a three-year course of study and which, at the firm's last count, only seven attorneys in the Commonwealth held. She served on the Virginia CLE faculty in 2015 and is a member of the National Academy of Elder Law Attorneys, Elder Counsel and WealthCounsel. The firm handles wills, revocable and irrevocable trusts, powers of attorney, health care directives, asset protection, Medicaid crisis planning, care management, probate and trust administration, guardianships and conservatorships, and veterans benefits. It works with military families, blended families and LGBTQ+ families, and takes a team approach in which every member of the firm gets to know each client. The Law Office of Angela N. Manz is located at 500 Viking Drive, Suite 103, Virginia Beach, VA 23452 and can be reached by phone at 757-271-6275.
