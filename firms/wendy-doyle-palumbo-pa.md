# Wendy Doyle-Palumbo, PA

Wendy Doyle-Palumbo, PA is a personal injury law firm serving Pasco County, including Hudson, New Port Richey, Holiday, Port Richey, Trinity, Land O' Lakes, Spring Hill, Wesley Chapel, Zephyrhills and Dade City, as well as Hernando and Citrus counties and the greater Tampa Bay region. Attorney Wendy Doyle-Palumbo began her career as a paralegal before serving as an Assistant Attorney General and a Guardian Ad Litem Attorney, and she brings more than 30 years of litigation experience to the firm. The firm handles car, motorcycle, truck and rideshare accidents, pedestrian and bicycle accidents, slip and fall injuries, wrongful death, spinal cord and traumatic brain injuries, PIP disputes, insurance bad faith and negligent security claims, and it also handles family law matters such as divorce, child custody, child support and alimony. Every case is prepared as though it will go before a judge and jury, which gives clients leverage in negotiations with insurance companies. Injury cases are handled on a contingency fee basis with a free consultation, and the firm has offices in Hudson and New Port Richey. Wendy Doyle-Palumbo, PA is located at 14309 Old Dixie Hwy, Hudson, FL 34667 and can be reached by phone at 727-233-2134.
