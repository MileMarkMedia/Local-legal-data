# Shiver Hamilton Campbell

Shiver Hamilton Campbell is an Atlanta personal injury law firm focused on truck accidents, car accidents, catastrophic injury, and wrongful death, with additional offices in Savannah and St. Simons Island. Jeff Shiver and Alan Hamilton founded the firm in 2011, Scott Campbell joined in 2013, and the firm took its current name in 2022. Partners also include Kyle Wallace and Marla Eastwood. The firm has recovered over $500 million for clients through verdicts and settlements, including a $162,000,000 auto accident wrongful death settlement, a $140,000,000 premises liability wrongful death jury verdict, and a $17,716,401 automobile product liability verdict. Its practice also covers motorcycle, bicycle, and pedestrian accidents, negligent security, premises liability, burn injuries, and traumatic brain injury across Metro Atlanta, including Fulton, DeKalb, Cobb, Gwinnett, and Forsyth counties. Attorneys have been recognized by Super Lawyers, including Top 100 honors, The National Top 100 Trial Lawyers, and Georgia Trend Legal Elite. Cases are handled on a contingency basis, and the firm prepares cases for trial while settling when it benefits the client. Shiver Hamilton Campbell is located at 3490 Piedmont Rd NE, Suite 640, Atlanta, GA 30305 and can be reached by phone at 404-593-0020.
