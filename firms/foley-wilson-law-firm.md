# Foley & Wilson Law Firm

Foley & Wilson Law Firm is a boutique federal and state criminal defense practice with offices in Fort Myers and Naples, serving clients in Fort Myers, Naples, Cape Coral, Port Charlotte, Punta Gorda, and the surrounding Southwest Florida region. The firm is led by Robert Foley and Desiree Wilson, and its attorneys bring over 45 years of combined legal experience. Robert Foley, who serves as Of Counsel, spent 20 years as an FBI Special Agent, including as Special Agent in Charge of the Detroit Field Office, and is a former state prosecutor. Desiree Wilson is a former Assistant United States Attorney for the District of Wyoming and former Senior Assistant Appellate Counsel with the Wyoming Office of the Public Defender. The firm handles drug crimes, sex crimes, domestic violence, DUI, gun violations, violent crimes, white collar offenses, and record sealing, along with federal fraud, conspiracy, cyber crime, and drug trafficking cases. It stresses early intervention, including during investigations before charges are filed, and prepares every case as if it will go to trial. Services are available in English and Spanish. Foley & Wilson Law Firm is located at 12481 Brantley Commons Ct., Fort Myers, FL 33907 and can be reached by phone at 239-690-6080.
