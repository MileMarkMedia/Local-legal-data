# Bunde & Roberts, P.C.

Bunde & Roberts, P.C. is a boutique family law practice serving Pittsburgh and Western Pennsylvania, including Butler, Greensburg, and Washington. The firm focuses exclusively on divorce and domestic relations, handling high asset and high conflict divorce, property distribution and business valuation, closely held family businesses, alimony, child custody and relocation, child and spousal support, prenuptial and postnuptial agreements, protection from abuse actions, adoptions, arbitration, and appeals. Founding shareholder Robb D. Bunde and attorney Reid B. Roberts started the firm after years of practicing family law with larger firms, and the team brings more than 100 years of combined legal experience. Robb Bunde serves as co-chair of the Pennsylvania Family Law Section Arbitration Committee. Recognition includes Super Lawyers, an AV Preeminent rating, Best Law Firms, Best Lawyers, and American Academy of Matrimonial Lawyers membership. The attorneys provide sensitive and personalized representation for clients of all income levels and family types, negotiating settlements and litigating in court when needed, and the firm offers services in Spanish. Bunde & Roberts, P.C. is located at 223 4th Avenue, Suite 500, Pittsburgh, PA 15222 and can be reached by phone at 412-391-4330.
