# Farrell Disability Law

Farrell Disability Law is a Jacksonville firm focused on Social Security Disability, long-term disability insurance, and ERISA claims, serving clients throughout Florida and southern Georgia, with an additional office in Orlando. Founder and principal attorney Thomas M. Farrell, IV earned his journalism degree magna cum laude and his law degree from the University of Florida and has been a member of The Florida Bar since 1993. With 20 years of experience advocating on disability law issues, he represents people who have been denied SSDI, employer-provided and occupational long-term disability benefits, AD&D and life insurance claims, and Florida Retirement System regular and line of duty disability benefits. He has chaired the Social Security Disability Section of the Jacksonville Bar Association. The firm's recognitions include Florida Super Lawyers, an AV Preeminent rating from Martindale-Hubbell, an Avvo Rating of 10, and the Expertise.com Best of 2024 award. Mr. Farrell has been interviewed by News 6 in Orlando about disability hearing wait times. Initial consultations are free and cases are handled on a contingency basis. Farrell Disability Law is located at 4651 Salisbury Rd, Suite 400, Jacksonville, FL 32256 and can be reached by phone at 904-388-8870.
