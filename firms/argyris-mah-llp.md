# Argyris Mah, LLP

Argyris Mah, LLP is a San Jose family law firm founded in 2015 by partners Arlette Argyris and Andrea Mah, both Certified Family Law Specialists certified by the State Bar of California Board of Legal Specialization. The firm practices family law exclusively, serving clients in Santa Clara County along with Alameda and San Mateo counties, including Campbell, Cupertino, Los Gatos, Palo Alto, and Sunnyvale. Arlette Argyris has practiced only family law since earning her law degree from the University of Oklahoma College of Law in 2010 and has been named a Super Lawyers Rising Star. Andrea Mah, a University of San Francisco School of Law graduate, spent more than five years as a senior associate at a San Jose firm before co-founding the practice. Together they bring more than 20 years of collective experience to divorce, legal separation, high-asset divorce, property and retirement division, child custody and visitation, move-away cases, child and spousal support, paternity, domestic violence restraining orders, and prenuptial and postnuptial agreements. The firm emphasizes mediation, keeping matters out of court when possible and litigating when necessary. Argyris Mah, LLP is located at 1065 Asbury Street, San Jose, CA 95126 and can be reached by phone at 408-214-6366.
