# Florin Gray

Florin Gray is a Tampa Bay law firm representing injured people, employees and whistleblowers throughout Florida, with offices in Tampa and Lutz and appointment offices in Orlando and Tallahassee. Led by Wolfgang Florin, Chris Gray and co-founder Miguel Bouzas, the firm brings more than 100 years of combined legal experience to car, truck, motorcycle and boating accidents, premises liability, nursing home abuse and neglect, catastrophic injuries and wrongful death, as well as employment discrimination, harassment, retaliation, wrongful termination, unpaid wages and overtime, and False Claims Act and qui tam whistleblower cases. Its results include a $7.5 million personal injury settlement, a $6.8 million race discrimination settlement, a $6 million False Claims Act settlement and a $4.5 million disability and age discrimination jury verdict. The firm's attorneys are recognized by Super Lawyers and the Million Dollar Advocates Forum, handle their cases personally, and serve clients in English and Spanish. Consultations are free and cases are handled on a contingency fee basis. Florin Gray is located at 777 S Harbour Island Blvd, Suite 128, Tampa, FL 33602 and can be reached by phone at 727-220-4000.
