# Donna Hung Law Group

Donna Hung Law Group is an Orlando family law firm led by owner and attorney Donna C. Hung, serving clients throughout Orange, Osceola and Seminole Counties and nearby Central Florida communities. Attorney Hung was admitted to The Florida Bar in 2006 and is a Supreme Court Certified Family Law Mediator and collaboratively trained attorney. She began her career as an Assistant State Attorney in the Ninth Judicial Circuit, where she handled over a thousand criminal cases and served as a special prosecutor for domestic violence. The firm handles contested, uncontested and high-asset divorce, time-sharing and parenting plans, child support, alimony, property division, prenuptial agreements, paternity, modifications, and domestic violence injunctions. Attorney Hung has been AV Preeminent rated by Martindale-Hubbell since 2015, was named a Florida Rising Star and later a Super Lawyer, and has been recognized in The Best Lawyers in America for Family Law and by Florida Legal Elite. Most of the firm's cases resolve through mediation or settlement, and services are available in Spanish. Donna Hung Law Group is located at 605 East Robinson St., Suite 730, Orlando, FL 32801 and can be reached by phone at 407-999-0099.
