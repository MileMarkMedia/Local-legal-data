# Jami L. Huber, P.A.

Jami L. Huber, P.A. is a West Palm Beach law firm focused on probate, trust, and guardianship matters for clients throughout Palm Beach, Broward, and Martin Counties. Founder and principal attorney Jami Huber established the firm in 2013 after practicing at large national firms and boutique practices, and she has been a practicing attorney for more than 25 years. A graduate of Emory University and the University of Georgia School of Law, she handles probate and trust administration, contested probate and trust litigation including will contests and trustee objections, and voluntary and contested guardianships for minors and adults, representing personal representatives, trustees, heirs, and beneficiaries. She is a member of The Florida Bar's Real Property, Probate and Trust Law Section and its Probate Litigation Committee, and previously co-chaired the Elder Law Committee of the South Palm Beach County Bar Association. Jami Huber has been recognized by Super Lawyers, and many of the firm's clients are referred by other estate planning, probate, and trust attorneys. The firm takes a resolution-oriented approach through negotiation, mediation, or litigation. Jami L. Huber, P.A. is located at 500 South Australian Avenue, Suite 500, West Palm Beach, FL 33401 and can be reached by phone at 561-283-4770.
