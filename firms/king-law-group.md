# King Law Group

King Law Group is a criminal defense law firm serving Clearwater, New Port Richey, and the greater Tampa Bay area, including Pinellas and Pasco counties. Founder Cameron King is a former felony prosecutor for Florida's Sixth Judicial Circuit, where he handled thousands of cases ranging from murder and manslaughter to armed robbery, drug possession, domestic violence, and DUI. After leaving the State Attorney's Office, he practiced criminal defense before founding King Law Group. A Tampa Bay native, he earned his undergraduate business degree and law degree from Florida State University. The firm handles DUI, drug crimes, theft, violent crimes, sex crimes, domestic violence, juvenile offenses, white collar crimes, weapons charges, and vehicular homicide, along with bail and bond hearings, probation violations, and record sealing and expungement. Cameron King has received the Avvo Clients' Choice Award. The firm has offices in Clearwater and New Port Richey, offers a free first consultation, and answers its phone 24/7. King Law Group is located at 13575 58th St N, Suite 285, Clearwater, FL 33760 and can be reached by phone at 727-538-4265.
