# WiseLieberman, PLLC

WiseLieberman, PLLC is a Boca Raton law firm that focuses exclusively on divorce and family law, serving clients throughout Palm Beach and Broward counties. Attorneys Todd A. Wise and Andrew S. Lieberman, who is Florida Board Certified in Marital and Family Law, bring 16 years of combined experience in family law practice. The firm handles divorce, alimony, child custody, child support, property division, modifications, paternity, domestic violence, mediation and collaborative law. Todd A. Wise has been selected to Super Lawyers and named a Rising Star, Andrew S. Lieberman has been selected to Super Lawyers, and the firm's attorneys hold an AV Preeminent rating from Martindale-Hubbell. The firm aims to take the anxiety out of the process by giving clients a full understanding of their case and control over its direction, with accessible attorneys and an efficient, affordable approach. In addition to its Boca Raton office, the firm meets clients by appointment at an office in Plantation. WiseLieberman, PLLC is located at 1800 NW Corporate Blvd., Suite 310, Boca Raton, FL 33431 and can be reached by phone at 561-488-7788.
