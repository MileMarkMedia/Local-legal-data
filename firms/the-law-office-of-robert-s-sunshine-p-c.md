# The Law Office of Robert S. Sunshine, P.C.

The Law Office of Robert S. Sunshine, P.C. is a boutique family law firm in Pearl River, New York, serving Rockland, Westchester and Orange counties and the Lower and Mid-Hudson Valley, including New City, Nanuet, Newburgh and White Plains. Robert S. Sunshine founded the firm in 2002 after serving as a principal at the New York City litigation firm Jones Hirsch Connors & Bull, P.C., and earlier as an Assistant Corporation Counsel for the City of New York. A Fordham University School of Law graduate with more than 25 years of legal experience, he handles divorce, including high-asset and complex cases, marital asset distribution, spousal support, child custody and support, modifications, enforcement, appeals, prenuptial agreements, domestic violence and paternity, and serves as Guardian Ad Litem when appointed by the courts. He is a Founding Member of the American Academy of Certified Financial Litigators and lectures to other attorneys on family law. He was named to the Nation's Top One Percent by the National Association of Distinguished Counsel in 2015. The firm offers mediation, negotiation, litigation and unbundled representation. The Law Office of Robert S. Sunshine, P.C. is located at 1 Blue Hill Plaza, Tenth Floor, Pearl River, NY 10965 and can be reached by phone at 845-735-1300.
