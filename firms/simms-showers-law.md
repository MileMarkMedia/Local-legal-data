# Simms Showers Law

Simms Showers Law is a Leesburg, Virginia law firm handling criminal defense and a broad civil practice for individuals, businesses, churches, and nonprofits across Virginia and Maryland. H. Robert Showers formed the firm in 2002, and it operates as a partnership between principal attorneys H. Robert Showers in Leesburg and J. Stephen Simms in Baltimore. Robert Showers is a former federal prosecutor who founded the Department of Justice Child Exploitation and Obscenity Section, and J. Stephen Simms is a former Department of Justice Antitrust Division trial attorney. Partners include Caleb A. Kershner, William B. Mann VI, Kyle D. Winey, and William R. Thetford Jr. The firm brings over 140 years of combined legal experience and has handled over 100 jury trials. Practice areas include DUI, reckless driving, drug, and federal charges, civil and commercial litigation, business law, employment law, estate planning, family law, intellectual property, personal injury, and church and nonprofit law. Offices are located in Leesburg, Winchester, Manassas, Fairfax, Williamsburg, Baltimore, and Hunt Valley, and the team speaks Spanish. Simms Showers Law is located at 305 Harrison Street SE, Third Floor, Leesburg, VA 20175 and can be reached by phone at 703-879-1364.
