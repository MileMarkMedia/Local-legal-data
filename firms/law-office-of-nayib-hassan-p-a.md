# Law Office of Nayib Hassan, P.A.

The Law Office of Nayib Hassan, P.A. is a criminal defense firm in Miami Lakes, Florida, defending clients in state courts across Florida and in federal courts nationwide. Attorney Nayib Hassan began his career as an Assistant State Attorney in Broward County, where he handled thousands of cases and tried hundreds to verdict, and he now brings nearly two decades of experience and that prosecutor's perspective to the defense side. The firm focuses on federal criminal defense and white collar crime, including healthcare, Medicare, PPP, mail, wire, and tax fraud, along with federal and state drug trafficking, firearm offenses, violent crimes, and DUI. Hassan is admitted in the Southern, Middle, and Northern Districts of Florida, the District of Columbia federal court, and the Eleventh Circuit, and his cases have drawn coverage from CNN, The New York Times, and the Miami Herald. He has been named to The National Trial Lawyers Top 100, holds a 10.0 Avvo rating, serves on the Miami board of the Florida Association of Criminal Defense Lawyers, and is fluent in Spanish. The firm intentionally limits its caseload so each client gets personal attention. The Law Office of Nayib Hassan, P.A. is located at 6175 NW 153 St, Suite 209, Miami Lakes, FL 33014 and can be reached by phone at 305-403-7323.
