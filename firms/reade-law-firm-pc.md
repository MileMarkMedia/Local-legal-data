# Reade Law Firm, PC

Reade Law Firm, PC is a family law practice in Peabody serving individuals and families throughout Essex and Middlesex County, Massachusetts, including Danvers, Lynn, Lowell, and Woburn. The firm is led by managing attorney Teresa Reade and senior attorney Richard Reade, who litigated in several areas of law for many years before focusing on family law. Attorney Kate Feeney brings twenty-seven years of experience in Massachusetts domestic relations matters. The firm handles contested and uncontested divorce, high-asset and business owner divorce, child custody and support, alimony, property division, prenuptial and postnuptial agreements, paternity, modifications, mediation, and collaborative divorce. Teresa Reade is rated by Super Lawyers, the firm is Lead Counsel verified, and its affiliations include the Massachusetts Council on Family Mediation, the Academy of Professional Family Mediators, and the International Academy of Collaborative Professionals. The firm offers mediation and collaborative processes while remaining prepared to take matters to court or trial. Reade Law Firm, PC is located at 3 Essex Green Dr, Suite 2, Peabody, MA 01960 and can be reached by phone at 978-767-8383.
