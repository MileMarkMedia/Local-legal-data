# Sluka Law PLC

Sluka Law PLC is a Woodstock law firm focused exclusively on workers' compensation, representing injured employees throughout Vermont, from Burlington and St. Albans to Rutland, Brattleboro, and Bennington. Attorney Justin Sluka began his workers' compensation practice in 2005 and brings nearly 20 years of experience, including more than 12 years defending employers and insurance companies before turning to the representation of injured workers. He has tried numerous cases before the Vermont Department of Labor, has litigated before the Vermont Supreme Court, and has brought claims against employers including UPS, Lowe's, Walmart, Dollar Tree Stores, and the State of Vermont. He is an approved workers' compensation mediator, a member of the Vermont Association for Justice, and was named a Super Lawyers New England Rising Star in Workers' Compensation in 2015. The firm handles benefits claims, denied claims, occupational disease, PTSD and mental injury, and death benefits for workers in healthcare, manufacturing, construction, delivery, agriculture, and hospitality. Consultations are free and can take place by phone or video, and clients pay no fee unless there is a recovery. Sluka Law PLC is located at 13 Elm St, 2nd Floor, Woodstock, VT 05091 and can be reached by phone at 802-457-1000.
