# Greater Orlando Family Law

Greater Orlando Family Law is a family law and divorce firm serving Orlando, Winter Park, and Central Florida communities across Orange, Seminole, Osceola, and Lake counties. Its attorneys include Michael J. Vaghaiwalla, who is board certified in Marital and Family Law by The Florida Bar, a distinction held by fewer than seven percent of Florida lawyers, and Lorna Truett, who was recognized by Best Lawyers for 2025. The firm handles contested, uncontested, collaborative, high net worth, military, same-sex, and gray divorce, along with child custody and timesharing, child support, alimony, equitable distribution, paternity, relocation, prenuptial agreements, domestic violence injunctions, and family appeals. Recognition for the firm includes Best Lawyers, Super Lawyers, an Avvo Rating of 10.0 Superb, and Florida Legal Elite. The firm offers 30-minute complimentary consultations, same-day and after-hours appointments, an attorney assigned to each client, open discussion of fees, and services in English, Spanish, and Mandarin, with mediation as the usual path for contested cases. Greater Orlando Family Law is located at 631 South Orlando Avenue, Suite 301, Winter Park, FL 32789 and can be reached by phone at 407-377-6399.
