# Hillen Druek, PLLC

Hillen Druek, PLLC is a boutique matrimonial and family law firm serving Suffolk County, Nassau County, and all of Long Island, with offices in Islandia, Westhampton Beach, and Carle Place. The practice began in 2010 as part of Vlahadamis & Hillen, LLC, where Founding Partner Evans M. Hillen led the matrimonial and family law department before founding Hillen Law, PLLC. The firm took the Hillen Druek name in 2025 when Managing Partner Brandon M. Druek joined, and Of Counsel Catherine C. DeSanto adds nearly three decades of legal experience. Together the attorneys bring more than thirty years of combined experience to contested and uncontested divorce, child custody, child support, paternity and parental rights, grandparents' rights, prenuptial and postnuptial agreements, modification and enforcement, and QDROs, with particular experience in high-asset divorce and the division of retirement accounts, including LIRR and MTA pensions. The firm explains clients' rights in plain language, sets realistic expectations, and pursues negotiated resolutions while staying prepared for court. Hillen Druek, PLLC is located at 1455 Veterans Hwy, Suite 104, Islandia, NY 11749 and can be reached by phone at 631-998-0303.
