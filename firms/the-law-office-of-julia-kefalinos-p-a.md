# The Law Office of Julia Kefalinos P.A.

The Law Office of Julia Kefalinos P.A. is a Miami firm focused on bankruptcy and criminal defense, serving clients throughout Miami-Dade and Broward counties. Attorney Julia Kefalinos founded the firm in 2006 after 16 years as a Miami-Dade County Assistant Public Defender, where she defended thousands of clients in felony, traffic, juvenile delinquency and domestic violence cases. In bankruptcy, she represents individuals and businesses in Chapter 7, Chapter 11 and Chapter 13 cases, debt relief and student loan repayment matters, helping clients stop lawsuits, creditor harassment, foreclosure and wage garnishment. In criminal matters, she defends clients facing federal and state charges, DUI, domestic violence, drug crimes, juvenile and traffic offenses, and helps clients seal or expunge arrests. She has been interviewed by CBS 4 about a high-profile DUI arrest. Julia Kefalinos is a member of the National Association of Consumer Bankruptcy Attorneys and the Florida Association of Criminal Defense Lawyers, and the firm serves clients in Spanish, Greek, German and French. Clients receive her cell phone number and email and work with her directly throughout their case. The Law Office of Julia Kefalinos P.A. is located at 2121 SW 3rd Ave., Suite 600, Miami, FL 33129 and can be reached by phone at 305-856-2713.
