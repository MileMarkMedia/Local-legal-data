# My Florida Deed

My Florida Deed, a division of DeBoard Law, PLLC, is an Orlando law practice focused on Florida real estate deed preparation and recording for property owners throughout the state. The firm prepares warranty deeds, quit claim deeds, Lady Bird deeds, and corrective deeds, and no deed is delivered or recorded until it has been reviewed by a Florida real estate attorney. Clients complete a short online form with the property address, county, and new owner details, sign the deed before a notary, and return it to the firm for recording. Work is billed as a flat fee that includes recording, with documentary stamp tax paid separately to the Florida Department of Revenue, and each deed is backed by a guarantee of legal sufficiency. The firm's founder brings decades of real estate experience, including more than 20 years as a licensed real estate broker, ownership of a title company, a mortgage company, and a real estate brokerage, and more than a decade practicing real estate law. Legal representation is also available for title insurance, real estate closings, escrow and contract disputes, and partition actions. My Florida Deed is located at 529 N. Fern Creek Ave., Suite A, Orlando, FL 32803 and can be reached by phone at 407-205-2906.
