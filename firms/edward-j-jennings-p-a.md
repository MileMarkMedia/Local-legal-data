# Edward J. Jennings, P.A.

Edward J. Jennings, P.A. is a Fort Lauderdale law firm focused on probate litigation, serving clients throughout Broward County. Founder Edward J. Jennings has practiced law for more than 30 years. He began his career as an associate at a firm representing the Broward Sheriff's Office and local police agencies, and for more than twenty years he served as a court-appointed volunteer Guardian Ad Litem protecting children in Broward County. The firm's probate practice covers will contests, beneficiary disputes, breach of fiduciary duty claims, trustee and personal representative disputes, contested guardianships, and notice of proceedings issues that arise during estate administration. It also handles residential and commercial real estate closings, foreclosure defense, quiet title and landlord and tenant disputes, business litigation involving contracts and business torts, and family law matters including divorce, custody, support and adoption. The firm aims to resolve disputes through negotiation when possible while remaining prepared to take cases to trial. Edward J. Jennings, P.A. is located at 200 SE 18th Court, Fort Lauderdale, FL 33316 and can be reached by phone at 954-764-4330.
