# Alan Goldfarb, P.A.

Alan Goldfarb, P.A. is a Miami personal injury and medical malpractice law firm serving injured people and families throughout Miami-Dade County and the greater Miami area. Founded by trial attorney Alan Goldfarb, who has tried more than 250 cases to verdict, the firm brings more than 50 years of personal injury experience to car and truck accidents, premises liability, negligent security, construction accidents, product liability, cruise ship and boat accidents, nursing home abuse, wrongful death, and medical malpractice. The firm reports more than 100 verdicts and settlements over $1,000,000, including a $16 million wrongful death result and a $15 million medical malpractice result. Alan Goldfarb is AV rated by Martindale-Hubbell and has been listed among Florida Super Lawyers' Top 100 Lawyers and in The Best Lawyers in America, with firm recognition from Florida Legal Elite. Its cases have been covered by the Miami Herald, Local 10 WPLG, and CBS4. A full-time nurse paralegal helps evaluate medical claims, Spanish-speaking staff are available, and the attorneys will meet clients at home or in the hospital. Alan Goldfarb, P.A. is located at 100 Southeast 2nd Street, Suite 4500, Miami, FL 33131 and can be reached by phone at 305-371-3111.
