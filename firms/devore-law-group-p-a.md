# Devore Law Group, P.A.

Devore Law Group, P.A. is an immigration and nationality law firm in Palm Beach Gardens serving individuals, families and businesses in West Palm Beach, Boca Raton and throughout Palm Beach County, as well as clients across the United States and abroad. Jeffrey A. Devore, a member of The Florida Bar since 1990, leads the firm with attorneys Tulio G. Suarez, Luciana C. Ugarte and Fiorella S. Herrera. Mr. Devore is Board Certified in Immigration and Nationality Law by The Florida Bar and is admitted before the U.S. Supreme Court and several federal circuit and district courts. The firm focuses exclusively on U.S. immigration law, including business-based immigration and H-1B visas, family-based petitions, green cards, deportation defense, bond hearings, asylum, and citizenship. Mr. Devore holds an AV Preeminent rating from Martindale-Hubbell, has been recognized by Florida Legal Elite and Super Lawyers, is listed in Who's Who Legal for business immigration, and is a two-time recipient of the Legal Aid Society of Palm Beach County's Immigration Law Award for pro bono service. Attorneys and staff serve clients in English and Spanish. Devore Law Group, P.A. is located at 4100 RCA Blvd., Suite 110, Palm Beach Gardens, FL 33410 and can be reached by phone at 561-478-5353.
