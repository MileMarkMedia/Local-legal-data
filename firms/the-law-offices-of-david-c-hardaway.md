# The Law Offices of David C. Hardaway

The Law Offices of David C. Hardaway is a Central Texas criminal defense firm founded in San Marcos in 2010 by David C. Hardaway, with offices in San Marcos, Brenham and Fredericksburg. The firm defends clients in Hays, Comal, Caldwell, Guadalupe, Travis, Williamson, Bexar, Washington, Gillespie and surrounding counties against DWI and intoxication charges, drug possession and trafficking, assault and family violence, sex crimes, theft and property crimes, human smuggling, and misdemeanor and felony charges at every level, and it helps eligible clients pursue expunctions and record sealing. David Hardaway has been named a Texas Super Lawyer, one of The National Trial Lawyers Top 100 Trial Lawyers and one of America's Top 100 Attorneys, and he is a member of the National College for DUI Defense and the Texas Criminal Defense Lawyers Association. Results include not guilty verdicts in DWI and felony injury to a child cases and an intoxication manslaughter charge reduced to DWI in Comal County. The Law Offices of David C. Hardaway is located at 100 E San Antonio St, Suite 100, San Marcos, TX 78666 and can be reached by phone at 512-805-6613.
