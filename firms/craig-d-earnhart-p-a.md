# Craig D. Earnhart, P.A.

Craig D. Earnhart, P.A., also known as Earnhart Law, is a personal injury firm serving Delray Beach, Boca Raton, Boynton Beach, and the surrounding Palm Beach County area. Founder Craig D. Earnhart, a Delray Beach native and Atlantic High School graduate, has been practicing law since 1985 and handles car, truck, and motorcycle accidents, boating accidents, slip and fall and premises liability claims, dog bites, workplace injuries including workers' compensation and third-party claims, uninsured motorist claims, and wrongful death. Early in his career he worked for Progressive Insurance, giving him added insight when pursuing claims against insurers. He meets clients personally from the start, attends status conferences and hearings, and stays in close contact rather than passing files off to associates or paralegals. The firm offers free consultations, home and hospital visits for clients who cannot travel, an after-hours emergency number, and contingency fees with no legal fees unless money is recovered. A native Spanish-speaking legal assistant serves Spanish-speaking clients, and the firm is familiar with working with the Haitian community in Delray Beach. Earnhart Law is located at 70 SE 4th Ave, Delray Beach, FL 33483 and can be reached by phone at 561-265-2220.
