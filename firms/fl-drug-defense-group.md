# FL Drug Defense Group

FL Drug Defense Group is a drug crime defense firm serving Orlando and Central Florida, including clients charged in Orange, Osceola, and Seminole counties. The firm is led by attorney Matt Olszewski, a former drug prosecutor who also coordinated the Florida Traffic Safety Resource Prosecutor Program, training prosecutors and officers across the state. He helped draft the Florida Department of Law Enforcement Instruction Manual and worked with FDLE on curriculum used at Florida law enforcement training academies. Matt has handled over 100 criminal trials and personally manages each case from start to finish, covering drug possession, sales and trafficking, search and seizure issues, and drug court and diversion. He received the 2018 Gladiator Award from the Florida Association of Criminal Defense Lawyers, one of only five practicing criminal defense attorneys in the state to receive it, and holds an Avvo Rating of 10.0 and a 2019 Avvo Client's Choice Award. Reported results include dismissed trafficking charges involving cocaine and heroin. Free case evaluations are offered, and Spanish-speaking services are available during regular office hours. FL Drug Defense Group is located at 200 East Robinson Street, Suite 1140, Orlando, FL 32801 and can be reached by phone at 407-775-9052.
