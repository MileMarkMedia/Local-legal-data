# Law Office of Vanessa R. Dozier, LLC

The Law Office of Vanessa R. Dozier, LLC is a Maryland law firm focused on family law and personal injury, serving clients throughout Central Maryland and Washington, D.C., including Columbia, Rockville, Silver Spring, Upper Marlboro, and Baltimore, as well as Howard, Montgomery, Prince George's, Charles, and Anne Arundel counties. Co-founding partner Vanessa R. Dozier earned her undergraduate degree from Howard University and her law degree from Columbia Law School and has practiced since 2016. The firm handles contested, uncontested, collaborative, high-asset, and military divorces, child custody and visitation, child support and alimony, domestic violence protective orders, paternity, adoption, grandparents' rights, mediation, and prenuptial and postnuptial agreements. Vanessa also represents injured clients in car, truck, motorcycle, pedestrian, and slip and fall claims. Her recognition includes Super Lawyers Rising Stars from 2024 through 2026, Lawyers of Distinction, a 2019 Top 40 Under 40 honor from the National Black Lawyers, and multiple Avvo Clients' Choice Awards. The Law Office of Vanessa R. Dozier, LLC is located at 10490 Little Patuxent Pkwy, Suite 600, Columbia, MD 21044 and can be reached by phone at 240-403-4033.
