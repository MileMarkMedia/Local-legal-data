# Seward Law Office, P.A.

Seward Law Office, P.A. is a Tampa real estate law firm focused on homeowners and condominium associations, landlord and tenant matters, and property owner representation, with offices in Tampa and St. Petersburg. Founder Alicia Seward worked in the real estate field representing associations and landlords before forming the firm, and she earned her law degree from Stetson University College of Law with a concentration in trial advocacy. The firm represents associations, self-managed communities, management companies, unit owners, landlords, investors, and property owners in association rule enforcement, lien foreclosure, commercial and residential evictions, ejectments, unlawful detainers, deed preparation, contract disputes and terminations, fraud and misrepresentation, and real estate litigation. It also provides in-house title services and handles business law and probate matters. Clients work directly with Alicia Seward and her team, and consultations are confidential and by appointment. The firm serves the Tampa Bay area, including St. Petersburg, Brandon, Riverview, Manatee, Sarasota, and Orlando. Seward Law Office, P.A. is located at 5401 W. Kennedy Blvd, Suite 100, Tampa, FL 33609 and can be reached by phone at 813-252-6789.
