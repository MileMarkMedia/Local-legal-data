# Connolly Injury Law

Connolly Injury Law is a Chicago firm focused on workers' compensation and personal injury cases, serving injured workers and accident victims throughout Chicago and Cook, DuPage, Kane, Lake, McHenry, and Will counties, as well as Peoria, Rockford, and communities across Illinois. Principal attorney Mark Connolly opened the firm in 2021 and has represented injured plaintiffs and petitioners as a trial attorney since 2009. A former union laborer and member of Laborers' Local 4, he earned his law degree cum laude from Northern Illinois University College of Law, served as Assistant Counsel to the Speaker of the Illinois House and has argued before the Illinois Appellate Court. The firm handles construction, workplace, and delivery driver injuries, third-party claims, head and brain injuries, motor vehicle and truck accidents, slip and falls, nursing home abuse, and wrongful death. Results include a $1,300,000 traumatic brain injury recovery, a $1,100,000 truck accident recovery, and a $950,000 airport tarmac accident recovery. Mark Connolly personally handles each case, consultations are free in person or by Zoom, and clients pay no fee unless the firm recovers. Connolly Injury Law is located at 1720 W Division Street, Chicago, IL 60622 and can be reached by phone at 312-780-0816.
