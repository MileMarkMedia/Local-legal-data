# Bigos Law

Bigos Law is a personal injury and sexual abuse survivor law firm based in Auburn, Maine, serving clients throughout Maine and New Hampshire, with appointment offices in Portland and Bangor. Founded by trial lawyer Michael Bigos, the firm focuses on catastrophic injury and wrongful death cases, including traumatic brain injuries, burns, amputations, defective products, fires and explosions, and car and truck crashes. Bigos Law provides trauma-informed representation to abuse survivors in cases against institutions such as churches, private schools, and summer camps. Reported results include a $4.5 million sexual abuse verdict, a $1.4 million catastrophic injury verdict, and a $1.2 million personal injury settlement, and Michael Bigos served as lead attorney on damages for a team that obtained a multi-eight-figure settlement in Maine. The firm's cases have been covered by WGME, WMTW, the Bangor Daily News, and NewsCenter Maine. Its attorneys speak French, Arabic, and Turkish, and the firm works with local interpreters for other languages. Bigos Law prepares every case as though it will go to trial, offers free consultations, and works on a contingency fee. Bigos Law is located at 2 Great Falls Plaza, Suite 630, Auburn, ME 04210 and can be reached by phone at 207-536-6300.
