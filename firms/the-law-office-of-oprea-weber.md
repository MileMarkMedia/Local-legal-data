# The Law Office Of Oprea & Weber

The Law Office Of Oprea & Weber is an Austin, Texas family law firm led by attorneys Kevin Weber and Karen Oprea, serving families in Travis, Williamson and Hays counties and communities across Central Texas, including Round Rock, Cedar Park, Georgetown and San Marcos. The firm handles uncontested, mediated, collaborative, cooperative and litigated divorce, along with property division, child custody and visitation, child support, spousal support, modifications, relocation, paternity and prenuptial and postnuptial agreements. Kevin Weber's practice centers on marital agreements, collaborative divorce, complex property division and family law mediation, and he has significant civil and family appeals experience. Karen Oprea began her career defending clients at the New York Legal Aid Society, has federal and state trial and appellate experience, and focuses her current practice on estate planning and probate. Both attorneys bring large firm backgrounds to the practice. The firm keeps its caseload manageable so clients work directly with the attorneys rather than layers of staff or associates, favoring cooperative resolution while preparing to litigate when a case requires it. The Law Office Of Oprea & Weber is located at 1411 West Ave, Suite 200, Austin, TX 78701 and can be reached by phone at 512-344-9070.
