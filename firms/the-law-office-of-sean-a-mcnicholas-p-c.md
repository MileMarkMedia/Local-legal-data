# The Law Office of Sean A. McNicholas, P.C.

The Law Office of Sean A. McNicholas, P.C. is a criminal defense firm in Kew Gardens serving clients throughout Queens County, New York, including Jamaica, Flushing, Astoria and Forest Hills. Sean A. McNicholas grew up in Queens and began his career prosecuting criminal cases with the Queens District Attorney's Office, and his practice is devoted entirely to criminal defense. He represents individuals facing felonies and misdemeanors, including DWI, domestic violence, assault, drug and weapons offenses, theft, robbery, sex offenses, white collar crimes, homicide, youthful offender matters and appeals. He appears regularly in Queens Criminal Court, and his experience as a prosecutor helps him identify weaknesses in the government's case and pursue dismissal, reduced charges, negotiated resolutions or trial. The firm provides immediate assistance to clients who have been arrested, received a Desk Appearance Ticket or are being held at Rikers Island. Sean McNicholas is a member of the Queens County Bar Association and the Guild of Catholic Lawyers of the Archdiocese of New York, and clients receive regular case updates and realistic advice. The Law Office of Sean A. McNicholas, P.C. is located at 125-16 C Queens Blvd, Kew Gardens, NY 11415 and can be reached by phone at 917-885-3167.
