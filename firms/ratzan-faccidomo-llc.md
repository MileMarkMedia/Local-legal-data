# Ratzan & Faccidomo LLC

Ratzan & Faccidomo LLC is a Miami criminal defense firm founded in 2013 by Mycki Ratzan and Jude M. Faccidomo. The firm focuses on criminal defense, professional license defense, and college student defense, representing clients in state and federal court in Miami and throughout Florida in matters including DUI, drug crimes, sex crimes, white collar and financial crimes, theft, violent crimes, and domestic violence. Mycki Ratzan has over 20 years of Florida criminal defense experience and previously spent 17 years as a partner at another Miami firm. Jude Faccidomo, the firm's managing partner, is a former Assistant Public Defender with the Miami-Dade Public Defender's Office, was installed as the 34th president of the Florida Association of Criminal Defense Lawyers, and was appointed Chair of The Florida Bar's Criminal Procedure Rules Committee effective July 1, 2026. Recognitions include Florida Super Lawyers, Florida Legal Elite, Best Lawyers, and an Avvo Rating of 10.0. The firm offers free initial consultations, visits incarcerated clients in person, and provides Spanish language services as needed. Ratzan & Faccidomo LLC is located at 2850 Tigertail Avenue, Suite 400, Miami, FL 33133 and can be reached by phone at 305-600-3519.
