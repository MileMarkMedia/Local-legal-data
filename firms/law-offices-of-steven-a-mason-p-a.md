# Law Offices of Steven A. Mason, P.A.

The Law Offices of Steven A. Mason, P.A. is a Hollywood, Florida family law firm serving clients throughout Broward, Miami-Dade and Palm Beach counties. Since 1981, the firm has handled divorce, alimony, child custody and support, division of marital assets, military divorce and mediation, along with estate planning, probate, trusts, real estate, and homeowners and condominium association matters. Attorney Steven A. Mason, a graduate of Johns Hopkins University and Villanova University School of Law, brings more than 40 years of experience. He served on active duty as a captain in the U.S. Army JAG Corps and retired from the JAG Reserve as a lieutenant colonel. He is a Florida Supreme Court certified family and circuit civil mediator, qualified arbitrator and community association mediator, and a past president of the Association of South Florida Mediators and Arbitrators. Mason was named to Florida Super Lawyers in 2014 and holds an AV Preeminent rating from Martindale-Hubbell. He also speaks German. The firm favors negotiated resolutions while remaining prepared to go to trial. The Law Offices of Steven A. Mason, P.A. is located at 4700 Sheridan St., Suite J, Hollywood, FL 33021 and can be reached by phone at 954-963-5900.
