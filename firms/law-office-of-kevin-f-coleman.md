# Law Office of Kevin F. Coleman

The Law Office of Kevin F. Coleman is a family law firm serving St. Petersburg, Florida and nearby communities, including Gulfport, Seminole, Snell Isle, and South Pasadena. Attorney Kevin F. Coleman has spent the past decade focusing almost exclusively on family law, handling more than 100 family law cases, including multiple trials. He previously served as a prosecutor for the State Attorney for the Sixth Judicial Circuit, where he tried more than twenty jury trials and supervised a division of prosecutors. He graduated magna cum laude from the University of Miami, earned his law degree from Florida State University College of Law, and serves on the board of the Canakaris American Inn of Court. The firm handles divorce, including high net worth, military, and LGBTQ+ cases, along with child custody and support, alimony, equitable distribution, relocation, paternity, modifications, domestic violence injunctions, and prenuptial and postnuptial agreements. Kevin is each client's point of contact from consultation through resolution, using negotiation, mediation, or litigation, and the firm offers a free initial phone consultation. The Law Office of Kevin F. Coleman is located at 447 3rd Ave N, Suite 203, St. Petersburg, FL 33701 and can be reached by phone at 727-291-9010.
