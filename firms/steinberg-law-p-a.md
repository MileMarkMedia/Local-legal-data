# Steinberg Law, P.A.

Steinberg Law, P.A. is a personal injury law firm with offices in Delray Beach and Palm Beach Gardens, serving Boca Raton, Boynton Beach, West Palm Beach, Jupiter, and Palm Beach, Broward, and Miami-Dade counties. Founder and president Brett Steinberg is a trial attorney who began his career as an Assistant Public Defender in Miami-Dade, where he tried over 25 cases to verdict. Since 2014 he has recovered over $25 million in verdicts and settlements, including a $2,600,000 sexual assault jury verdict against a recovery center after a $20,000 settlement offer, along with $1,850,000 and $1,800,000 pedestrian settlements. Early in his career he also assisted in a mesothelioma trial that produced a $24,170,000 verdict. The firm handles car, truck, motorcycle, rideshare, and pedestrian accidents, wrongful death, slip and fall, premises liability, medical malpractice, product liability, and cruise ship injuries. Brett Steinberg has been named a Florida Super Lawyer every year since 2015, is AV rated by Martindale-Hubbell, and holds an Avvo rating of 10.0. Cases are handled on a contingency fee basis, and clients work directly with Brett and his team. Steinberg Law, P.A. is located at 1625 S Congress Ave, Suite 325, Delray Beach, FL 33445 and can be reached by phone at 561-630-0053.
