# Law Office of Michael Lawrence Varon, PLLC

The Law Office of Michael Lawrence Varon, PLLC is an administrative law practice in White Plains, New York, focused on Social Security Disability, workers' compensation and veterans benefits claims. Attorney Michael Varon has more than 20 years of legal experience, much of it concentrated on SSD and workers' compensation matters, and he has also handled veterans benefits claims for over 10 years, including cases before the Regional Office, the Board of Veterans' Appeals and the Court of Appeals for Veterans Claims. The firm represents clients in workers' compensation and SSD cases throughout Westchester County, New York City and the surrounding areas, and handles veterans benefits claims nationally. Claims are handled on a contingency fee basis, so clients do not pay unless a recovery is obtained. The Law Office of Michael Lawrence Varon, PLLC is located at 14 Mamaroneck Avenue, Suite 400, White Plains, NY 10601 and can be reached by phone at 914-228-1770.
