# Fuxa & Tyler

Fuxa & Tyler is a Florida law firm focused on insurance claims and property damage disputes, representing homeowners, condominium associations, and business owners statewide from its office in Sunrise. Named attorneys Andrew Fuxa and Jeremy F. Tyler and their team handle first-party and third-party coverage disputes, bad faith insurance claims, hurricane and windstorm damage, denied and underpaid claims, appraisal, and construction defects. Andrew Fuxa began his career almost 30 years ago as a property restoration contractor, later owned and operated a public insurance adjusting company, and has personally completed more than 400 property damage appraisals. The firm brings decades of experience representing policyholders in trial and complex litigation, and works with a network of public adjusters. Reported results include a $1,600,000 settlement in a first-party coverage dispute after a $525,000 pre-trial offer and a $1,550,000 bad faith insurance settlement after a $125,000 offer. Cases are handled on a contingency fee basis with a free initial consultation, and several attorneys and staff speak Spanish. Fuxa & Tyler is located at 490 Sawgrass Corporate Pkwy, Suite 110, Sunrise, FL 33325 and can be reached by phone at 954-990-5251.
