# Wadhwani & Shanfeld

Wadhwani & Shanfeld is a Southern California bankruptcy and debt relief law firm founded in 2005 that has helped more than 30,000 individuals and families get out from under debt. Founding shareholders Raj Wadhwani and Greg Shanfeld are both Board-Certified Consumer Bankruptcy Specialists through the American Board of Certification, and the firm's attorneys bring more than 90 years of combined experience. Raj Wadhwani has handled more than 15,000 bankruptcy filings and was the first attorney to electronically file a bankruptcy case in the Central District of California, and Greg Shanfeld has handled more than 10,000 cases over three decades. The firm handles Chapter 7 and Chapter 13 bankruptcy, small business bankruptcy, debt settlement, foreclosure protection, wage garnishment, repossession, IRS tax debt, and creditor lawsuits from offices in Sherman Oaks, Los Angeles, Ontario, Long Beach, Lancaster and Palmdale, with free consultations and fully remote options. Wadhwani & Shanfeld is located at 15233 Ventura Blvd, Suite 1000, Sherman Oaks, CA 91403 and can be reached by phone at 818-658-2669.
