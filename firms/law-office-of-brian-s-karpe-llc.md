# Law Office of Brian S. Karpe, LLC

The Law Office of Brian S. Karpe, LLC is a Canton, Connecticut law firm focused on estate planning, probate, and elder law for clients in Canton, Hartford, Avon, Bloomfield, Farmington, Simsbury, Torrington, and throughout Hartford County. Founder Brian S. Karpe, a Connecticut native, began practicing law in Colorado in 1985 and has over 30 years of legal experience. He earned his law degree from Drake University School of Law and a Master of Laws in estate planning and elder law from Western New England School of Law, and he is admitted in Connecticut and Colorado, before the United States Supreme Court, and in the United States Tax Court. He is a member of the National Academy of Elder Law Attorneys and the Connecticut Bar Association. The firm handles wills, revocable living trusts, special needs trusts, trust administration, asset protection, Medicaid planning, conservatorships, guardianships, and probate. Drawing on prior insurance defense work and appointments as a special public defender, the firm also represents clients in personal injury, workers' compensation, and criminal defense matters. The firm offers free consultations. The Law Office of Brian S. Karpe, LLC is located at 326 Albany Turnpike, Suite A, Canton, CT 06019 and can be reached by phone at 860-217-1458.
