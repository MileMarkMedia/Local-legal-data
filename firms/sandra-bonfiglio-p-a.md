# Sandra Bonfiglio, P.A.

Sandra Bonfiglio, P.A. is a Fort Lauderdale law firm practicing family law exclusively, serving clients throughout Broward County, Palm Beach County, and Miami-Dade County. Attorney Sandra Bonfiglio was admitted to The Florida Bar in 1999 and established her own firm in 2001, bringing more than 20 years of family law experience to each case. She has taught as an adjunct professor of law at Nova Southeastern University and served as supervising attorney for the university's family and civil law clinic. The firm handles uncontested, contested, and collaborative divorce, alimony, property division, child custody and timesharing, relocation, child support and modifications, paternity, guardianship, grandparents' rights, prenuptial and postnuptial agreements, and stepparent adoption, and also offers mediation. Sandra Bonfiglio is a member of the Family Law Sections of the Broward County Bar Association and The Florida Bar. The firm favors amicable resolutions through mediation and collaborative divorce and litigates when agreement is not possible, with clients served in Fort Lauderdale, Hollywood, Pompano Beach, Deerfield Beach, Pembroke Pines, and Boca Raton. Sandra Bonfiglio, P.A. is located at 105 South Avenue Of The Arts, Fort Lauderdale, FL 33312 and can be reached by phone at 954-945-7591.
