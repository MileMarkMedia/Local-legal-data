# The Law Offices of Christopher Eads, PLLC

The Law Offices of Christopher Eads, PLLC is a Mt. Juliet, Tennessee law firm focused on personal injury, employment law and criminal defense, with offices in Mt. Juliet and Gallatin serving clients throughout Middle Tennessee, including Lebanon, Murfreesboro, Hendersonville and Nashville. Founder Christopher Eads, licensed in Tennessee since 2015, is a former insurance defense attorney who practiced with Hall Booth Smith and McAngus Goudelock & Courie, and he uses that knowledge of how insurers evaluate claims for injured clients. Reported results include $800,000 in a supervisor sexual harassment case, $775,000 for a client rear-ended by a dump truck, and $400,000 in a workers' compensation back injury case, and the firm handled more than 75 cases in its first year of operation. He has been recognized by Super Lawyers Rising Stars in 2023, Best Lawyers: Ones to Watch and The National Trial Lawyers Top 40 Under 40. The firm handles vehicle accidents, catastrophic injuries, premises liability, workers' compensation and wrongful death, along with harassment, discrimination and wrongful termination claims. Injury cases are taken on contingency, and clients work directly with their attorney. The Law Offices of Christopher Eads, PLLC is located at 1400 N Mt Juliet Rd, Suite 206, Mt. Juliet, TN 37122 and can be reached by phone at 615-622-6060.
