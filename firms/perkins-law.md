# Perkins Law

Perkins Law is a Boca Raton intellectual property and brand protection firm serving clients in Palm Beach County, throughout South Florida and Florida, and across the United States. Founder and CEO L.A. Perkins brings more than 24 years of experience litigating cases in federal and state courts, including jury trials, and was previously a partner at Fox Rothschild, LLP and Buckingham, Doolittle & Burroughs, LLP. The firm handles trademark registration, prosecution, monitoring, and infringement, copyright registration and infringement, trade secret protection and employment agreements, patent litigation, licensing and assignment agreements, counterfeiting and unfair competition claims, and local counsel work for out-of-state attorneys. Ms. Perkins is a Florida Supreme Court Certified Circuit Civil and Family Mediator and a former President of the South Palm Beach County Bar Association. Her recognitions include Super Lawyers, an AV Preeminent rating from Martindale-Hubbell, and a 10.0 Avvo Rating, and she has appeared in the Daily Business Review, The Florida Bar News, and The National Law Journal. Perkins Law is located at 2295 NW Corporate Boulevard, Suite 117, Boca Raton, FL 33431 and can be reached by phone at 561-467-4001.
