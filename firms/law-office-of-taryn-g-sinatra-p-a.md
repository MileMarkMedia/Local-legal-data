# Law Office of Taryn G. Sinatra, P.A.

The Law Office of Taryn G. Sinatra, P.A. is a Boynton Beach family law firm that concentrates exclusively on marital and family law for clients throughout Palm Beach and Broward counties. Founded in 2010 by Taryn Goodman Sinatra, the firm handles divorce, including high-net-worth and business owner divorces, along with alimony, child support, equitable distribution, time-sharing and parenting plans, relocation, paternity, domestic violence injunctions, prenuptial and postnuptial agreements, stepparent adoption, military and same-sex divorce, and international child abduction cases. Taryn Sinatra is Board Certified in Marital and Family Law by The Florida Bar, a distinction held by less than 1% of Florida attorneys, and is a Florida Supreme Court Certified Family Mediator. Admitted to the Florida Bar in 2005, she brings more than two decades of experience, including service as an Assistant State Attorney in Broward County. Her honors include the Florida Bar Family Law Section Spotlight Award, Super Lawyers Rising Star selections in 2014 and 2015, and Super Lawyers selections from 2018 through 2021. The Law Office of Taryn G. Sinatra, P.A. is located at 1405 S. Federal Highway, Boynton Beach, FL 33435 and can be reached by phone at 561-430-4121.
