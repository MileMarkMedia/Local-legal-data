# Evans Bowers

Evans Bowers is an Atlanta law firm focused on defamation and environmental law, representing clients throughout Georgia. The firm represents individuals and businesses harmed by false statements, including libel and slander, as well as property owners and communities harmed by environmental contamination. Its attorneys handle claims involving water pollution, air pollution, soil contamination, and hazardous waste, bringing actions under laws such as the Clean Water Act and Clean Air Act, and have worked on nationally significant pollution cases involving PFAS, coal ash, acid mine drainage, and industrial adhesives and sealants. Members of the team represented plaintiffs in some of the first civil environmental enforcement cases in the country to reach trial or settlement over coal ash and PFAS contamination, and have successfully challenged environmental permits. The firm also represents businesses in commercial litigation, including partnership disputes, breach of contract claims, business torts, and internal investigations. Evans Bowers is recognized in the Chambers USA 2026 Spotlight guide for Environmental Law. Its lawyers are frequent speakers at law schools, universities, and in the media on emerging environmental enforcement issues. Evans Bowers is located at 729 Piedmont Ave NE, Atlanta, GA 30308 and can be reached by phone at 404-850-6750.
