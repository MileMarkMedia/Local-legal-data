# Arwani Law Firm

Arwani Law Firm is an Orlando divorce and family law firm serving clients throughout Orange, Osceola, Seminole, Polk, Volusia, and Lake counties. Managing attorney Rania Arwani, a graduate of Barry University School of Law, is a collaboratively trained family law attorney, a member of the Collaborative Family Law Group of Central Florida, a TEDx speaker on the impact of domestic violence, and author of the memoir Unintentional Hero. Senior attorney Victoria Snell brings more than 23 years of experience to the practice. The firm handles contested, uncontested, collaborative, high-asset, military, and same-sex divorce, along with child custody, time-sharing, child support, alimony, property division, domestic violence, prenuptial and postnuptial agreements, international family law, and modification and enforcement of prior orders. Arwani Law Firm was recognized in Orlando Family Magazine's Awesome Attorneys 2023. Its bilingual team serves clients in English and Arabic, represents either side of a family law matter, and offers mediation and collaborative options alongside litigation, with early case preparation and honest counsel about realistic outcomes. Arwani Law Firm is located at 55 E Washington St, Orlando, FL 32801 and can be reached by phone at 407-254-0060.
