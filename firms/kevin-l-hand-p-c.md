# Kevin L. Hand, P.C.

Kevin L. Hand, P.C. is a Newtown law firm concentrating on divorce and family law, along with criminal defense, personal injury, estate matters, and appeals, for clients in Bucks County, Montgomery County, Mercer County, and the surrounding region. The firm has served clients since 1981, and its lawyers are admitted to local, state, and federal courts in Pennsylvania and New Jersey. Partner Kevin L. Hand earned his J.D. from Temple University and previously served as a Falls Township police officer, including work as an undercover narcotics agent, and as Deputy Solicitor for the Pennsylvania State Police, before becoming a partner at a Bucks County law firm. He handles divorce, equitable distribution, alimony, child support, child custody and relocation, protection from abuse matters, prenuptial and postnuptial agreements, adoption, and juvenile and adult criminal cases in state and federal courts. The firm's honors include Super Lawyers, Rising Stars, 10 Best, and Top 40 Under 40, and Kevin L. Hand has received a Client Distinction Award from Martindale-Hubbell. The firm resolves cases through negotiation, mediation, or litigation and aims to return calls with substantive answers within 24 hours. Kevin L. Hand, P.C. is located at 626 South State Street, Newtown, PA 18940 and can be reached by phone at 215-968-6602.
