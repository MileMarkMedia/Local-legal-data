# Hoy Law

Hoy Law is a Sioux Falls personal injury firm representing injury victims throughout South Dakota, along with clients in nearby Minnesota and Iowa, with a focus on truck, car, and motorcycle accidents. Scott G. Hoy chose to focus solely on plaintiff law in 1991, and Carleton R. "Tex" Hoy and James L. Hoy joined him in 2004. The firm brings more than 30 years in business and over 150 years of combined attorney experience to semi-truck and commercial vehicle crashes, rideshare and pedestrian accidents, traumatic brain and spinal cord injuries, wrongful death, and insurance bad faith claims. Results include an $8.5 million recovery in a commercial vehicle and moped crash, a $5.425 million truck accident recovery, a $2.3 million auto accident verdict, and a $1.8 million insurance bad faith verdict. The firm's recognitions include Super Lawyers, The National Trial Lawyers Top 100, Lead Counsel, and the National Association of Distinguished Counsel. Every case is prepared for trial, consultations are free in person or by Zoom or FaceTime, and fees are charged only if compensation is recovered. Hoy Law is located at 4900 S Minnesota Ave, Suite 200, Sioux Falls, SD 57108 and can be reached by phone at 605-334-8900.
