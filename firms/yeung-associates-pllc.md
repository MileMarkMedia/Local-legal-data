# Yeung & Associates, PLLC

Yeung & Associates, PLLC is a Brooklyn law firm focused on real estate transactions, estate planning and probate for clients in Brooklyn, Queens, Manhattan, the Bronx, Staten Island and across New York State. Founder and lead attorney Sandy Yeung earned her law degree from Hofstra University School of Law, where she co-founded the First-Generation Law Student Association, after working for nine years as a paralegal at a New York real estate law firm. She is admitted to practice throughout New York and is a member of the New Jersey Bar. The firm handles residential and commercial purchases and sales, commercial leases, financing and lending, refinancing, deed transfers, investment property transactions, title searches and closings, as well as wills, trusts, powers of attorney, advance directives and estate administration. Sandy Yeung speaks Mandarin and Cantonese and serves clients in neighborhoods including Bensonhurst, Bay Ridge, Sunset Park, Sheepshead Bay, Flushing, Bayside and Elmhurst. The firm does not rely on brokers or third-party referrals, and Ms. Yeung handles each matter personally, keeping clients informed at every step. Yeung & Associates, PLLC is located at 2059 86th St, Suite 200, Brooklyn, NY 11214 and can be reached by phone at 718-889-7568.
