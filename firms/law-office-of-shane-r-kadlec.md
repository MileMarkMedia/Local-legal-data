# Law Office of Shane R. Kadlec

The Law Office of Shane R. Kadlec is a Houston personal injury firm that has represented injured people across Houston and all of Harris County since 1996. Founder Shane R. Kadlec is Board Certified in Personal Injury Trial Law by the Texas Board of Legal Specialization, a credential he first earned in 2003 and one held by fewer than 3 percent of Texas lawyers. The firm handles car, truck, motorcycle and 18-wheeler crashes, along with premises liability, product liability, medical and dental malpractice, catastrophic injury and wrongful death claims. It also takes on the workplace injuries common to the Houston economy, including construction, industrial, oil rig, refinery, and longshore and harbor accidents. Recent results include a $907,500 settlement for a client hurt in a rear-end collision. With 30 years of practice behind it, the firm works on a contingency fee basis, offers free consultations and serves Spanish-speaking clients. The Law Office of Shane R. Kadlec is located at 9575 Katy Freeway, Suite 200, Houston, TX 77024 and can be reached by phone at 281-643-2000.
