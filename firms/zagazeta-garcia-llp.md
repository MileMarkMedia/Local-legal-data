# Zagazeta Garcia LLP

Zagazeta Garcia LLP is a Mississauga law firm focused on family law, divorce, and Canadian and US immigration, along with real estate and notary services for clients in Mississauga, Toronto, Brampton and across the GTA. The firm was co-founded by M. Elka Zagazeta Garcia and M. Norka Zagazeta Garcia, and its lawyers bring more than 30 years of combined experience. Both earned their law degrees at Thomas M. Cooley Law School and completed NCA accreditation, and Norka Zagazeta Garcia is licensed in Florida, practiced immigration law in Miami and is approved as a Foreign Legal Consultant by the Law Society of Upper Canada. The firm handles divorce, child custody, child support, spousal support, property division, Hague Convention matters and family mediation, Canadian family-based immigration, work permits, LMIAs and Express Entry, and US business immigration including E-1, E-2 and TN visas. Real estate work includes residential purchases, sales and mortgages. Consultations are available in English or Spanish, initial consultations are free, and the firm has offices in Mississauga and Oakville and a location in Lima, Peru. Zagazeta Garcia LLP is located at 33 City Centre Drive, Unit 600, Mississauga, ON L5B 2N5 and can be reached by phone at 905-232-0398.
