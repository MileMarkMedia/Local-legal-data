# Henrietta Ezeoke Law Firm

Henrietta Ezeoke Law Firm, PLLC is a personal injury law firm serving Missouri City, Sugar Land, Houston, Pearland, and communities across Fort Bend, Brazoria, and Harris Counties, with offices in Missouri City and Houston. Attorney Henrietta N. Ezeoke, a graduate of South Texas College of Law, has represented injured people in personal injury cases for more than 20 years. The firm handles car, truck, motorcycle, rideshare, pedestrian, and bicycle accidents, premises liability and slip and fall claims, dog bites, nursing home abuse and neglect, workplace and construction accidents, boating and maritime accidents, catastrophic injuries such as brain and spinal cord injuries, and wrongful death. Clients meet with Henrietta Ezeoke directly and keep the same attorney throughout their case, and the firm limits its caseload so each matter receives individual attention. Consultations are free and available in person, by phone, or by video, Spanish is spoken, and representation is on a contingency basis with no fee unless compensation is recovered. Henrietta Ezeoke Law Firm, PLLC is located at 7070 Knights CT, Suite 1104, Missouri City, TX 77459 and can be reached by phone at 713-234-0030.
