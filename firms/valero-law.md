# Valero Law

Valero Law is a South Florida firm focused on probate and trust litigation, real estate litigation and business litigation for clients in Miami-Dade and Broward County, including property owners who live out of state or abroad. The firm was founded by David Thomas Valero, a graduate of the University of Florida Levin College of Law with a background in real estate, who has litigated at the trial and appellate levels across Florida, including the Florida Supreme Court. Attorney Amanda Elizabeth Valero-Vincent, a graduate of St. Thomas University School of Law who interned with Judge Barry Seltzer of the U.S. District Court for the Southern District of Florida, focuses on civil litigation and appeals. The firm handles will contests, trustee removal, breach of fiduciary duty, partition actions between co-owners, forged and fraudulent deeds, quiet title actions, company dissolution, non-compete enforcement and commercial contract disputes, along with civil appeals before the Third and Fourth District Courts of Appeal. The firm offers a free initial consultation, serves clients in Spanish, and has offices in Davie and Miami. Valero Law is located at 6100 Griffin Rd, Suite 337, Davie, FL 33314 and can be reached by phone at 305-607-7011.
