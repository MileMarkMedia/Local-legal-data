# Pendas Law Firm

The Pendas Law Firm is a personal injury firm established in 2004 and based in Orlando, serving clients across Florida, Georgia, Washington State, and Puerto Rico. Its Florida offices include Orlando, Ocala, Tampa, Bradenton, Fort Myers, Jacksonville, Daytona Beach, Melbourne, Miami, Naples, Fort Lauderdale, and West Palm Beach, with additional offices in Atlanta, Seattle, and San Juan. The firm's trial lawyers handle car, truck, motorcycle, and rideshare accidents, slip and falls, medical malpractice, nursing home abuse, workplace injuries, product liability, negligent security, insurance bad faith, catastrophic injuries, and wrongful death, as well as Puerto Rico hurricane insurance claims. Reported results include $3.83 million for a catastrophic motorcycle crash, $3 million in a trucking accident, and $1.7 million in an auto accident. Most of the firm's lawyers and paralegals are fluent in Spanish. Cases are handled on a contingency fee basis, the firm advances case costs, and clients pay nothing unless it wins. The firm is also active in community outreach, including its Annual Turkey Giveaway. The Pendas Law Firm is located at 625 E Colonial Dr, Orlando, FL 32803 and can be reached by phone at 844-200-0000.
