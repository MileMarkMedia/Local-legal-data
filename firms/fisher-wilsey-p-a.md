# Fisher & Wilsey, P.A.

Fisher & Wilsey, P.A. is a family-run St. Petersburg law firm focused on estate planning and probate, serving clients throughout St. Petersburg, Pinellas County and the greater Tampa Bay area. The firm was founded in 1962 by attorneys Robert W. Fisher and George F. Wilsey. Today George's sons, David F. Wilsey and Steven M. Wilsey, are the firm's partners and primary shareholders, practicing alongside attorneys Seymour A. Gordon, Abigail W. Bahr, and Chase L. Wilsey. For more than 60 years, the firm has handled Florida probate estates and provided practical legal advice to families and individuals. Its attorneys work on wills, trusts, powers of attorney, health care directives, Medicaid and long-term care planning, tax planning, probate administration and probate litigation, trust administration, elder law, real estate law, business law, and civil litigation. That long history has given the firm experience with the estate planning and probate needs of small-business owners, veterans, same-sex couples, and people of many ethnic and religious backgrounds, and it guides families from around the world through Florida probate. Fisher & Wilsey, P.A. is located at 1000 16th Street N, St. Petersburg, FL 33705 and can be reached by phone at 727-898-1181.
