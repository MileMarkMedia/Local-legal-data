# Law Office of Israel Garcia

The Law Office of Israel Garcia is a San Antonio personal injury firm focused on auto and truck accident cases for injury victims throughout Bexar County and nearby communities such as New Braunfels, Schertz, and Seguin. Serving the community since 2003, the firm is led by attorney Israel Garcia, who trained at the Trial Lawyers College, is a member of the Texas Trial Lawyers Association, and is a Life Member of the Million Dollar Advocates Forum. The firm handles 18-wheeler and delivery truck crashes, company vehicle and rideshare accidents, drunk and distracted driving collisions, motorcycle, pedestrian, and bicycle accidents, catastrophic injuries, and wrongful death claims. Reported results include a $4 million settlement for an 18-wheeler driver injured by improperly loaded cargo, a $3.274 million bench trial verdict in a drunk driver and bicycle case, and a $2.551 million jury verdict in a car accident case. Recognition includes a 10.0 Avvo rating and the Avvo Clients' Choice Award. The attorney and staff are bilingual in English and Spanish, consultations are free, and cases are handled on a contingency fee basis. The Law Office of Israel Garcia is located at 926 Chulie Drive, San Antonio, TX 78216 and can be reached by phone at 210-529-9999.
